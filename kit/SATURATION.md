# Discovery-rate saturation protocol v1

A perfect score on a repaired development bank is regression closure, not general saturation.
Sector runs may claim operational saturation only when all requirements below are met.

## Breadth before stopping

- Verify at least four source families where feasible, covering at least three geographic or
  temporal grains. Record actual sample responses, sparsity, units, revisions, completeness limits,
  and upstream observed/modelled/proxy status.
- Maintain at least 250 active single-turn development questions across ordinary, indirect,
  compositional, adversarial, ambiguity, source-gap, and algebra-breaker registers.
- Maintain at least 50 deliberate expressiveness probes. Inexpressible questions become proposals;
  never weaken their golds to fit the released algebra.
- Publish a machine-readable coverage matrix. Raw question count without matrix diversity does not
  satisfy breadth.

## Freeze and untouched evidence

1. Development questions may drive fixes. Run the complete active wall after every parser, prompt,
   connector, executor, scorer, repair, or gold change.
2. Freeze the parser, prompt, connector set, executor, schema, scorer, synthesis checks, and active
   development banks. Record cryptographic hashes in an epoch manifest.
3. Generate holdout questions and golds only after the freeze. The generator must not see parser
   outputs, repairs, or failure traces. Golds must be execution-admitted and semantically audited.
4. Any fix or repair suggested by an untouched holdout invalidates the epoch. Classify and absorb
   admissible discoveries into development, rerun the full wall, and create a new freeze.
5. Pass at least three consecutive untouched holdouts of at least 40 questions each. Use at least
   two independent generation or judging families across the sequence where available.

## Plateau criterion

Across the qualifying three-bank sequence there must be no new uncharacterized failure class,
fewer than one new issue per 50 questions, stable strict semantic scores, and no manual
answer-surface, provenance, completeness, source-routing, or corpus-integrity violation. Known
residue may remain only when repeated, classified, evidence-backed, and explicitly bounded.

The final report must name the tested distribution, all excluded or defective golds, model and
generator provenance, freeze hashes, discovery curve, residual blind spots, and the precise claim.
This is empirical operational saturation, never a mathematical proof of general correctness.
