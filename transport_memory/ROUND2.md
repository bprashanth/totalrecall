# Round 2 — strategy for defensible transport saturation

Adopted from the livelihoods Round-2 protocol (../livelihoods_memory/ROUND2.md, read-only
reference); adapted to this sector's sources and Round-1 evidence base.

## Correction to the Round 1 strategy

Round 1 closed the replication contract: 45 questions across three banks, full regression
discipline, frontier parity, final 0.985 weighted. That demonstrated the harness can close a
bounded suite; it did not demonstrate generalization over distributions nobody tuned against.
In Round 2, **regression closure is a milestone; saturation is a DISCOVERY-RATE claim over
untouched distributions**. The question is no longer "can we reach green on banks we mined"
but "how often does a fresh, never-seen question surface a new, uncharacterized issue".

## What "saturation" means here

Round 2 may claim operational saturation only when all of these hold:

1. **Data breadth.** At least four independently verified keyless source families spanning at
   least three geographic/temporal grains, with the upstream evidence status (observed
   administrative vs upstream-modeled/estimated vs proxy) of EVERY source documented and
   propagated. Round 1 shipped two families (OSM Overpass points + route relations; World Bank
   country series). Round 2 adds at least two more (census below), and re-audits the Round-1
   families' modeling status — the livelihoods run caught modeled ILO statistics entering as
   'observed'; the same audit applies to WB transport indicators (ICAO staff estimates) and to
   any ridership feed whose upstream fields literally say "estimated".
2. **Capability breadth.** Coverage is measured by the matrix (`coverage/matrix.json`,
   regenerated by `harness/coverage.py`), not by question count. Paraphrases add rows, not
   cells. Empty and singleton cells (source × type, type × skeleton, capability families)
   drive generation; raw counts do not.
3. **Breaker pressure.** At least 30 deliberate probes targeting semantics OUTSIDE the current
   op set: FILTER/attribute predicates, GROUP/partition, positive UNION, set intersection,
   rate denominators, temporal alignment, quantiles, row-level ordering. Every probe is judged
   into exactly one bin: admissible-under-v2.1 (stress test), executor-semantics gap
   (development fix with guards), or inexpressible (evidence-backed entry in
   spec-proposals.md). A gold is NEVER weakened below the user's question to obtain execution.
   Negated union is already expressible via De Morgan (chained `beyond`) — proven by the
   livelihoods run; it is not re-proposed here.
4. **Training/test separation — the frozen-epoch rule.** Development banks may drive fixes.
   After the FREEZE (checksums recorded below), the claim requires **3 consecutive untouched
   holdout banks of ≥40 questions each**, generated after the freeze, first contact, scored
   as-is. **No fix of any kind may land mid-sequence: any change to parser.py, executor.py,
   connectors.py, ir_schema.py, scorer.py, the few-shot curriculum, or any active bank
   invalidates the epoch and restarts it** (new checksums, three fresh holdouts). Holdout
   generation varies register/prompt per bank; the generator never sees parser outputs,
   repairs, or failure traces.
5. **Discovery plateau.** Across the three frozen holdouts: **fewer than 1 NEW uncharacterized
   issue per 50 questions**, no new failure CLASS (an instance of a Round-1/Round-2
   characterized class — multi-part composite, split-entity mode ambiguity, model-bind
   fragility, documented source thinness — does not count as new), stable layer scores, and no
   answer-surface/provenance/corpus-integrity violation. Known residue may remain only if
   classified and evidence-backed.
6. **Full guards during development.** Every development fix re-runs ALL active development
   banks (seed, gen-001, gen-002-indirect, gen-003, multiturn) before it ships.

This is empirical saturation over the tested distribution (single-turn English place-questions
about transport, compiled by the frozen 2B against these five source families), not a proof.
Residual blind spots are stated in REPORT-ROUND2.md.

## Round 2 workstreams

- **A. Source expansion** — census ≥2 new keyless families beyond OSM points/routes + WB
  series; verify real rows for multiple places before adoption; document evidence status per
  source; reject phantoms at census time (Round-1 discipline).
- **B. Coverage matrix** — `harness/coverage.py` (own adaptation of the livelihoods reference)
  over all admitted transport banks; machine-readable; diffed at every checkpoint;
  empty/singleton cells drive gen-003 and the holdout prompts.
- **C. Breaker program** — ≥30 probes as in (3); outcomes in `questions/breakers-round2.json`,
  judged in FINDINGS.md, proposals in spec-proposals.md.
- **D. Development ticks** — gen-003 (new-source distribution), full guards, Round-1
  discipline unchanged (few-shots ≤15, layer-correct fixes, chronology).
- **E. Freeze + holdout epoch** — checksums below; then 3 × ≥40 untouched questions
  (varied registers), discovery-rate table in REPORT-ROUND2.md.
