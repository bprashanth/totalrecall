CONTENTS AND USE
The source contains 410 amphibian and reptile occurrences from
time-constrained encounter surveys in six rainforest remnants during
September-October 2017, plus 25 incidental records. Method, observer effort,
microhabitat, weather and day/night annotations stay attached to each row.
Incidental and systematic records must not be treated as equal-effort samples.
