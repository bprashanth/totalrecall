CONTENTS AND USE
The source covers 11 threatened tree species, point-centred-quarter trees
around them, and a plant inventory along 65 trails surveyed from October 2020
to March 2022. Occurrence rows are admissible as observed locations. Sampling
protocol and effort text remain attached to rows; the current generic index
does not yet turn the compound effort strings into a denominator plane.
