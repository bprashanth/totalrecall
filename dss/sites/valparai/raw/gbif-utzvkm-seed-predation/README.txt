CONTENTS AND USE
The archive contains focal native rainforest trees and animal detections at
those trees from January to August 2020. The shared Darwin Core eventID can
support an inspectable co-event layer, but co-detection must not be presented as
proof of seed dispersal, predation or another interaction unless the source row
explicitly records that behaviour.
