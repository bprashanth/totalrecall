CONTENTS AND USE
The source reports frog abundance from 25 x 4 m belt transects in terrestrial
rainforest interiors and streams across ten fragments and three control sites
during the 2018 south-west monsoon. Survey duration and transect information
must be retained when comparing counts or discussing missing records.
