This is a method and published-summary snapshot, not a local occurrence source. It retains the
analysis notebooks, reusable R functions, species-selection summaries, goodness-of-fit results,
model-averaged predictor effects and published appendices.

Checklist rows, observer scores, coordinates, checklist covariates, rasters, fitted model objects
and archives are deliberately excluded because the underlying eBird and environmental products
have their own access and redistribution terms. A future authorised connector must retrieve those
inputs under their governing terms and record its own version. No included model summary should
be described as a Valparai observation or an estate-scale prediction.
