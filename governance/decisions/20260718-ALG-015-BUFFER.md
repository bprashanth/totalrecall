## Conditional implementation record — 2026-07-19

The accepted core is implemented under the explicit `v2.4.0-draft` profile. Default kit parser,
validator, and executor APIs remain v2.3.0 and reject BUFFER. The reference implementation includes
additive nested canonicalization, support interning without operand copying, typed radius holes,
`method:"bbox-approx"` support/provenance, approximation-aware synthesis audit, and fail-closed
dateline/pole handling.

The neutral ten-question gold corpus validates and fixture-executes 10/10, including the required
search-radius versus pairwise-threshold example. Its generated training rows are in
`kit/conformance/buffer_parse_v2.4.jsonl`. Parser promotion is not met: the untrained base 2B
matches 5/8 required canonical trees and the existing merged 9B adapter matches 4/8. These are
development results, not saturation evidence. ALG-015 is therefore `implemented`, not `validated`,
and remains absent from the release manifest until a trained v2.4 bundle passes the wall and the
ALG-002 bundle dependency is reconciled.
