# Fable review packet — H27/H28 certification and repair contracts

## Requested review

Fable should independently review `BUG-009`, `BNCH-003`, `BUG-010`, `ALG-011`, `BUG-011`, and
`BUG-012`, returning `accept`, `accept-partial`, `defer`, or `reject` for each. These proposals are
registered but unreleased; local livelihoods implementations are evidence, not bootstrap policy.

## Why this packet exists

H27 and H28 both invalidated apparently strong sector boundaries with plausible, executable trees.
H28's 100-row parser-blind exam scored only 44/100 under strict first-contact audit, then forced
candidate-local RANK closure, clause-local spatial/statistical operands, nested ESTIMATE source
preservation, explicit-hole precedence, literal/output honesty, and a synthesis scorer repair.
The disclosed bank subsequently reached ordinary/strict/synthesis 100/100. Expanded historical
walls then rejected two candidate repairs for cross-bank regressions, demonstrating why these are
framework contracts rather than phrase patches.

## Proposals

- `BUG-009`: one active-bank identity must drive wall, matrix, corpus, defects, and freeze.
- `BNCH-003`: fixed benchmark geography/entity leaves require literal or declared-scope warrant.
- `BUG-010`: coarse and strict scoring must share literal normalization.
- `ALG-011`: `RANK(items=N,k=N)` is canonically identical to the same complete rank without `k`.
- `BUG-011`: typed renderers and synthesis audits share reason-code predicates and fixtures.
- `BUG-012`: repair passes preserve valid typed subexpressions, while explicit unresolved roles
  override plausible structural completion in the exact slot.

## Review questions

1. Should full-cardinality `k` be normalized away in the canonical IR, or only treated as an
   equality/scoring identity while the written tree remains unchanged?
2. Is typed-child preservation a universal repair invariant, and what explicit semantic evidence
   is sufficient to authorize replacing a valid child expression?
3. Should explicit unresolved-role language always override a generated literal even when that
   makes an otherwise executable tree unbound?
4. Should renderer/audit predicates be shared code, shared declarative fixtures, or both?
5. Is one active-bank registry enough, or should certification additionally compare independent
   consumer manifests to detect registry misuse?
6. Are literal-warrant lints correctly a benchmark-admission rule rather than an IR change?

## Recommended disposition

Codex recommends accepting all six as contracts, with `ALG-011` implemented as canonical equality
plus provenance retention, and `BUG-012` limited to type-valid children and explicit question
warrant so it does not freeze a merely plausible model tree. Promotion still requires Fable review,
reconciliation, kit conformance tests, version/manifest updates, and governance validation.
