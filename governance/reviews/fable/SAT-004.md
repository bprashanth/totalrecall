# Fable review — SAT-004 (tiered saturation: practical vs hard)

**Verdict: accept-partial** (accept the tier distinction and all seven practical-saturation
requirements; three binding conditions below). Reviewed 2026-07-13 from review-packet-sat004.md +
required reading; independent of codex's verdict.

## Rationale
The economics are correct: on a certified 1,382-question wall with discovery rate ≈ 0, further
clean banks tighten confidence they do not buy discovery, and their judge/gold cost competes
directly with opening a new sector — which is where discovery now lives. The design avoids the
peeking sin: the stopping target was committed BEFORE H28 contact, and H28 is framed as exam, not
evidence. Requirement 6 ("any legitimate solver/framework change = exam failed; absorb, rerun the
wall, refreeze, new exam") is exactly the right severity. This also matches our own civic-side
finding that plateau-by-rotation marks in-context saturation: marginal information per unit work is
the honest stopping variable, not green walls.

## Conditions (the "partial")
1. **Tier must be machine-visible and non-upgradeable by prose.** framework-lock.json and the
   sector REPORT must record `saturation_tier: practical|hard`. Practical NEVER gates: LoRA
   merges/second-round training claims, cross-sector model integration, deployment-strength
   claims, or residual-tail estimation — SAT-001 hard 3/3 stays required there (the packet says
   this; make it a manifest-checkable field, not a sentence).
2. **The 1×80 single-bank option is weaker than 2×40-independent.** Permit it only with an
   independent gold author/judge where quota allows; otherwise prefer 2×40. State which variant
   was used in the certification JSON.
3. **Cross-sector comparability**: the report sentence "hard 3/3 not completed" is required
   verbatim (already in req. 7) AND head-to-head tables across sectors must not mix tiers without
   labeling them.
