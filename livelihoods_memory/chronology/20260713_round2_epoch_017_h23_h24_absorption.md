# Round 2 epoch 017 — H23/H24 pressure absorption

## Why this experiment exists

The post-H22 development wall was exact, but H23 and H24 had already been generated against the
retired epoch-016 compiler. They could not be countable blind evidence for epoch 017, yet they were
valuable independent pressure. Stopping at the wall would have frozen a compiler that still failed
ordinary rank, arithmetic, spatial, anaphoric, and source-gap surfaces.

Both raw banks were audited before qwen contact. Forty rows per bank were selected, validated, and
executed against their expected outcome classes. The selected pressure files are immutable; all
later corrections live in separate disclosed development releases.

Independent adjudication classified all 32 H23 mismatches as genuine compiler divergences. H24

- computed rank quantities, candidate closure, direction, and cardinality;

The pressure also exposed a dangerous false-grounding path. In `h23-061`, qwen's raw leaves were
the requested night market and cold-storage depot, but named-entity restoration rewrote the

silently broadened a restrictive subtype to every marketplace, and H10's “coworking access” gold
silently chose raw facility count as its proxy. Those cases corroborate `BUG-003`: unmatched
modifiers and distinct prefix lexemes must fail closed.

## Generalized absorption

The parser repairs are grammar families rather than row-specific answers. They cover endpoint and
ratio rank blueprints, top-N and ascending language, compact mixed-source arithmetic, per-operand
scope, direct prefixed statistics, half-kilometre thresholds, nested relation composition,
nearest-distance output, spatial arithmetic, anaphoric record/count roles, deictic role holes,
direct transfer reconstruction, and complete source-gap literals. Deterministic coverage increased
from 104 to 114 tests, including adjacent negative guards.

The harness now refuses zero-overlap entity restoration, enforces the frozen Records input types
for `ANNOTATE`, `RELATE`, `AGGREGATE`, and `ESTIMATE`, requires a `REGION` node when an ESTIMATE
target is structured, and recognizes Rwanda/Tanzania suffix equivalence in strict audit. The
resolver replaces arbitrary lexical prefix/subset matching with bounded plural normalization and
declared aliases. Unknown restrictive subtypes therefore reach a typed DataRequest instead of a
plausible wrong source.

Two H24 questions identified useful real-source breadth rather than algebra gaps. The World Bank
`SI.POV.GINI` series was verified for Brazil, India, and Kenya; OSM metro stations were verified in
Bengaluru using `railway=station` plus `station=subway`. The source census now passes 14/14 probes
across four tested families. Because these routes were added after immutable pressure contact,
H24's original DataRequest expectations remain untouched while the disclosed development release
correctly expects Answers for `h24-078` and `h24-080`.

## Saturation decision

H23 and H24 contribute zero saturation passes because they predate the compiler boundary they
helped create. Their perfect disclosed replays demonstrate absorption, not generalization. The
resolver, schema, parser, connector, and audit changes retire the preceding wall. A complete wall
must pass again before epoch 017 can freeze, and every countable bank must be newly authored from
that exact checksum boundary.
