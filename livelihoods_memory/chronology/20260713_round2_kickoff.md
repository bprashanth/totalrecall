# Round 2 kickoff — 2026-07-13

## Why Round 1 ended too early

Round 1 stopped when 52 active questions, five dialogue cases, and the frontier control were green.
That was replication closure. The generated banks were source-aware, repaired banks were counted as
evidence of generalization, and only OSM plus World Bank were deeply exercised. The user correctly
challenged the saturation claim.

## What changes now

Round 2 uses `ROUND2.md`: expand source and semantic grains, publish a capability matrix, create
hundreds of ordinary and adversarial questions, separate development from untouched holdouts, and
measure the rate of newly discovered failure classes. A perfect repaired bank is not a stopping
condition. Three consecutive frozen holdouts with no new class are required.

## Initial checkpoint

The frozen v2.1 algebra remains unchanged. Round 1 artifacts remain the development baseline and
are not rewritten. The next work is harness/coverage taxonomy followed by an official-source census;
only verified sources enter routing or gold questions.
