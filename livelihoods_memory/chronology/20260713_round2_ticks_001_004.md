# Round 2 checkpoints 2–5 — broad development pressure

## Bank construction and gold admission

`round2-dev.json` contains 214 unique questions generated from a deterministic stratification
blueprint and executed before admission. Combined with Round 1, the active development surface is
266 questions. The new bank contributes 74 ILOSTAT, 72 Eurostat, 30 World Bank, eight explicitly
mixed-source, 18 OSM, ten ambiguity/behavior, and two honest source-gap cases. Its matrix cells
include point/window SELECT, endpoint change, bounded trend, place comparison, same-unit ratio,
ascending/n-ary/top-k ranks, mixed-source comparison, presence, co-occurrence, and annotation.

Separately, 60 expressiveness probes cover ten missing-semantic families. They are not executable
golds and do not inflate the active score.

## Tick 001 — first contact: 0.875, execution class 0.45

The coarse shape score was 0.91, but only 45% had the expected execution class. Dominant failures:

- qwen dropped measure nouns (`rate`, `persons`, `workers`) and routed to no connector;
- city-centered phrases replaced NUTS-2 names (Madrid/Warsaw);
- statistical point/window lookups acquired spatial count/density wrappers;
- ratio operands collapsed or disappeared;
- wide ranks lost items, order, year, or top-k and sometimes copied the marketplace exemplar;
- ANNOTATE produced a dict in `layer`, which passed schema and crashed the executor;
- dormant presence/co-occurrence/annotation surfaces compiled to SELECT/count;
- deictic and anaphoric holes were under/over-bound.

The old scorer intentionally ignored every AGGREGATE, and the executor passed every aggregation
over a Series through. Thus a spatial density over a statistical rate could become green after an
entity repair. This was classified as type-integrity debt, not parser-only residue.

## Ticks 002–004 — repair, strict audit, regression

Repairs are question-grounded/type-driven: restore only supported phrases literally present in the
question; restore named NUTS-2 aliases; compile explicit series rank/ratio/presence/co-occurrence/
annotation patterns; bind explicit point/windows; enforce subtract-X-from-Y orientation; preserve
two distinct mixed-source leaves; and remove invented time. Validator leaf types now reject dict
annotation layers and invalid threshold/k types. Executor permits only the documented
mean-by-time identity over a source-provided Series.

Tick 002 scored 1.000 mechanically, but new `semantic_audit.py` found 17 green mismatches: missing
window/k, reversed subtraction, duplicated mixed-source leaves, and damaged gap entities. These
were fixed rather than accepting the score. Tick 004 is 214/214 on the ordinary scorer **and**
214/214 on canonical semantic IR (entities, regions, times, fields, operands, rank order/k,
thresholds, layers, and holes).

Round 1 guards initially exposed prompt rotations on one count-over-relation, one named trend, and
two dialogue parses. General literal/proximity and binding normalization repaired them. Final guards:
seed 24/24, neutral 17/17, indirect 11/11, and dialogue 5/5 with both binding strategies.
