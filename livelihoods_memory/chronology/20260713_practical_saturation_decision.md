# Practical saturation decision for first-pass sector benchmarks

The livelihoods run established a strong epoch-020 boundary: 1,382 active questions, 1,379/1,379
eligible ordinary and strict rows, 1,382/1,382 synthesis/evidence rows, 157/157 regressions, 14/14
source probes, 5/5 dialogue cases on both binders, 39 skeletons, and a zero-mismatch 58-file freeze.
H27 had already supplied 100 broad adversarial questions and caused real general repairs, so it is
development evidence and cannot count as an untouched pass.

The original hard protocol then asked for three further clean, post-freeze banks. That remains a
good high-confidence rule, but its marginal value is now mainly repeated tail-risk measurement.
With several other sectors still untested and external authoring quotas exhausted, spending three
more author/audit cycles here would displace more informative cross-sector work.

Judge decision: first-pass sector benchmarks use a distinct practical-saturation claim. After all
broad certification gates pass, one fresh parser-blind bank of at least 80 questions (or two of at
least 40), at least half adversarial and spanning every major family, must pass immutable first
contact without requiring a valid change. If it fails, absorb the discovery, refreeze, and require
one new qualifying bank. The report must say that hard 3/3 remains pending.

For livelihoods, H28 is the candidate practical exam: 100 unique questions, 55 adversarial rows,
24 spatial, 22 statistical, 18 rank, 14 transfer, 20 contrastive pairs, and 10 output-head
contrasts, authored after epoch 020 without parser contact. It has not yet been admitted, executed,
or scored. This decision was recorded before those steps so the stopping rule is not chosen after
seeing the result.
