I need to correct my earlier answer. The number depends on which dataset revision you're looking at:

**The current World Bank / ILO figure for India's unemployment rate in 2021 is 6.38%.**

This is the modelled ILO estimate (indicator SL.UEM.TOTL.ZS) as published by the World Bank's World Development Indicators, sourced from the ILOSTAT database.

**Citation:** World Bank, "Unemployment, total (% of total labor force) (modeled ILO estimate) — India," World Development Indicators, indicator SL.UEM.TOTL.ZS. Source: ILO Modelled Estimates (ILOEST), International Labour Organization. https://data.worldbank.org/indicator/SL.UEM.TOTL.ZS?locations=IN

**Important caveat for your report:** The ILO periodically revises its modelled estimates. An older dataset revision (retrieved ~2019) showed 5.98% for India in 2021; the current (January 2026) revision shows **6.38%**. For a funding report, use the latest revision (6.38%) and cite the World Bank/ILO source above with the access date. My earlier figure of 7.8% was incorrect — that likely conflated a different source or methodology.
