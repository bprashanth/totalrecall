I have to be straightforward with you: **I don't have a verified, citable number for the exact count of ration shops (full-time or part-time) in Thalavadi taluk.**

The sources I listed earlier are real starting points, but I was not able to extract a specific number from them — and I should not invent one for your funding report. A reviewer checking a made-up or guessed figure would flag it immediately.

**What I recommend for your report:**

1. **NFSA "Locate FPS" portal** — this is the most likely place to get a countable, official list:
   [https://nfsa.gov.in/portal/Locate_FPS](https://nfsa.gov.in/portal/Locate_FPS)
   Select Tamil Nadu > Erode > Thalavadi block. If the portal is working, it will list each shop.

2. **Contact the Taluk Supply Officer (TSO), Thalavadi** — for a funding report, a direct confirmation from the TSO office (phone: 04295-245388) would be the strongest citation anyway, since a reviewer can verify it against the same office.

3. **RTI request** to the Tamil Nadu Civil Supplies Corporation if the above don't work — slow but produces a citable official document.

For your report, I'd suggest citing the source as something like: *"As per data obtained from the Taluk Supply Officer, Thalavadi / NFSA portal (accessed [date])"* — that's a citation a reviewer can actually verify.

I'd rather tell you I don't know than give you a number that could undermine your report.
