To ensure your funding report is airtight and can withstand any reviewer's check, you should select the exact number that matches the **geographic scope** of your project/funding. 

Here is the definitive breakdown of the official Census of India 2011 figures for Erode and exactly how they are used:

### 1. For Municipal Corporation-wide Projects (Recommended)
* **The Exact Number:** **498,121**
* **What it represents:** The population of the **Erode City Municipal Corporation (ECMC)** within its current, expanded boundaries (109.52 sq. km). 
* **Why use this:** In late 2011, the corporation expanded to merge surrounding municipalities and panchayats. This is the official baseline population utilized by the Erode Corporation and the Government of India for all current urban planning and infrastructure funding.
* **Citation:** [Erode City Municipal Corporation - About Corporation](https://www.tnurbantree.tn.gov.in/erode/)

### 2. For Metropolitan/Regional Projects
* **The Exact Number:** **521,891** (or **521,776** in some provisional datasets)
* **What it represents:** The population of the **Erode Urban Agglomeration (Metropolitan Area)**.
* **Why use this:** Use this if your funding relates to the wider metropolitan region (beyond the municipal boundary lines).
* **Citation:** [Census of India 2011 - Erode Metropolitan Urban Region Population](https://www.census2011.co.in/census/metropolitan/441-erode.html)

### 3. For Old/Core City Proper Projects
* **The Exact Number:** **157,101**
* **What it represents:** The population of the **pre-expansion Erode Municipal Corporation** (8.44 sq. km).
* **Why use this:** Only use this if your project is restricted to the legacy core city area. 
* **Important Note:** A reviewer performing a simple, un-nuanced Google search will likely see this number first, as it is the official figure listed for the unexpanded city boundaries in the main Census tables. If you use **498,121**, cite the expanded city limits to avoid them mistakenly flagging it.
* **Citation:** [Census of India 2011 - Town-wise Population](https://web.archive.org/web/20150924144408/www.censusindia.gov.in/pca/SearchDetails.aspx?Id=686226)
