Yes. Because reviewers check different databases depending on the scope of the funding body, there is no single absolute number. Using the wrong one will result in a flag. 

To ensure your report is reviewer-proof, select **one** of the three standard options below based on who is reviewing your report:

---

### Option A: For International Reviewers (World Bank / UN / Global Donors)
Use this if your funding body or reviewer relies on international development databases. They will check the World Bank World Development Indicators (WDI) calendar-year database.

* **The Number:** **6.38%** (for calendar year 2021)
* **Citation:** 
  > *World Bank / International Labour Organization (ILO).* [Unemployment, total (% of total labor force) (modeled ILO estimate) - India](https://data.worldbank.org/indicator/SL.UEM.TOTL.ZS?locations=IN).

---

### Option B: For Domestic Reviewers (Indian Government / Local Ministries)
Use this if you are submitting to an Indian government agency. They only accept official national data from the Ministry of Statistics and Programme Implementation (MoSPI). Note that India reports on a fiscal July-to-June cycle rather than a calendar year.

* **The Number:** **4.2%** (for July 2020 – June 2021) or **4.1%** (for July 2021 – June 2022)
* **Citation:** 
  > *National Sample Survey Office, Ministry of Statistics and Programme Implementation, Government of India.* [Annual Report: Periodic Labour Force Survey (PLFS) 2021-22](https://mospi.gov.in/sites/default/files/publication_reports/AnnualReportPLFS2021-22F1.pdf).

---

### Option C: For Private Sector / Economic Research Reviewers (Think Tanks / Venture Funds)
Use this if your funding report needs to reflect real-time household data, specifically capturing the severe economic impact of the COVID-19 Delta wave in mid-2021, which official government statistics are often criticized for smoothing over.

* **The Number:** **7.8%** (the 12-month average for calendar year 2021)
* **Citation:** 
  > *Centre for Monitoring Indian Economy (CMIE).* [India Unemployment Rate (Yearly) Historical Trends via YCharts](https://ycharts.com/indicators/india_unemployment_rate_annual).
