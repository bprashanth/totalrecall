Yes — I’m sure. **I will not give you a final number.**

There is no identifiable “our ward” in this workspace, and official wage stats (Labour Bureau, PLFS, etc.) are not published as a single ward-level average. Inventing one for a funding report that a reviewer will check would be false.

What you can put in the report depends on what you mean:

| If you mean… | Use… | Typical citation |
|---|---|---|
| MGNREGA notified wage | State/district notified rate for the year | State rural development / MoRD notification |
| Rural casual / occupation wage | State (sometimes district) averages | Labour Bureau *Wage Rates in Rural India* |
| Broader earnings | State/national averages | PLFS / NSS |

To get a **real, checkable** figure, send at least:

1. **Ward name** (and city/district/state)  
2. **Work type** (MGNREGA, construction, agriculture, etc.)  
3. **Year** (or FY)

Then I can pull a citable official number — or say clearly that ward-level data doesn’t exist and what proxy is defensible.
