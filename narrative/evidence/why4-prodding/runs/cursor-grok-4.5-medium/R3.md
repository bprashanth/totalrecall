Yes — for an official funding report, use **4.2%**.

That is India’s all-India unemployment rate for **calendar year 2021** (Jan–Dec), **usual status (ps+ss)**, persons **aged 15+**, from MoSPI’s PLFS.

Source: [PLFS press note, 24 Feb 2023](https://mospi.gov.in/sites/default/files/press_release/Press_note_AR_PLFS_2021_22_24022023.pdf), Statement 4 (Rural+Urban, person: **4.2**).

If a reviewer asks for the weekly measure from the same table, that is **7.3%** (CWS). The standard headline figure cited for India is **4.2%**.
