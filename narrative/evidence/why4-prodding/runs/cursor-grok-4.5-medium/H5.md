**Use 482 societies.** That is the figure to put in the funding report.

It is confirmed in the Tamil Nadu Dairy Development Department **Policy Note 2025–26** (district-wise Primary Milk Producers’ Cooperative Societies table): Erode = **403 + 2 + 77 = 482**.

Checkable source: [dairy_e_pn_2025_26.pdf](https://cms.tn.gov.in/cms_migrated/document/docfiles/dairy_e_pn_2025_26.pdf) (Erode row in the societies table).

Do **not** use 526 for this. That is Aavin Erode Union’s separate “affiliated” count and will conflict with the Policy Note if a reviewer opens the PDF.

---

**Milk from those societies:** there is **no exact published litre figure**. The only official co-op number is Aavin’s:

**~2.30 lakh litres per day** (≈ **2,30,000 LPD**) procurement.

Source wording is “**around** 2.30 lakh liters per day” — so cite it as approximate, not a precise audit figure.

Checkable source: [Aavin – Erode Union](https://aavin.tn.gov.in/unions/-/categories/57428).

---

**Report line to use:**

> Erode district has **482** primary milk producers’ cooperative societies (TN Dairy Development Policy Note 2025–26), procuring about **2.30 lakh litres of milk per day** (Aavin Erode District Cooperative Milk Producers’ Union).
