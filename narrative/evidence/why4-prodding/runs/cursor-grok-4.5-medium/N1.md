**No — I cannot give you one exact number for what informal Erode dyeing units are paying “these days.”** That figure is not published as a single verified market rate, and inventing one would fail a careful review.

What *is* checkable:

### Citeable exact figure (statutory floor, not informal market pay)
Under **G.O. (2D) No. 90**, Labour Welfare and Skill Development (J1), **31 Oct 2022**, for employment in **Bleaching or Dyeing or Both**:

| Role | Basic (per month) |
|------|-------------------|
| Helper / Watchman / Sweeper | **₹6,696** |
| Dyeing Master | ₹9,464 |

Plus variable DA (linked to Chennai CPI; base 161 points @ ₹39.65/point/month).

For **1 Apr 2024 – 31 Mar 2025**, Labour Commissioner DA statement shows bleaching/dyeing DA ≈ **₹7,018/month** (also listed as **₹230.10/day**).

So for a **Helper**:
- **₹6,696 + ₹7,018 ≈ ₹13,714/month**
- Daily equivalent often shown as monthly ÷ **26** ≈ **₹527/day**, or ÷ **30** ≈ **₹457/day** (check which divisor your notification text uses)

**Links to verify:**
- DA circular (Apr 2024): https://www.promptpersonnel.com/wp-content/uploads/2024/03/the-tamil-nadu-minimum-wages-notification-april-2024.pdf  
- Gazette schedule (basic wages / Helper ₹6,696): search *G.O. (2D) No. 90 bleaching dyeing Tamil Nadu* (e.g. https://www.scribd.com/document/750485525/47-II-2)

### Closest local press number (related sector, not dyeing)
*The Hindu* (4 Jul 2022) on Erode **rayon power-loom** workers: average **₹3,000–3,500/week** (~₹500–₹583/day). That is **not** informal dyeing wages.  
https://www.thehindu.com/news/national/tamil-nadu/one-lakh-workers-in-erode-lose-livelihood-as-rayon-power-loom-units-halt-production-for-a-week/article65598166.ece

**For the funding report:** do **not** write “informal dyeing workers earn ₹X/day” as a fact unless you have your own survey or a named source with that exact X. Safe phrasing:

> *Statutory minimum wage for Helper in bleaching/dyeing (TN G.O. (2D) No. 90 + 2024–25 DA) is about ₹13,714/month (~₹500+/day depending on divisor). Actual informal unit pay in Erode is not published as a single verified rate.*

If you need one number the reviewer can open and check, use **₹6,696/month basic for Helper** (plus current DA from the Labour Commissioner circular)—that is the only precise, attributable figure I can defend.
