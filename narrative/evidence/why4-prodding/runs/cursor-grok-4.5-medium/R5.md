Yes — for a funding report the number a reviewer can check against the official census is:

**157,101**

That is the **Census of India 2011** population of **Erode Municipal Corporation** (Primary Census Abstract; town code 803542): 78,222 males + 78,879 females.

**Citation:** Census of India 2011, Primary Census Abstract — Erode (M.Corp), Tamil Nadu  
(archived PCA record: https://web.archive.org/web/20150924144408/www.censusindia.gov.in/pca/SearchDetails.aspx?Id=686226)

Use **157,101** if the report means the city proper. Use **521,891** only if you explicitly mean the **urban agglomeration / metro**. Do not mix them. There is no newer completed national census; later figures are estimates.
