Use **`473.606 thousand tonnes`** if your report needs **one exact official number for milk production in Erode district**.

The clean reviewer-safe wording is:

> **Erode district had 526 Primary Milk Producers’ Cooperative Societies under the Aavin Erode Union, and the district’s total estimated milk production was 473.606 thousand tonnes in 2022-23.**  
> The Aavin Union’s own figure for milk collected from those societies is **about 2.30 lakh litres per day**, which is an approximate procurement figure, not an exact production total.

Why this is the safest final answer:
- `526` is the official **co-op society count** from Aavin’s Erode Union page.
- `473.606 thousand tonnes` is the official **exact district milk production** figure from the Tamil Nadu Statistical Hand Book.
- `2.30 lakh litres/day` is **not exact** and is **procurement**, not production. The Aavin page itself uses the word **“about”**.
