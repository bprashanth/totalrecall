`₹536.62/day`

That is the cleanest exact number I can defend for a 2026 funding report if you need one figure for a general worker/helper in an Erode-side dyeing unit. It is the current Tamil Nadu notified minimum for `Helper`-level work in `Employment in Bleaching or Dyeing or Both`, derived as:

`(₹6,696 basic + ₹7,256 DA) / 26 = ₹536.62/day`

Checkable sources:
- The public copy of the bleaching/dyeing wage schedule shows `Helper` at `₹6,696` basic, and states daily wage is monthly wage divided by `26`; for `2024-25` that gave `₹527.46/day`: [Scribd mirror](https://www.scribd.com/document/839042879/Minimum-wages-Bleaching-or-Dyeing-or-Both-2024-2025)
- The `2026-27` Tamil Nadu minimum-wage compilation shows `Employment in Bleaching or Dyeing or Both` with DA `₹7,256/month` under the same `G.O. (2D) No. 90` schedule: [Ascent HR PDF](https://ascent-hr.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/03/Tamil-Nadu-Minimum-Wages-26-27.pdf)

Use `₹537/day` if your report wants a rounded whole-rupee figure.
