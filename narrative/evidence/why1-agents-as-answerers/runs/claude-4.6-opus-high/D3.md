As per **Census 2011**, cultivators account for approximately **14.9%** of total workers in Erode district.

Here are the numbers:

| Category | Number |
|---|---|
| **Total Workers** | 11,95,773 |
| **Cultivators** (main + marginal) | 1,78,170 |
| **Share of cultivators** | **14.90%** |

If counting only **main cultivators** (excluding marginal cultivators): **1,73,376** out of 11,95,773 total workers = **~14.5%**.

The breakdown of main vs marginal cultivators (from Wikipedia/Census data):
- Main cultivators: 1,73,376
- Marginal cultivators: 4,794
- Total cultivators: 1,78,170
