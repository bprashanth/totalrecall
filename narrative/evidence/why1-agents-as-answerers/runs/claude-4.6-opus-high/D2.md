Q: In 2020, how many road related complaints came from Hoodi ward in Bengaluru? Citation also.
elapsed: 63s attempt:1

**In 2020, there were 332 road-related complaints from Hoodi ward in Bengaluru**, filed under the "Road Maintenance(Engg)" category in BBMP's grievance system. Here's the breakdown by sub-category:

| Sub-category | Count |
|---|---|
| Potholes | 134 |
| Road side drains | 80 |
| Road cutting | 45 |
| Water stagnation | 36 |
| Footpath encroachment | 15 |
| Debris Removal / Construction Material | 10 |
| Water leakage on road | 7 |
| Footpath | 5 |
| **Total** | **332** |

**Citation:** BBMP Grievances Data (2020), ward-level grievances filed starting 8th Feb 2020. Published by OpenCity, sourced from BBMP. Available at: https://data.opencity.in/dataset/bbmp-grievances-data/resource/58808356-4b0a-4b02-9d70-75993b4dcd1c (CSV dataset, credit: Vivek Mathew).
