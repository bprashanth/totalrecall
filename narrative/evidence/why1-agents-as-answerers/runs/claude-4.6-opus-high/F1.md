The **MGNREGA daily wage rate for Tamil Nadu in FY 2025-26 is ₹336 per person per day**, effective from 1 April 2025. This is an increase of ₹17 (about 5.33%) from the previous year's rate of ₹319.

**Key details:**
- The wage is the same for both men and women.
- A 10% travel allowance is provided if the worksite is 5 km or more away.
- The revision is based on the Consumer Price Index for Agricultural Labourers (CPI-AL).
