Here's what I found. Unfortunately, there is **no single authoritative public figure** for what informal (unregistered) dyeing units in the Erode belt pay per day — precisely because these units operate outside formal wage-reporting systems. But several sources give reference points:

---

## Reported / Estimated Daily Wages

| Role / Source | Daily wage | Context |
|---|---|---|
| Power loom & textile processing workers (Erode/Tirupur) | **Rs 500–600/day** | 10-hour shifts; reported during 2022 mill strikes |
| Technicians / fitters in loom/dyeing units | **Rs 700–750/day** | Same report |
| Migrant workers in cloth printing units (Tiruppur, adjacent to Erode) | **Rs 900–1,200/day** for 8 hours | 2024 ground report on Bihari migrant-run units |
| Tamil Nadu statutory minimum for "Bleaching or Dyeing or Both" | Basic + DA; last CEIC-reported figure was **Rs 257–364/day** (2021–2022) | Official gazette rate, often not enforced in tiny units |
| Erode District Gazette minimum (general temp workers, local bodies) | **Rs 754–761/day** (2024–2026) | Government/Corporation workers, not private dyeing |

The key takeaway: most informal dyeing units in the Erode–Bhavani–Pallipalayam corridor likely pay somewhere in the **Rs 500–700 range per day** for unskilled/semi-skilled work, and **Rs 700–1,200** for skilled roles — but these are not formally documented and vary by unit.

## Caveats

- The Rs 500–700 figures are from 2022–2023 news reports; wages may have crept up slightly with inflation by mid-2026, but no fresh field data is publicly available.
- The statutory minimum for "Bleaching or Dyeing" in Tamil Nadu is significantly lower than what even informal units actually pay (market rates exceed gazette rates in practice).
- For precise current figures you'd need field surveys or AITUC/CITU local chapter contacts in Erode — published academic or journalistic studies specific to informal dyeing units in 2025–2026 don't appear to exist yet.
