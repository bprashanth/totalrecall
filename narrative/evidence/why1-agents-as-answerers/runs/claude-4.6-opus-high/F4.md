Q: How many registered industrial units are there in Erode district as per the MSME district industrial profile? Citations please.

Based on the **Brief Industrial Profile of Erode District (2012)**, published by the MSME-Development Institute (Ministry of MSME, Government of India), there are **19,521 registered industrial units** in Erode district.

This figure is broken down as follows in the "Industry at a Glance" section:

| Period | Registered Units | Employment | Investment (Lakh Rs.) |
|--------|-----------------|------------|----------------------|
| Up to 2007-08 | 15,278 | 71,948 | 20,77,742 |
| 2008-09 | 1,358 | 13,631 | 39,219.65 |
| 2009-10 | 1,758 | 14,728 | 2,11,955.25 |
| 2010-11 | 2,809 | 22,868 | 1,60,453.46 |
| **Total** | **19,521** | **77,503** | **5,26,440.38** |

Additionally, there are **38 registered medium and large units** in the district.

**Citation:**

"Brief Industrial Profile of Erode District," MSME-Development Institute, Chennai (Ministry of MSME, Government of India), 2012.
Available at: https://dcmsme.gov.in/dips/ips%20erode%202012_tn.pdf

Source of data within the document: District Industries Centre (DIC), Erode.
