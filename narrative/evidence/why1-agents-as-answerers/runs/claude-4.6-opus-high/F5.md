Q: The Kalingarayan canal takes water from which river? Give citations I can check.
elapsed: 20s attempt:1

---

The **Kalingarayan Canal** takes water from the **Bhavani River** (a tributary of the Kaveri/Cauvery). It originates at the Kalingarayan Anicut, a barrage built across the Bhavani River near Bhavani town in Erode district, Tamil Nadu. The canal then runs approximately 91 km before joining the Noyyal River at Kodumudi, making it one of India's oldest river-linking projects.
