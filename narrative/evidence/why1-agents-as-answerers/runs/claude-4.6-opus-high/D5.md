Q: What was India's unemployment rate in 2021 as per the ILO modelled estimate, the one World Bank publishes? Citation link please.
elapsed: 19s attempt:1

---

India's unemployment rate in 2021 was **6.38%** (of total labor force), as per the ILO Modelled Estimates published by the World Bank under indicator `SL.UEM.TOTL.ZS`.

**Source:** World Bank — World Development Indicators
[https://data.worldbank.org/indicator/SL.UEM.TOTL.ZS?locations=IN](https://data.worldbank.org/indicator/SL.UEM.TOTL.ZS?locations=IN)
