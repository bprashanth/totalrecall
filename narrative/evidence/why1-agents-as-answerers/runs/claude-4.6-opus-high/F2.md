Q: Erode turmeric got GI tag in which year? Citations please.
elapsed: 21s attempt:1

---

Erode turmeric (Erode Manjal) received its **GI (Geographical Indication) tag on 6 March 2019**, after an 8-year process. The application had been filed on 4 January 2011 by the Erode Manjal Vanigargal Matrum Kidangu Urimaiyalargal Sangham (Erode Turmeric Traders and Godown Owners Association).

**Sources:**

1. *The Hindu* – "Erode turmeric gets GI tag after an 8-year process":

2. TNDALU report on Erode Manjal GI:  
   https://www.tndalu.ac.in/pdf/2022/july/report_on_erode_manjal.pdf
