## Are complaints going up or coming down?

**Complaints are coming DOWN.**

The only complete year-over-year trend data with hard numbers comes from the **BBMP Sahaaya 2.0** dataset (Solid Waste / Garbage category, top ward — Rajarajeshwari Nagar):

| Year | Garbage Complaints |
