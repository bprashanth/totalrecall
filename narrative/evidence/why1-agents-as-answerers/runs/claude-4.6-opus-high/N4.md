### Key Notes

1. **No official all-India or state loom census has been published since the 4th All India Handloom Census (2019-20).** The exact total number of individual handloom units/looms in Erode is not publicly available for 2025-26.

2. **Powerloom units (~55,000)** is the most-cited figure from industry associations (Erode Power Loom Owners' Association), though ~3,000 looms have reportedly been scrapped in recent years due to economic stress ([DTNext](https://www.dtnext.in/news/tamilnadu/powerloom-sector-lifeline-of-erode-east-in-doldrums)).

3. **Cooperative societies (191 handloom + 57 powerloom)** is the official government figure updated for 2025-26 from the Erode District Collectorate website.

4. Tamil Nadu has recently requested the Centre to establish an **Integrated Powerloom Park in Erode** and a **Powerloom Modernisation Scheme** (Rs 50 crore from 2025-26) to upgrade 3,000 looms annually ([The Hindu, Jul 2026](https://www.thehindu.com/news/cities/Coimbatore/minister-submits-20-point-charter-to-centre-for-textile-sector/article71221086.ece)).

The most authoritative current source is the [Erode District Official Handlooms Page](https://erode.nic.in/handlooms-erode/), updated for the 2025-26 fiscal year. Granular unit-level census data (like IndiaStat's "Number of Functional Powerloom/Handloom Units") is behind a paywall and not publicly accessible.
