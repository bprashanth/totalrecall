Q: Between 2019 and 2022, which ward in Bengaluru had the most garbage complaints? What is the count? Citations please.
elapsed: 71s attempt:1

---

Here's the answer:

**Rajarajeshwari Nagar** had the most garbage (Solid Waste) complaints in Bengaluru between 2020 and 2022, with a total of **2,661 complaints**.

(Note: The BBMP Sahaaya 2.0 grievance portal only has data starting from February 2020 — there is no 2019 data available in this system. So the analysis covers 2020–2022.)

Year-wise breakdown for the top ward:
| Year | Complaints |
|------|-----------|
| 2020 | 1,087 |
| 2021 | 866 |
| 2022 | 708 |

**Sources:**
1. **Dataset:** [Vonter/bbmp-citizen-grievances](https://github.com/Vonter/bbmp-citizen-grievances) — consolidated dataset of citizen grievances from BBMP's Sahaaya 2.0 portal, sourced from the Bengaluru Smart City Limited Website.
2. **OpenCity Data Portal:** [BBMP Grievances Data](https://data.opencity.in/dataset/bbmp-grievances-data) — ward-level BBMP grievance data categorised by year (2020–2025), authored by Vaidya R / Vivek Mathew, licensed as Public Domain.
3. **Original source:** BBMP (Bruhat Bengaluru Mahanagara Palike) Sahaaya 2.0 portal at [smartoneblr.com](https://smartoneblr.com/NicApplicationStatus.htm).
