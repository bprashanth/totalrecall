## Garbage Complaints in Bellandur Ward: They went UP

Based on official BBMP grievance data (category: "Solid Waste (Garbage) Related", Ward: "Bellandur") filed via the Sahaaya 2.0 portal:

| Year | Garbage complaints | Change from previous year |
|------|-------------------|--------------------------|
| 2020 | 347 | (baseline; portal started Feb 2020) |
| 2021 | 375 | +8.1% |
| 2022 | 431 | +14.9% |

**Overall trend: Complaints rose by ~24% from 2020 to 2022** (347 to 431).

### Important caveats

- **No 2019 data exists** on this portal. The Sahaaya 2.0 system only went live on 8 Feb 2020, so comparable digital complaint records before that date are not available.
- The 2020 figure covers ~11 months (Feb-Dec), making the actual year-over-year increase from a full 2020 baseline potentially slightly lower.
- Rising numbers may also reflect growing awareness and adoption of the digital complaint portal, not just worsening conditions.
