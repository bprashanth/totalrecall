Q: The Kalingarayan canal takes water from which river? Give citations I can check.
elapsed: 20s attempt:1

---

The **Kalingarayan canal** takes water from the **Bhavani River**, diverted at the **Kalingarayan Anicut** near Bhavani town in Erode district, Tamil Nadu. The canal then runs to join the Noyyal River near Kodumudi.

**Citations you can check:**

1. **International Commission on Irrigation & Drainage (ICID)** — [Kalingarayan Anicut and Kalingarayan Channel System](https://icid-ciid.org/award/his_details/151)  
   States the channel “takes off from the right side of the Kalingarayan Anicut” and that the anicut was “constructed across the river Bhavani.”

2. **The Hindu** — [Kalingarayan anicut: A 13th century irrigation structure…](https://www.thehindu.com/news/national/tamil-nadu/a-13th-century-irrigation-structure-that-serves-as-a-lifeline-even-today/article67826880.ece)  
   Describes a barrage across the Bhavani, with water branched into a canal that joins the Noyyal at Kodumudi.

3. **Wikipedia** — [Kalingarayan Canal](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kalingarayan_canal)  
   Lists it as a branch of the Bhavani River and notes the canal begins at the Kalingarayan Anicut on the Bhavani.
