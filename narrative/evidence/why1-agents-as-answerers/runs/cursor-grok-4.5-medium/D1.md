Q: Between 2019 and 2022, which ward in Bengaluru had the most garbage complaints? What is the count? Citations please.
elapsed: 114s attempt:1

---

**Horamavu**, with **104** garbage-related complaints (2019–2022).

That count is from the [I Change My City Complaints Log – 2019–2022](https://data.opencity.in/dataset/i-change-my-city-data/resource/a60abf5c-3a15-4967-af32-c3074248580f) on OpenCity (Janaagraha iCMyC), filtering `category_title` to **Garbage and Unsanitary Practices** and **Sanitation**, and excluding the non-ward label “Other” (157).

Next named wards in that dataset: HSR Layout (98), Bellanduru (90), Begur (82).

**Note:** BBMP’s official grievances series ([OpenCity BBMP Grievances](https://data.opencity.in/dataset/bbmp-grievances-data)) starts in Feb 2020, so it does not cover 2019. In that BBMP data for 2020–2022, **Rajarajeshwari Nagar** leads with **2,642** Solid Waste (Garbage) Related complaints.
