Q: As per Census 2011, cultivators are what share of total workers in Erode district? Show the numbers and citation.
elapsed: 26s attempt:1

**About 14.9%** of total workers in Erode district were cultivators (Census 2011).

| Category | Number | Share of total workers |
|---|---|---|
| Total workers | 1,195,773 | 100% |
| Cultivators (main + marginal) | 178,170 | **14.9%** |

Breakdown often cited from the same census:
- Main cultivators: 173,376  
- Marginal cultivators: 4,794  
- Combined: 173,376 + 4,794 = 178,170  

**Citation:** Census of India 2011, as compiled in *Tamil Nadu – An Economic Appraisal 2011-12 to 2013-14* (Government of Tamil Nadu), and reproduced in the Erode District Agriculture Plan (TNAU/NADP), Table 2.4 — cultivators **1,78,170 (14.90%)** of total workers **11,95,773**.
