**That figure is not available** — there is no published village-wise average monthly dairy income for Erode households that I can cite.

What the sources do show:

1. **MSME District Industrial Profile (2015–16)** lists “Milk and Milk Products” only under §3.14 *Potential areas for new MSMEs* (raw-materials-based). It records dairy as an aspirational opportunity, not as measured existing units, employment, or farmer income. Food processing (NIC-15) is aggregated at **3,539 units** with no dairy breakout.

2. The curated Erode livelihoods gap list explicitly flags this as unsourced: *“Village/ward-level dairy incomes or milk-collection volumes for Erode… No existing dairy-unit count, employment figure, or farmer-income figure was found.”*

**Citations**
- *Brief Industrial Profile of Erode District, 2015–16*, MSME-Development Institute, Chennai (Ministry of MSME): https://dcmsme.gov.in/dips/2016-17/DIP.ERODE.2015.16.pdf  
- Gap note from the same sourcing pass (GAPS on village/ward dairy incomes), tied to that DIC/MSME profile.

To get village-wise monthly dairy income you’d need a primary household survey (or Aavin/TNDDC milk-collection centre records by village), which isn’t in the public documents above.
