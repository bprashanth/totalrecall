Q: How many registered industrial units are there in Erode district as per the MSME district industrial profile? Citations please.
elapsed: 26s attempt:1

---

**19,521** registered industrial units.

That figure is from the Ministry of MSME’s *Brief Industrial Profile of Erode District* (2012), prepared by the MSME-Development Institute, Chennai. It appears under:

- **§3.1 Industry at a Glance** — Registered Industrial Unit: **19,521**
- **§3.2 Year Wise Trend of Units Registered** — cumulative total through 2010–11: **19,521** (source: DIC, Erode)

**Citation:** [Brief Industrial Profile of Erode District, 2012 (dcmsme.gov.in)](https://dcmsme.gov.in/dips/ips%20erode%202012_tn.pdf)
