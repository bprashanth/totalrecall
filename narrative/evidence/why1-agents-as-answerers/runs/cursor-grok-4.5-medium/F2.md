Q: Erode turmeric got GI tag in which year? Citations please.
elapsed: 20s attempt:1

---

**Erode turmeric (Erode Manjal) got its GI tag in 2019** — specifically on **6 March 2019**.

The application was filed on 4 January 2011 by the Erode Manjal Vanigarkal Matrum Kidangu Urimaiyalargal Sangam (Erode Turmeric Traders and Godown Owners Association), and registration was granted under Class 30 (spices).

**Citations**
1. [Spices Board of India – List of GI Tags for Spices](https://www.indianspices.com/sites/default/files/List_of_GItags_for_spices_BJ.pdf) — lists Erode Manjal (App. No. 231) with registration date **6.3.2019**
2. [The Hindu – “Erode turmeric gets GI tag after an 8-year process”](https://www.thehindu.com/news/national/tamil-nadu/erode-turmeric-gets-gi-tag-after-an-8-year-process/article26451340.ece) (March 2019)
3. [Times of India – “10 years after filing of plea, Erode manjal gets GI tag”](https://timesofindia.indiatimes.com/city/chennai/10-yrs-after-filing-of-plea-erode-manjal-gets-gi-tag/articleshow/68294710.cms)
4. [Tamil Nadu Dr. Ambedkar Law University report](https://www.tndalu.ac.in/pdf/2022/july/report_on_erode_manjal.pdf) — states GI conferred on **6 March 2019**
