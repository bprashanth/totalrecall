Q: For MGNREGA work in Tamil Nadu, what is the daily wage rate for 2025-26? Give citations also, I want to check.
elapsed: 26s attempt:1

**₹336 per person per day** for unskilled manual work under MGNREGA in Tamil Nadu for FY 2025-26 (effective 1 April 2025). That is up from ₹319 in 2024-25 (about 5.33%).

### Citations to check

1. **Primary (Centre)** — Ministry of Rural Development notification **S.O. 1463(E), dated 27 March 2025**, Gazette of India. Schedule entry: **Tamil Nadu — Rs. 336.00**; in force from **1 April 2025**.  
   PDF copy of the Gazette text: https://hrinformative.com/wp-content/uploads/2025/06/MGNREGA-New-Wage-Rates-2025-%E2%80%93-State-Wise-List.pdf

2. **State follow-up** — *The Hindu* (6–7 May 2025): TN Rural Development & Panchayat Raj notified the same rate, ₹336/day, with effect from 1 April 2025 (previous rate ₹319).  
   https://www.thehindu.com/news/national/tamil-nadu/state-govt-notifies-hike-in-wage-rate-to-mgnregs-beneficiaries/article69545739.ece

3. **Secondary table (same figures)** — *The Indian Express* state-wise FY26 wage list: Tamil Nadu **319 → 336**.  
   https://indianexpress.com/article/india/mgnregs-wage-rates-hike-fy-2025-26-states-punjab-odisha-mp-9908765/
