Q: Garbage complaints in Bellandur ward, Bengaluru: from 2019 to 2022 did they go up or come down? By how much? Citations.
elapsed: 133s attempt:1

---

**They went up** on the official BBMP record for the years we can compare cleanly.

### BBMP Sahaaya (main source)
Bellandur ward, category **“Solid Waste (Garbage) Related”**:

| Year | Complaints |
|------|------------|
| 2020 | 347 |
| 2021 | 375 |
| 2022 | 431 |

**2020 → 2022: +84 complaints (+24%).**  
Including Sanitation: 356 → 443 (+87, +24%).

**Caveat on 2019:** BBMP Sahaaya 2.0 data on OpenCity starts **8 Feb 2020**, so there is no full official BBMP series for 2019. Even allowing for 2020 being a short year (~327 days), annualizing to ~387 still leaves 2022 higher (~+11%).

**Source:** OpenCity BBMP Grievances Data (ward-level CSVs for 2020–2022), originally from BBMP / Sahaaya —  
https://data.opencity.in/dataset/bbmp-grievances-data

### Why iChangeMyCity looks different
Janaagraha **iChangeMyCity** logs for Bellanduru “Garbage and Unsanitary Practices” fall from **55 (2019) → 3 (2022)**. That is not a reliable measure of garbage conditions: total Bellanduru filings on that portal also collapsed (**444 → 48**), consistent with citizens moving to BBMP Sahaaya.

**Source:** https://data.opencity.in/dataset/i-change-my-city-data

**Bottom line:** For Bellandur, official garbage complaints **rose by about a quarter from 2020 to 2022**; a true 2019 vs 2022 BBMP comparison isn’t available in the public ward datasets.
