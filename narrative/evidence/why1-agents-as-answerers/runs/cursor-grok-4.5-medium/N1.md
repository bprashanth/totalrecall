I couldn’t reach the web from this session, so I can’t quote a live “as of today” figure with checked links. Here’s what public reporting has typically shown for **Erode informal dyeing/processing units**, and where you can verify.

**Typical range (recent years, informal units):** helpers/general workers often around **₹400–700/day**; more skilled dyeing/finishing hands sometimes **₹700–1,000+/day**, depending on shift length, piece-rate vs day-rate, and whether food/overtime is included. Treat this as a ballpark from past press/labour coverage, not a 2026 field survey.

**Citations / places to check:**

1. **Tamil Nadu Labour Department – Minimum Wages**  
   Search: *“Tamil Nadu minimum wages dyeing bleaching”* or *“scheduled employment dyeing”*.  
   Official notifications set floors by skill grade; informal units often pay near or above that in practice, but not always.

2. **Erode district / local press**  
   - *The Hindu* (Erode / Coimbatore) – dyeing unit labour, Cauvery pollution crackdowns, wage disputes  
   - *The Times of India* / *Dinakaran* / *Dinamalar* (Erode) – unit closures and worker pay mentions  

3. **Tirupur–Erode textile labour studies** (spillover wages are often similar)  
   Reports by labour NGOs, university field studies, and Tirupur Exporters’ Association / trade press sometimes publish daily wage bands for processing workers.

4. **District Statistical Handbook / Labour Bureau**  
   Erode District Statistical Handbook and Labour Bureau wage series (manufacturing / textiles) for rough official benchmarks.
