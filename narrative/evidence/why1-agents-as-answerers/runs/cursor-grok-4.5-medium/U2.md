**Going up.** BBMP citizen grievances rose every full year from 2020 through 2025, and early 2026 is still higher than the same period in 2025.

| Year | Complaints | YoY change |
|------|------------|------------|
| 2020 | 91,620 | — |
| 2021 | 103,504 | +13.0% |
| 2022 | 118,394 | +14.4% |
| 2023 | 119,140 | +0.6% |
| 2024 | 207,016 | +73.8% |
| 2025 | 342,560 | +65.5% |

- **2020 → 2025:** 91,620 → 342,560 (**+274%**)
- **H1 2025 vs H1 2026 (Jan–Jun):** 138,644 → 194,413 (**+40%**)
