Q: Erode turmeric got GI tag in which year? Citations please.
elapsed: 18s attempt:1

---

Erode turmeric, officially `Erode Manjal (Erode Turmeric)`, got its GI tag in **2019**.

More precisely, the **certificate date was 6 March 2019**, according to the Government of India GI registry entry. The Spices Board’s GI list also shows `Erode Manjal (Erode Turmeric)` with date `6.3.2019`.

Citations:
- [Geographical Indications Registry, Intellectual Property India - Application 231: Erode Manjal (Erode Turmeric)](https://search.ipindia.gov.in/GIRPublicSearch/Application/Details/231)
- [Spices Board India - List of GI Tags Registered for Spices](https://www.indianspices.com/sites/default/files/List_of_GItags_for_spices_BJ.pdf)
