Q: What was India's unemployment rate in 2021 as per the ILO modelled estimate, the one World Bank publishes? Citation link please.
elapsed: 47s attempt:1

According to the World Bank's World Development Indicators (which publishes the International Labour Organization's modeled estimate), India's total unemployment rate in 2021 was **6.38%** of the total labor force.

### Citation Link
* [World Bank Data: Unemployment, total (% of total labor force) (modeled ILO estimate) - India](https://data.worldbank.org/indicator/SL.UEM.TOTL.ZS?locations=IN)
