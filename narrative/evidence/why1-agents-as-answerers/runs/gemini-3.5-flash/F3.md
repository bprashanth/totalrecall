Q: What is the total population of Erode district as per Census 2011? Give citation link also.

According to the 2011 Census of India, the total population of Erode district is **2,251,744**. 

### Population Breakdown:
* **Male Population:** 1,129,868
* **Female Population:** 1,121,876
