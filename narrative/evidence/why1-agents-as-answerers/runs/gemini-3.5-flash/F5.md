Q: The Kalingarayan canal takes water from which river? Give citations I can check.
elapsed: 18s attempt:1

---

The Kalingarayan canal takes water from the **Bhavani River** (a major tributary of the Kaveri River) in Tamil Nadu, India. 

The canal branches off from the right side of the **Kalingarayan Anicut**, a 13th-century barrage constructed across the Bhavani River just above its confluence with the Kaveri River near Bhavani town in the Erode district. It is one of the oldest river-linking projects in India, designed by the local chieftain Kalingarayan to connect the Bhavani and Noyyal rivers.
