According to the **"Brief Industrial Profile of Erode District (2012)"** prepared by the **MSME-Development Institute, Chennai** (under the Ministry of MSME, Government of India), the details on registered industrial units in Erode district are as follows:

### 1. Total Registered Industrial Units
* **Registered Micro & Small Enterprise (MSE) Units**: **19,521** units (cumulative up to the year 2010–11)
* **Registered Medium & Large Units**: **38** units

---

### 2. Year-Wise Registration Trend
The cumulative total of **19,521** micro and small registered units is broken down by year as follows:

| Year | Number of Registered Units | Employment Generated | Investment (Lakh Rs.) |
| :--- | :---: | :---: | :---: |
| Up to 2007–08 | 15,278 | 71,948 | 2,077,742.00 |
| 2008–09 | 1,358 | 13,631 | 39,219.65 |
| 2009–10 | 1,758 | 14,728 | 211,955.25 |
| 2010–11 | 2,809 | 22,868 | 160,453.46 |
| **Total** | **19,521** | **77,503** | **526,440.38** |

*(Source: District Industries Centre, Erode)*

---

### 3. Key Industry Sectors (Micro & Small Enterprises)
The largest concentrations among the registered micro and small units in the district belong to:
* **Manufacturing of Textiles (NIC Code 17)**: 4,406 units (with an investment of 249,370.07 Lakh Rs. and employing 25,296 people)
* **Manufacturing of Wearing Apparel; Dressing and Dyeing of Fur (NIC Code 18)**: 3,659 units (with an investment of 118,811.66 Lakh Rs. and employing 13,628 people)
* **Manufacturing of Food Products and Beverages (NIC Code 15)**: 3,539 units (with an investment of 46,090.70 Lakh Rs. and employing 11,786 people)
