For the financial year **2025-26** (effective from April 1, 2025), the daily wage rate for MGNREGA (unskilled manual workers) in Tamil Nadu is **₹336 per day**. 

This represents an increase of **₹17** (approximately 5.33%) from the previous year's rate of ₹319.

### Citations

1. **Official Gazette Notification**: 
   The Ministry of Rural Development, Government of India, issued the state-wise wage rate amendment notification under the Mahatma Gandhi National Rural Employment Guarantee Act, 2005. Under the substituted schedule, Tamil Nadu is listed at Serial No. 25 with a wage rate of **Rs. 336.00**.
   * Reference: [Official Gazette Notification PDF](https://hrinformative.com/wp-content/uploads/2025/06/MGNREGA-New-Wage-Rates-2025-%E2%80%93-State-Wise-List.pdf)

2. **Tamil Nadu Government Notification**:
   The Rural Development and Panchayat Raj Department of Tamil Nadu officially notified the daily wage rate of ₹336 per person.
   * Reference: [The Hindu - Tamil Nadu government notifies hike in wage rate to MGNREGS beneficiaries](https://www.thehindu.com/news/national/tamil-nadu/state-govt-notifies-hike-in-wage-rate-to-mgnregs-beneficiaries/article69545739.ece)
