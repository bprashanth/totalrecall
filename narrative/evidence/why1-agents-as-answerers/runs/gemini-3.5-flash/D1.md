Q: Between 2019 and 2022, which ward in Bengaluru had the most garbage complaints? What is the count? Citations please.
elapsed: 149s attempt:1

---

The answer depends on which of the two primary datasets present in this environment is used, as their coverage and categories differ slightly. Both views are detailed below with their respective counts and citations.

---

### 1. View via BBMP Grievances Portal (Sahaaya)
* **Dataset File:** `citizen-grievances.csv` (BBMP Sahaaya logs)
* **Coverage:** Covers **2020–2022** (there is no 2019 data available in this official feed).
* **Category:** `"Solid Waste (Garbage) Related"`

Under the official government portal, the ward with the most garbage complaints is **Rajarajeshwari Nagar** with **2,642 complaints**.

#### Top 5 Wards (2020–2022):
| Rank | Ward Name | Complaint Count |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Rajarajeshwari Nagar** | **2,642** |
| 2 | Thanisandra | 2,363 |
| 3 | Jnanabharathi Ward | 2,304 |
| 4 | Vidyaranyapura | 1,536 |
| 5 | Someshwara | 1,398 |

* **Citation:** Official BBMP Sahaaya Portal, hosted on [OpenCity BBMP Grievances Data](https://data.opencity.in/dataset/bbmp-grievances-data).

---

### 2. View via Janaagraha iChangeMyCity (iCMyC)
* **Dataset File:** `icmc_complaints_2019_2022.csv`
* **Coverage:** Fully covers **2019–2022** inclusive.
* **Category:** `"Garbage and Unsanitary Practices"` (and optionally `"Sanitation"`)

If we look at the iChangeMyCity dataset, excluding the general placeholder label `"Other"` (which had 156 complaints), the top named ward is **Horamavu** with **100 complaints** (or **104** if combined with the related `"Sanitation"` category).

#### Top 5 Named Wards (2019–2022):

**Category: "Garbage and Unsanitary Practices" Only**
1. **Horamavu**: **100**
2. HSR Layout: 98
3. Bellanduru: 90
4. Begur: 79
5. BTM Layout: 75

**Categories: "Garbage and Unsanitary Practices" + "Sanitation" Combined**
1. **Horamavu**: **104**
2. HSR Layout: 98
3. Bellanduru: 90
4. Begur: 82
5. BTM Layout: 75

* **Citation:** Janaagraha iChangeMyCity Complaints Log (2019–2022), hosted on [OpenCity iChangeMyCity Complaints Data](https://data.opencity.in/dataset/i-change-my-city-data).
