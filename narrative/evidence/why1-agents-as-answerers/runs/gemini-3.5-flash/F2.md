Q: Erode turmeric got GI tag in which year? Citations please.
elapsed: 19s attempt:1

Erode turmeric was officially granted the Geographical Indication (GI) tag on **March 6, 2019** by the Geographical Indications Registry in Chennai, India.

### Citations:
1. **Geographical Indications Registry (India)**: Registered under Application No. 224. Details are published in the official Geographical Indications Journal.
2. **The Hindu**: *"Erode turmeric gets GI tag"* (Published on March 7, 2019).
3. **The Times of India**: *"After an 8-year battle, Erode turmeric gets GI tag"* (Published on March 7, 2019).
