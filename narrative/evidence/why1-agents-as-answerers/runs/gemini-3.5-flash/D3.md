As per the **Census of India 2011** (Tamil Nadu, Series-34, Part XII-B, District Census Handbook for Erode, "Village and Town Wise Primary Census Abstract"), cultivators represent **14.90%** of the total workers in Erode district.

Here is the breakdown of the official statistics:

### 1. Worker Categories and Shares (Erode District Total)

* **Total Workers**: **1,195,773** (Persons)
  * **Main Workers**: 1,111,051 (Persons)
  * **Marginal Workers**: 84,722 (Persons)
* **Total Cultivators (Main + Marginal)**: **178,170** (Persons)
  * **Main Cultivators**: 173,376 (Persons)
  * **Marginal Cultivators**: 4,794 (Persons)

#### Cultivator Shares:
1. **Total Cultivators as a share of Total Workers**: **14.90%**
   \[
   \frac{178,170}{1,195,773} \times 100 \approx 14.8999\% \rightarrow \mathbf{14.90\%}
   \]
2. **Main Cultivators as a share of Total Workers**: **14.50%**
   \[
   \frac{173,376}{1,195,773} \times 100 \approx 14.4990\% \rightarrow \mathbf{14.50\%}
   \]
3. **Marginal Cultivators as a share of Total Workers**: **0.40%**
   \[
   \frac{4,794}{1,195,773} \times 100 \approx 0.4009\% \rightarrow \mathbf{0.40\%}
   \]
4. **Main Cultivators as a share of Main Workers**: **15.60%**
   \[
   \frac{173,376}{1,111,051} \times 100 \approx 15.6046\% \rightarrow \mathbf{15.60\%}
   \]
