Q: In 2020, how many road related complaints came from Hoodi ward in Bengaluru? Citation also.

Depending on the dataset referenced, there are two different counts for road-related grievances in Hoodi ward in 2020:

### 1. BBMP Sahaaya / Nammabengaluru App Grievances
In the BBMP grievances dataset (`citizen-grievances.csv` and `bbmp_2020.csv`), there are **332** complaints under the **"Road Maintenance(Engg)"** category. If you also include the 2 complaints for **"Without permission digging of road"** under the "Optical Fiber Cables (OFC)" category, the total is **334**.

**Breakdown of "Road Maintenance(Engg)" (332 complaints):**
* **Potholes:** 134
* **Road side drains:** 80
* **Road cutting:** 45
* **Water stagnation:** 36
* **Footpath encroachment:** 15
* **Debris Removal / Construction Material:** 10
* **Water leakage on road:** 7
* **Footpath:** 5

---

### 2. ICMC Complaints Dataset
In the ICMC complaints dataset (`icmc_complaints_2019_2022.csv`), there are **25** complaints under the **"Mobility - Roads, Footpaths and Infrastructure"** category. If you also include the 3 footpath-related complaints listed under the "Yellow Spot" category, the total is **28**.

**Breakdown of "Mobility - Roads, Footpaths and Infrastructure" (25 complaints):**
* **Tarring Or Asphalting Of Mud/Kutcha/Unpaved Road:** 7
* **Tarring Or Asphalting Of Existing Road:** 6
* **Fixing/Reparing Potholes:** 5
* **Construction Of Roadside Drains:** 3
* **Flooding/Waterlogging Of Roads And Footpaths:** 2
* **Management Of Hawkers and Vendors:** 1
* **Need Roadside Rainwater Harvesting System:** 1
