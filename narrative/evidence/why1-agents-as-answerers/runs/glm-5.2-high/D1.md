## Answer

Between 2019 and 2022, the Bengaluru ward with the most garbage complaints was **Rajarajeshwari Nagar**, with **2,642 complaints**.

The analysis filtered the `citizen-grievances.csv` dataset to rows where:
- `Category` contains "garbage" (matched the single category `Solid Waste (Garbage) Related`)
- `Grievance Date` year is between 2019 and 2022 inclusive

This yielded 71,034 total garbage complaints across all wards in that period. The top 10 wards were:
