## What I know (and don't know)

- **Erode** (Tamil Nadu) has a large informal textile/dyeing cluster around areas like **Chithode, Perundurai, and the SIPCOT belt**, with many unregistered bleaching and dyeing units operating outside the formal wage/EPF framework.
- Wages in these informal units are typically **piece-rated or daily-rated, paid in cash, and fluctuate with order flow and water/dye-chemical seasonality**. They also differ sharply by role — a dyeing helper (`coolie`), a dyeing master/operator, a loader, and a fabric folder are paid very different rates.

## Where you can get a citable figure

These are sources you can check directly and cite:

1. **Tamil Nadu Minimum Wages Notification** — Labour Department, GoTN. Check the scheduled employment entry for "Textile" / "Dyeing and Bleaching". The *minimum* (not actual informal) wage is the legal floor:
   - https://labour.tn.gov.in/ → "Minimum Wages" → search "Dyeing" / "Textile"
   - Citation form: Government of Tamil Nadu, Labour Department, Minimum Wages Notification (date), scheduled employment: Dyeing and Bleaching.

2. **Tirupur/Erode-based labour NGOs and studies** — these have done field surveys of informal dyeing unit workers:
   - **SAVE (Social Awareness and Voluntary Education)**, Tirupur — has published reports on textile/dyeing labour conditions in the Tirupur–Erode belt.
   - **Cividep** (Chennai/Coimbatore) — garment and textile labour research; ask for their dyeing-unit wage data.
   - **Tirupur People's Forum / Garment Labour Union** reports.

3. **Tirupur Exporters' Association (TEA)** and **Erode Textile Cluster** industry bodies — sometimes release wage band surveys for knit/dyeing labour.

4. **Periodic Labour Force Survey (PLFS), NSSO / MoSPI** — gives casual/daily-wage rates for "textile manufacturing" in Tamil Nadu, which you can use as a benchmark (though not Erode-specific).

5. **Newspaper field reports** — *The Hindu* (Tamil Nadu bureau), *Times of India* Coimbatore edition, and *Puthiya Thalaimurai* / *Hindu Tamil Thisai* occasionally run on-the-ground pieces quoting dyeing unit wages. Search their archives for "Erode dyeing" / "சாயமிடும் தொழில்" / "Erode சாயமிடும் குடிசை".

6. **Dutch/German due-diligence reports** — the Tirupur dyeing cluster has been studied under *ZDHC* and *Clean by Design* / *Wet Processing* audits; some of these reports include wage data in their social-compliance sections.
