I can't answer this without more information, and I won't make up employment figures — fabricated data with citations would be misleading.

To get real, citable employment data for a village-level comparison, I need:

1. **The names of both villages** (and ideally the district/tehsil/block)
2. **The country** — village-level employment data sources differ a lot:
   - **India**: Census of India 2011 (village-level data is downloadable via the Village Directory / DCHB), Periodic Labour Force Survey (PLFS) is district-level only, not village-level
   - **USA**: Census Bureau ACS 5-year estimates (block group/tract level)
   - **UK**: NOMIS / ONS (parish-level is limited)
   - **Other countries**: their national statistics office

3. **What "doing better on employment" means to you** — e.g., workforce participation rate, unemployment rate, share in non-agricultural work, or main vs. marginal workers

One important caveat: most official employment surveys are designed to be reliable at the **district or state level**, not the individual-village level. So even with the names, the honest answer may be that only Census-style village directories (which classify workers, not unemployment) are available per village — and those are often a decade old.

If you tell me the two village names, district, and country, I'll pull the best available official numbers and cite the source for each.
