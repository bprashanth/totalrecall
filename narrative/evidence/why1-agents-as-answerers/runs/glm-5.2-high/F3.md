As per **Census 2011**, the total population of **Erode district** (Tamil Nadu) was **22,51,744** (approximately 2.25 million).

**Breakdown:**
- **Males:** 11,34,603
- **Females:** 11,17,141
- **Population density:** ~400 persons per sq. km
- **Literacy rate:** ~72.55%
