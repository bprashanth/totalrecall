Short answer: **experienced dyeing-unit jobs in Perundurai usually pay better than farm work nearby; entry-level factory jobs do not always.**

The cleanest comparison I could verify is this:

- A **Perundurai textile quality-checker** opening listed **`₹10,299-₹15,400/month + overtime`** plus hostel and meals. Source: [Jobnow247live / Sakthi Infra Tex](https://jobnow247live.com/jobdetail/270/Sakthi-Infra-Tex-Hiring-T-Shirt-Quality-Checker-Job-2025).

For nearby farm work, the best public numbers I found are **Tamil Nadu rural wage averages** from CEIC, which says the data is reported by the **Labour Bureau**:

- **General agricultural labour, Tamil Nadu, Nov 2025**: **men `₹626.13/day`**, **women `₹381.01/day`**. Source: [CEIC general agricultural wages](https://www.ceicdata.com/en/india/average-daily-wage-rate-rural-agricultural-by-state-general-agricultural-including-watering-and-irrigation).
- **Sowing / planting / transplanting / weeding, Tamil Nadu, Nov 2025**: **men `₹715.21/day`**, **women `₹453.89/day`**. Source: [CEIC sowing/weeding wages](https://www.ceicdata.com/en/india/average-daily-wage-rate-rural-agricultural-by-state-sowing-including-planting-transplanting-and-weeding).
- As a public rural-work benchmark, Tamil Nadu’s **MGNREGS** wage was raised to **`₹336/day`** from April 1, 2025. Source: [DT Next](https://www.dtnext.in/news/tamilnadu/mgnregs-wage-in-tn-to-go-up-by-rs-15-rs-336-per-day-from-april-1-828120).

So the practical read is:

- **Skilled dyeing-side roles**: yes, they pay clearly better than farm work.
- **Entry-level factory/helper/checking roles**: they are **often better than women’s average farm wages and better than MGNREGS**, but **not obviously better than men’s peak-season farm wages** in Tamil Nadu.
- Factory jobs may still win on **steady monthly income, overtime, hostel/meal support, PF/ESI**, while farm work can win on **cash-in-hand per day** during busy seasons.

One caution: the **Perundurai factory numbers are local job ads**, while the **farm numbers are Tamil Nadu-wide averages**, not a Perundurai-only survey. That means this is a solid directional answer, but not a perfect street-by-street wage map.
