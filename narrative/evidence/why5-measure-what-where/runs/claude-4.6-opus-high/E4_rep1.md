## Short answer: Yes, dyeing units in Perundurai generally pay more than nearby farm work.

Here's the comparison:

### Dyeing / Textile Processing (Perundurai SIPCOT area)

| Role | Monthly pay |
|------|-------------|
| Entry-level (quality checker, operator) | ₹10,000–₹18,000/month |
| Lab technician / mid-level | ₹18,000–₹35,000/month |
| Dyeing & finishing supervisor | ₹25,000–₹35,000/month |

These are regular, full-time positions often including PF, ESI, canteen, and accommodation.

### Agricultural labour (Erode / nearby districts)

| Metric | Rate |
|--------|------|
| Tamil Nadu average agricultural daily wage | ~₹540/day |
| Cotton-belt peak demand wage (Perambalur/Erode region) | ₹500–₹700/day |

At ₹540/day for ~25 working days that's roughly ₹13,500/month — but farm work is seasonal, and many workers don't get 25 days of employment every month.

### Why the gap persists

A DFID-funded study of the Tiruppur/Perundurai cluster found that garment/dyeing wages are "generally higher than daily agricultural wages — the main alternative source of employment for people living in the villages around Tiruppur," and that industrial demand has tightened the rural labour market, forcing landowners to raise farm wages as well.
