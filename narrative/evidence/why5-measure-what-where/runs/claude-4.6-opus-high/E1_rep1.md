# Livelihood Survey Design for Erode District — Rs 2 Lakh Budget

## 1. What Already Exists (Don't Re-collect)

Substantial livelihood data for Erode is already published. Use these instead of spending survey budget:

### Demographics & Basic Socioeconomics
- **Census of India 2011, District Census Handbook – Erode** (Directorate of Census Operations, Tamil Nadu): Population, workers/non-workers, main vs marginal workers, industrial category of workers (cultivator, agricultural labourer, household industry, other), SC/ST population, literacy — all at village/town level. Available at censusindia.gov.in.
- **Census 2021 data** (when released) will update these figures.

### Agriculture
- **Season and Crop Report, Department of Economics & Statistics, Government of Tamil Nadu** (annual): Block-wise area, production, and yield for every major crop in Erode (turmeric, sugarcane, paddy, coconut, banana, maize, etc.). Published annually.
- **Agricultural Census 2015-16** (Department of Agriculture, Cooperation & Farmers' Welfare, GoI): Operational holdings by size class, tenancy, irrigation status at district level. Available at agcensus.nic.in.
- **Groundwater Resource Assessment, CGWB/State Ground and Surface Water Resources Data Centre**: Block-wise groundwater exploitation stage — critical for understanding irrigated agriculture viability.

### Industry & Employment
- **Annual Survey of Industries (ASI)** (MoSPI): Factory-sector employment, output, and wages — Erode has significant textile (especially knitwear and handloom) and turmeric processing units.
- **Periodic Labour Force Survey (PLFS)** (NSSO/MoSPI): Quarterly and annual estimates of employment, unemployment, wages, and informality. State-level (Tamil Nadu) estimates are published; district-level unit data available for researchers on request from MoSPI microdata portal.
- **District Industries Centre (DIC) Erode — MSME registration data**: Number and type of registered MSMEs, available from the DIC or MSME-databank (udyamregistration.gov.in).

### Poverty & Consumption
- **SECC 2011 (Socio-Economic and Caste Census)**: Household deprivation indicators at village level. Summary data released by MoRD.
- **NFHS-5 (2019-21), District Fact Sheet – Erode** (IIPS/MoHFW): Wealth quintiles, women's employment, asset ownership, nutrition — all at district level. Available at rchiips.org/nfhs.
- **Multidimensional Poverty Index — NITI Aayog (2023)**, district-level estimates based on NFHS-5.

### Migration & Remittances
- **Census 2011 Migration Tables (D-series)**: In-migration and out-migration by reason, duration, origin — district level.

### Markets & Prices
- **Agmarknet (agmarknet.gov.in)**: Daily mandi prices for turmeric, coconut, banana, etc. from Erode APMC — one of India's largest turmeric markets. Historical data freely downloadable.

## 2. What You MUST Collect (Gaps That Existing Data Cannot Fill)

Existing data is weak on **informal livelihoods, household-level income composition, coping strategies, and intra-district variation**. These are where your Rs 2 lakh should go.

### Recommended Survey Modules

| Module | Key Variables | Why It's Not Available |
|---|---|---|
| **Household income portfolio** | Share of income from farm, farm-labour, MGNREGA, non-farm enterprise, salaried job, remittances, pensions/transfers | Census gives worker category, not income shares |
| **Informal/non-farm enterprises** | Type, revenue, inputs, seasonality, credit source, number of workers | DIC only covers registered MSMEs |
| **Agricultural risk & coping** | Crop loss events, insurance uptake (PMFBY), response strategies, debt | Aggregate crop insurance data exists but not household-level experience |
| **Market access & value chains** | Where produce is sold, price received vs mandi price, intermediaries, transport cost | Agmarknet gives mandi arrivals, not farmgate reality |
| **Migration (seasonal/circular)** | Duration, destination, occupation, remittance amount, household left behind | Census captures only "permanent" migration |
| **Women's livelihoods** | SHG membership, independent income, decision-making, time use | NFHS has some; not enough for livelihood analysis |
| **Access to schemes** | MGNREGA days worked, PM-Kisan receipt, PDS, PMJJBY, housing scheme | Administrative data exists but household-level uptake + leakage isn't visible |

## 3. Where to Sample (Stratification)

Erode district has **4 revenue divisions** and **14 blocks** with distinct livelihood zones. Stratify by:

| Stratum | Blocks (examples) | Dominant Livelihoods |
|---|---|---|
| **Irrigated plains (Cauvery/Bhavani command)** | Bhavani, Erode, Modakkurichi | Paddy, sugarcane, banana; textile wage work |
| **Dryland/rainfed** | Anthiyur, Sathyamangalam (plains portion) | Millets, groundnut, livestock, rain-dependent |
| **Turmeric belt** | Gobichettipalayam, Bhavanisagar | Turmeric, coconut; linked to Erode mandi |
| **Forest-fringe / tribal** | Sathyamangalam (hills), Thalavadi | NTFP, plantation labour, tribal livelihoods (Irula, Kurumba communities) |
| **Urban/peri-urban** | Erode Corporation, Perundurai | Textile industry, trade, services, construction |

## 4. Budget Allocation (Rs 2,00,000)

With this budget, target **~300–400 households** across 20–25 villages/wards (12–16 HHs per village), using a structured questionnaire of ~45 minutes.

| Item | Amount (Rs) |
|---|---|
| Enumerator honorarium (4 enumerators × 20 days × Rs 800/day) | 64,000 |
| Travel & field logistics (fuel, local transport) | 30,000 |
| Supervisor / field coordinator (1 × 20 days × Rs 1,000/day) | 20,000 |
| Tablet/phone-based data collection (KoboToolbox — free; SIM/data costs) | 5,000 |
| Printing, pilot testing, training workshop (2 days) | 12,000 |
| Key informant interviews (village leaders, FPO heads, SHG leaders — 25 KIIs) | 10,000 |
| Focus group discussions (8 FGDs — one per stratum, gender-separated) | 12,000 |
| Data cleaning, entry, analysis support | 25,000 |
| Contingency (~10%) | 22,000 |
| **Total** | **2,00,000** |

## 5. What You Can Estimate From This Design

| From Primary Survey (new data) | From Secondary Data (existing, cited above) |
|---|---|
| Income diversification index by stratum | Total workforce structure (Census 2011) |
| Informal enterprise density & characteristics | Crop area, yield, production trends (Season & Crop Report) |
| Seasonal migration rates & patterns | Groundwater stress by block (CGWB) |
| Scheme coverage vs entitlement gaps | Market prices & arrivals (Agmarknet) |
| Gender dimensions of livelihood control | Health/nutrition/asset indicators (NFHS-5) |
| Climate/risk exposure & coping capacity | Poverty headcount (SECC/MPI) |
| Value chain bottlenecks (turmeric, banana, textiles) | Factory employment & wages (ASI) |

---

## 6. Key Recommendations

1. **Use KoboToolbox** (free, open-source) for mobile data collection — saves data entry costs and reduces errors.
2. **Pilot in 2 villages first** (1 day) to test skip logic and timing.
3. **Oversample tribal/forest-fringe blocks** (Sathyamangalam, Thalavadi) — these are most data-poor and have distinct livelihood systems.
4. **Pair every primary indicator with its secondary benchmark** — e.g., compare your farm income estimates against Season & Crop Report yields × Agmarknet prices as a consistency check.
5. **Conduct 8 FGDs** (one per stratum × gender) to capture qualitative dimensions (why people choose certain livelihoods, barriers to diversification) that structured surveys miss.

This design maximizes insight per rupee by spending primary collection budget only on what cannot be found in existing public data.
