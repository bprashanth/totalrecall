Q: How many powerloom workers are living in Chithode area today? Citations please.

I was unable to find a precise, authoritative count of powerloom workers specifically living in the Chithode area today. Here's what the available data shows:

**District-level data (Erode District):**
- More than **50,000 powerloom weavers and ancillary workers** benefit from government schemes across Erode District, where Chithode is one of the major powerloom hubs.
  - *Source: [Department of Handlooms, Erode District](https://erode.nic.in/handlooms-erode/)*

**Cluster-level data (Chithode + surrounding areas):**
- More than **60,000 powerlooms** function across Chithode, Lakkapuram, Modakkuruchi, Chennimalai, and Vellode combined, employing more than **20,000 weavers directly** (plus thousands more in indirect jobs).
  - *Source: [Times of India, Erode](https://timesofindia.indiatimes.com/city/erode/anti-caa-protests-hit-power-loom-units/articleshow/73062503.cms)*

**Chithode town population:**
- Total population of Chithode Town Panchayat was **12,034** (2011 Census), with roughly 51.4% employed. The census does not break down employment by specific industry (e.g., powerloom work).
  - *Source: [Census India 2011 – Chithode](https://www.censusindia2011.com/tamil-nadu/erode/erode/chithode-tp-population.html)*

**Bottom line:** No government or industry source provides a standalone figure for how many powerloom workers live specifically within Chithode town limits. The data is aggregated at either the district level (~50,000+ weavers and ancillary workers) or the multi-town cluster level (~20,000+ direct weavers across Chithode and neighboring areas). A precise Chithode-only number would require local panchayat or cooperative society records that are not publicly available online.
