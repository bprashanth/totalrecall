Q: What is youth unemployment in Anthiyur block of Erode district right now? Give citations I can check.
elapsed:78s

---

**Short answer: There is no published, real-time "youth unemployment rate" for Anthiyur block specifically.** No government survey or dataset publishes block-level youth unemployment figures for India. Here's what is available, with citations:

---

### What the data actually covers

1. **Census 2011 — Anthiyur Town Panchayat work profile** (the most granular official data for Anthiyur):
   - Of 19,237 persons aged 7+, **9,167 were "not employed"** (this includes students, homemakers, retirees — not just job-seekers).
   - 10,070 were working (89.94% main workers, 10.06% marginal workers).
   - No age-disaggregated unemployment breakdown is provided.
   - **Citation:** [Census of India 2011 — Anthiyur Town Panchayat](https://www.census2011.co.in/data/town/803512-anthiyur-tamil-nadu.html) and [City Population — Anthiyur](https://citypopulation.de/en/india/tamilnadu/erode/3341009000__anthiyur/)

2. **Periodic Labour Force Survey (PLFS) 2024-25 — national/state level only** (most recent, released March 2026):
   - India-wide **youth (15–29) unemployment rate: 9.9%** (down from 10.3% the prior year).
   - Rural youth: 8.3%; urban youth: 13.6%.
   - The PLFS provides some district-level estimates for Erode, but **not block-level** and not youth-specific at the district level.
   - **Citation:** [PLFS Annual Report 2024-25, Press Note (MoSPI)](https://www.mospi.gov.in/uploads/latestReleases/latest_release_1774607827733_3e8964a9-268b-4cc9-ad65-cfc8a9e32f08_Press_note_AR_PLFS_2025_23032025_V2.1_26032026_final.pdf) and [PIB Press Release](https://www.pib.gov.in/PressReleasePage.aspx?PRID=2246009)

3. **Qualitative reporting on Anthiyur / Bargur Hills:**
   - The Hindu (2026 election coverage) reports widespread economic hardship, high school dropout rates due to migration, limited local livelihood opportunities, and delays in MGNREGA wage payments in Anthiyur constituency, particularly among tribal communities in the Bargur Hills.
   - **Citation:** [The Hindu — "Vedaparai reservoir project remains a long-pending demand in Anthiyur constituency"](https://www.thehindu.com/elections/tamil-nadu-assembly/vedaparai-reservoir-project-remains-a-long-pending-demand-in-anthiyur-constituency/article70791771.ece)

4. **Government employment schemes active in the area:**
   - **UYEGP** (Unemployed Youth Employment Generation Programme) — loans/subsidies for self-employment for youth 18–35 (general) / 18–45 (SC/ST/BC/MBC). **Citation:** [Erode District — UYEGP](https://erode.nic.in/scheme/unemployed-youth-employment-generation-proramme/)
   - **TORCH** (Tribal Employment Outreach for Remote Communities) — free coaching for competitive exams for tribal youth in Thalavadi, Kadambur, and Burgur (part of Anthiyur). ST literacy rate is 44.69% vs. district average 72.6%. **Citation:** [Ministry of Tribal Affairs — TORCH case study](https://knowledge.tribal.gov.in/case-studies/torch-tribal-employment-outreach-for-remote-communities-in-erode/)

5. **MGNREGA coverage for Erode district (July 2026):**
   - Employment provided to 43,942 of 1,83,819 registered households (~23.9%). Not block- or age-specific.
   - **Citation:** [The Hindu — DISHA panel reviews schemes in Erode (July 8, 2026)](https://www.thehindu.com/news/cities/Coimbatore/disha-panel-reviews-implementation-of-central-government-schemes-in-erode/article71198008.ece)

---

### Bottom line

No agency publishes a block-level youth unemployment rate for Anthiyur. The finest-grained official labor data for this area is the 2011 Census (now 15 years old). For anything more current, you would need to contact the **District Employment Office, Erode** or the **Block Development Office, Anthiyur** directly, or check whether Indiastat.com has district-level PLFS breakdowns behind their paywall at [indiastatdistrictinfra.com/tamilnadu/erode](https://www.indiastatdistrictinfra.com/tamilnadu/erode/labourandworkforce/employmentsituation).
