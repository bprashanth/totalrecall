Q: How many powerloom workers are living in Chithode area today? Citations please.
elapsed:51s

---

**No published figure isolates how many powerloom workers live in Chithode today.** Available sources only give district- or multi-locality totals that include Chithode.

### Closest cited figures

| Scope | Figure | Source |
|--------|--------|--------|
| **Erode district** (incl. Chithode) | **More than 50,000** powerloom weavers and ancillary workers | [Erode District Handlooms Dept.](https://erode.nic.in/handlooms-erode/) |
| **Veerappanchatiram, Ashokapuram, Manickampalayam, Lakkapuram & Chithode** (rayon units) | ~**30,000** units; ~**50,000** workers directly involved; ~**1 lakh** directly + indirectly | [The Hindu, July 2022](https://www.thehindu.com/news/national/tamil-nadu/one-lakh-workers-in-erode-lose-livelihood-as-rayon-power-loom-units-halt-production-for-a-week/article65598166.ece) |
| **Chithode & Veerappanchatiram** | **Over 30,000** powerloom units (units, not resident workers) | [The Hindu, March 2021](https://www.thehindu.com/news/cities/Coimbatore/pollution-a-grave-concern-in-the-region/article34086165.ece) |
| **Veerappanchatram, Chithode & Lakkapuram** | **>25,000** units; ~**1 lakh** people employed | [Daijiworld / IANS, 2021](https://daijiworld.com/news/newsDisplay?newsID=825741) |

Chithode is officially listed as a main powerloom area alongside Ashokapuram, Veerapanchatram, Lakkapuram, Sulai, and Perundurai ([erode.nic.in](https://erode.nic.in/handlooms-erode/)).

### Census (not powerloom-specific)

Census 2011 for Chithode Town Panchayat: **4,166** total workers (all occupations); estimated town population ~**12,600** in 2026 ([census2011.co.in](https://www.census2011.co.in/data/town/803537-chithode-tamil-nadu.html)). That does **not** break out powerloom workers or “living in the area today.”

**Bottom line:** Public sources do not give a Chithode-only resident powerloom-worker count for today. The usable cited range for the Chithode-centered cluster is on the order of **tens of thousands of workers** (often ~50,000 direct / ~1 lakh with indirect), shared with neighboring localities—not a single Chithode-resident number.
