Q: What is youth unemployment in Anthiyur block of Erode district right now? Give citations I can check.
elapsed:366s

---

**There is no published youth unemployment rate for Anthiyur block.** Official labour surveys do not release block-level (or even reliable district-level) unemployment rates for places like Anthiyur.

Closest figures you can check:

### Closest local proxy — Erode district (not Anthiyur-only, not youth-only)
As of **31 March 2024**, Erode’s employment exchange had **1,25,398** people on the live register (67,444 women). In 2023–24, **7,104** people newly registered; placements were **114** government and **2,337** private.

- Source: *District Statistical Handbook 2023–24*, Erode District, Tables 21.1–21.3  
- PDF: https://cdn.s3waas.gov.in/s3bca82e41ee7b0833588399b1fcd177c7/uploads/2025/01/2025012713.pdf  
- Listing page: https://erode.nic.in/document-category/statistical-report/

A live register is **not** an unemployment rate (it includes people of many ages and registration statuses).

### Closest youth rate — Tamil Nadu (PLFS, ages 15–29)
From the **Tamil Nadu Economic Survey 2024–25** (Chart 6.4, based on PLFS):

| Year | TN male youth UR | TN female youth UR |
|------|------------------|--------------------|
| 2023–24 | **12.7%** | **21.9%** |
| 2022–23 | 15.1% | 23.8% |

India comparison for 2023–24 in the same chart: male **9.8%**, female **11%**.

- Survey PDF: https://spc.tn.gov.in/wp-content/uploads/ES_TN.pdf  
- SPC page: https://spc.tn.gov.in/

Earlier MoSPI/DGE compilation (all persons, rural+urban): Tamil Nadu youth UR **20.0%** in 2021–22 and **17.5%** in 2022–23.

- https://dge.gov.in/sites/default/files/2024-06/Employment_Indicators_in_India_for_youth.pdf

### India-wide youth rate (for context)
India youth UR (15–29, usual status): **10.3%** (2024) → **9.9%** (2025).

- PIB: https://www.pib.gov.in/PressReleasePage.aspx?PRID=2246009  
- MoSPI PLFS press note: https://www.mospi.gov.in/uploads/latestReleases/latest_release_1774607827733_3e8964a9-268b-4cc9-ad65-cfc8a9e32f08_Press_note_AR_PLFS_2025_23032025_V2.1_26032026_final.pdf

**Bottom line:** No current Anthiyur-block youth unemployment rate exists in public official data. The best checkable numbers are Erode’s employment-exchange live register and Tamil Nadu’s PLFS youth rates above.Those background document pulls finished and confirmed the figures already shared: no Anthiyur-block youth unemployment rate is published; Tamil Nadu’s 2023–24 youth UR was **12.7%** (male) and **21.9%** (female) in the Economic Survey, and Erode’s live register stood at **1,25,398** as of 31 March 2024.
