While there is no official, block-level statistical percentage published specifically for the **Anthiyur block**, the youth unemployment situation in this region of Erode district, Tamil Nadu, is well-documented through recent state surveys, district initiatives, and news reports.

---

### 1. Youth Unemployment Context & Statistics
* **State-Level Youth Unemployment (Ages 15–29):** According to the *Tamil Nadu Budget & Economic Survey 2025-26* (released in May 2025), the youth unemployment rate in Tamil Nadu stands at **12.7% for males** and **21.9% for females**, which is higher than the national averages (9.8% and 11% respectively).
* **Literacy & Skill Gaps in Erode:** In Erode district, the Scheduled Tribe (ST) literacy rate is **44.69%**, compared to the district average of **72.6%**. This educational disparity severely restricts local youth from securing formal employment.
* **Rural Employment Scheme Coverage:** As of July 2026, the District Development Coordination and Monitoring Committee (DISHA) reported that out of 183,819 households registered under the rural employment scheme (MGNREGS) in Erode district, only 43,942 households had been actively provided employment.

---

### 2. Local Situation in Anthiyur Block
Anthiyur constituency spans both plains and the remote **Bargur Hills** (Burgur). The lack of local livelihood opportunities has created several distinct challenges:
* **Distress Migration:** Due to a lack of agro-based or industrial jobs in the hills, youth frequently migrate to the plains (or nearby industrial hubs like Tiruppur and Coimbatore) for daily-wage labor.
* **School Dropouts & Debt:** Limited local work has led to high school dropout rates, and many families are caught in debt cycles, forcing youth into early employment as manual laborers.

---

### 3. Targeted Government Interventions
To combat youth unemployment in Anthiyur and surrounding hilly blocks, several targeted schemes are active:
* **TORCH (Tribal Employment Outreach for Remote Communities):** Launched by the District Employment and Career Guidance Centre in Erode, this initiative specifically targets tribal youth in remote forest areas, including the **Bargur hills in Anthiyur**. It provides free competitive exam coaching and capacity building locally to eliminate the high cost of traveling to cities.
* **The Tholkudi Scheme:** Implemented by the Adi Dravidar and Tribal Welfare Department, this scheme conducts door-to-door surveys to identify unemployed tribal youth and sponsors them for specialized skill training (e.g., healthcare, Customer Relationship Management, and Computer Numerical Control) with guaranteed job placements.
* **UYEGP (Unemployed Youth Employment Generation Programme):** A district-level scheme in Erode providing educated unemployed youth (ages 18–35 for general, up to 45 for special categories) with subsidized bank loans (up to ₹10 lakh for manufacturing, ₹3 lakh for services) and a 25% government subsidy to start local businesses.
