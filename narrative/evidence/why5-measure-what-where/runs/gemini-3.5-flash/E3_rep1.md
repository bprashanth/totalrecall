While government databases and the Census of India do not publish isolated, real-time population registers specifically counting powerloom workers residing exclusively within the **Chithode Town Panchayat** boundary today, we can determine a highly accurate estimate using official census records, state handloom data, and regional industry reports.

Based on local demographics and textile cluster statistics, the following is a breakdown of the powerloom workforce in Chithode:

### 1. Chithode Town Panchayat Demographics & Workforce (2026 Estimates)
* **Total Projected Population (2026):** ~12,600 residents (projected from the [Census 2011 Chithode TP Database](https://www.census2011.co.in/data/town/803537-chithode-tamil-nadu.html)).
* **Estimated Working Population (2026):** ~6,100 active workers (extrapolated from the 2011 workforce participation rate of 48.7%).
* **Occupational Context:** In Chithode, nearly 80% of the active workforce is categorized under "Other Workers" (non-agricultural and non-traditional household artisans) in the census. This group largely comprises decentralized factory-style powerloom laborers, as Chithode is primarily an industrial rather than a traditional home-weaving hub.

### 2. The Scale of the Powerloom Sector in Chithode & Erode
* **A Concentrated Cluster:** Chithode is officially designated by the Tamil Nadu government as one of the primary powerloom hubs in the Erode district alongside Ashokapuram, Veerappanchatram, and Lakkapuram (see [Official Portal of Erode District, Gov of Tamil Nadu](https://erode.nic.in/handlooms-erode/)).
* **Active Units:** There are approximately **30,000 powerloom units** concentrated specifically within Chithode and Veerappanchatram (reported by [The Hindu](https://www.thehindu.com/news/cities/Coimbatore/pollution-a-grave-concern-in-the-region/article34086165.ece)). These units focus extensively on rayon fabric manufacturing and produce a significant portion of the free dhotis and sarees distributed by the Tamil Nadu government.
* **Regional Employment:** Across the greater Erode district, there are over **25,000–55,000 units** employing approximately **50,000 weavers directly** and up to **100,000 workers indirectly** (loadmen, drivers, sizing/knotting, and logistics), as reported by [Daijiworld](https://daijiworld.com/index.php/news/newsDisplay?newsID=825741) and [The Hindu](https://www.thehindu.com/news/national/tamil-nadu/one-lakh-workers-in-erode-lose-livelihood-as-rayon-power-loom-units-halt-production-for-a-week/article65598166.ece).

### 3. The Gangapuram-Chithode Mega Powerloom Cluster
* To modernize the industry, the central and state governments established the **Erode Mega Powerloom Cluster** at Gangapuram (located immediately adjacent to Chithode on NH-544).
* This mega cluster features **Texvalley**, a 1-million-square-foot integrated textile market. According to industrial cluster assessments, the initiative supports an ecosystem of over **18,000 direct workers** and **35,000 indirect workers** in the immediate Chithode/Gangapuram vicinity (see [Grokipedia: Texvalley Profile](https://grokipedia.com/page/Texvalley)).
