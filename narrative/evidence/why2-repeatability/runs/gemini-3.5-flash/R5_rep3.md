Q: What is the population of Erode? Give citation.
elapsed:27s

---

The population of Erode varies depending on whether you refer to the city, the metropolitan (urban) area, or the district. Since the official 2021 Census of India was postponed, the figures for 2026 are projected estimates:

*   **Erode City (Municipal Corporation):** 
    *   **2011 Census:** 157,101
    *   **2026 Estimate:** 234,000
    *   *Citation:* [Census 2011 India - Erode City Population](https://www.census2011.co.in/census/city/476-erode.html)
*   **Erode Metropolitan / Urban Agglomeration:** 
    *   **2011 Census:** 521,891
    *   **2026 Estimate:** 783,000
    *   *Citation:* [Census 2011 India - Erode Metropolitan Urban Region](https://www.census2011.co.in/census/metropolitan/441-erode.html)
*   **Erode District:** 
    *   **2011 Census:** 2,251,744
    *   **2026 Estimate:** ~2,412,519 to 2,550,000
    *   *Citation:* [Census of India - Erode District Population 2026](https://censusofindia.net/tamil-nadu/erode/610)
