Q: What is the population of Erode? Give citation.

According to the official 2011 Census of India, the population of Erode varies depending on whether you refer to the city proper, the metropolitan area, or the entire district:

* **Erode City (Municipal Corporation):** 157,101 (78,222 males and 78,879 females)
* **Erode Urban Agglomeration (Metropolitan Area):** 521,891 (261,630 males and 260,261 females)
* **Erode District:** 2,251,744 (1,129,868 males and 1,121,876 females)
