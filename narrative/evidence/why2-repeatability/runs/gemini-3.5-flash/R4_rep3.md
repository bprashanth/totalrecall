Q: Garbage complaints in Bellandur ward, Bengaluru: from 2019 to 2022 did they go up or come down? Citations.
elapsed:194s

---

Based on official Bruhat Bengaluru Mahanagara Palike (BBMP) citizen grievance data and local reports, garbage complaints in Bellandur ward (Ward 150) **went up** between 2019 and 2022.

### 1. The Data Trend (2020–2022)
Official ward-level data from the BBMP Sahaaya 2.0 (Namma Bengaluru) portal shows a steady year-over-year increase in registered **"Solid Waste (Garbage) Related"** complaints in Bellandur:
* **2020:** 347 complaints *(from the launch of Sahaaya 2.0 on Feb 8, 2020)*
* **2021:** 375 complaints
* **2022:** 431 complaints *(a 24.2% increase from 2020)*

*(Note: This upward trend continued sharply in subsequent years, reaching 587 in 2023 and 1,037 in 2024).*

### 2. The 2019 Context
In 2019, the BBMP was using the original Sahaaya 1.0 app, which was highly criticized for technical glitches and was phased out in early 2020 with over 30,000 unresolved complaints city-wide. Because Sahaaya 1.0 frequently closed complaints automatically without resolution, many grievances in 2019 went unregistered. This led to massive citizen protests, such as the *#MahadevapuraDemands* and *Save Bellandur* human chains, where residents escalated issues directly via Twitter, WhatsApp, and physical petitions.
