Q: Between 2019 and 2022, which ward in Bengaluru had the most garbage complaints? What is the count? Citations please.
elapsed:112s

---

Between 2019 and 2022, **Rajarajeshwari Nagar** (Ward 160) had the most garbage complaints in Bengaluru.

### Complaint Counts (2019–2022)

Depending on how garbage-related complaints are defined:

1. **Strict Definition** (Category: `Solid Waste (Garbage) Related`)
   * **Total Count:** **2,642** complaints
   * **Yearly Breakdown:**
     * **2019:** 0 (Sahaaya 2.0 portal launched in Feb 2020; no 2019 data is present)
     * **2020:** 1,085
     * **2021:** 856
     * **2022:** 701

2. **Broad Definition** (Keywords: garbage, waste, SWM, dumping, sweeping across all categories)
   * **Total Count:** **2,661** complaints
   * **Yearly Breakdown:**
     * **2019:** 0
     * **2020:** 1,087
     * **2021:** 866
     * **2022:** 708

### Citations

* **Primary Dataset:** BBMP Sahaaya 2.0 Citizen Grievances Dataset, hosted on GitHub at [Vonter/bbmp-citizen-grievances](https://github.com/Vonter/bbmp-citizen-grievances).
* **Original Portal Source:** Sourced from the [Bengaluru Smart City Limited Website](https://smartoneblr.com/NicApplicationStatus.htm).
