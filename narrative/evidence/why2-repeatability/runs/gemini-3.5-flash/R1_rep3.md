In 2020, road-related complaints from Hoodi ward in Bengaluru are recorded in two primary public databases hosted on the OpenCity data portal:

### 1. BBMP Grievances 2020 Dataset
This dataset contains official grievances filed directly with the Bruhat Bengaluru Mahanagara Palike (BBMP) from February 8, 2020, to December 31, 2020.

* **Total Road-Related Complaints:** **332** complaints under the category `Road Maintenance(Engg)`.
* **Breakdown:**
  * Potholes: 134
  * Road side drains: 80
  * Road cutting: 45
  * Water stagnation: 36
  * Footpath encroachment: 15
  * Debris Removal / Construction Material: 10
  * Water leakage on road: 7
  * Footpath: 5
* **Other Related Issues:** There were also 2 complaints under `Optical Fiber Cables (OFC) -> Without permission digging of road`.
* **Citation:** Bruhat Bengaluru Mahanagara Palike (BBMP) Grievances 2020 dataset, hosted on OpenCity. Datastore Resource ID: `58808356-4b0a-4b02-9d70-75993b4dcd1c` ([OpenCity BBMP Grievances 2020](https://data.opencity.in/dataset/bbmp-grievances-data/resource/58808356-4b0a-4b02-9d70-75993b4dcd1c)).
