Q: In 2020, how many road related complaints came from Hoodi ward in Bengaluru? Citation also.
elapsed:553s

---

In 2020, there were **332** road-related complaints registered under the official **`Road Maintenance(Engg)`** category for the Hoodi ward (Ward 54) in Bengaluru. If including unauthorized road digging from other categories, the total is **334**.

### Breakdown of Road-Related Complaints (2020)
* **Potholes:** 134
* **Road side drains:** 80
* **Road cutting:** 45
* **Water stagnation:** 36
* **Footpath encroachment:** 15
* **Debris Removal / Construction Material:** 10
* **Water leakage on road:** 7
* **Footpath:** 5
* **Without permission digging of road (OFC category):** 2

### Citation
The data is sourced from the **BBMP Grievances 2020** dataset compiled from the Bruhat Bengaluru Mahanagara Palike (BBMP) Sahaaya portal, hosted on the **Open City** data platform.
