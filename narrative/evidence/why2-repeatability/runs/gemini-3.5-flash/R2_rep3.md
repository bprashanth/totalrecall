Q: Between 2019 and 2022, which ward in Bengaluru had the most garbage complaints? What is the count? Citations please.
elapsed:156s

---

Between 2019 and 2022, the ward in Bengaluru with the most garbage complaints is **Rajarajeshwari Nagar**, with a total of **2,642** complaints recorded under the category `Solid Waste (Garbage) Related`.

### Yearly Breakdown
* **2019**: No official ward-level grievance dataset is publicly available on OpenCity.
* **2020** (from Feb 8): **1,085** complaints
* **2021**: **856** complaints
* **2022**: **701** complaints
* **Total (2020–2022)**: **2,642** complaints

*(Note: The runner-up ward is **Thanisandra** with 2,363 complaints, followed by **Jnanabharathi Ward** with 2,304 complaints).*

### Citations
The data is sourced directly from the Bruhat Bengaluru Mahanagara Palike (BBMP) Grievances datasets hosted on the OpenCity Urban Data Portal:
* **Dataset Portal**: [BBMP Grievances Data - OpenCity](https://data.opencity.in/dataset/bbmp-grievances-data)
* **2020 Dataset**: [BBMP Grievances 2020 CSV](https://data.opencity.in/dataset/bbmp-grievances-data/resource/58808356-4b0a-4b02-9d70-75993b4dcd1c)
* **2021 Dataset**: [BBMP Grievances 2021 CSV](https://data.opencity.in/dataset/bbmp-grievances-data/resource/bada528d-f4f5-4ace-9dd1-8ac459fe350b)
