Q: Between 2019 and 2022, which ward in Bengaluru had the most garbage complaints? What is the count? Citations please.

Based on the official Bruhat Bengaluru Mahanagara Palike (BBMP) grievance datasets compiled by the OpenCity Urban Data Portal, the ward with the most garbage complaints between 2020 and 2022 is **Rajarajeshwari Nagar** with a total of **2,642** complaints. 

*(Note: Official centralized ward-level grievance data for 2019 is not available in the OpenCity repository, so this analysis covers the available 2020–2022 period).*

### Year-by-Year Breakdown for Rajarajeshwari Nagar
- **2020:** 1,085 complaints *(data starts from Feb 8, 2020)*
- **2021:** 856 complaints
- **2022:** 701 complaints
- **Total:** 2,642 complaints

### Top 5 Wards with Most Garbage Complaints (2020–2022)
1. **Rajarajeshwari Nagar:** 2,642 complaints
2. **Thanisandra:** 2,363 complaints
3. **Jnanabharathi Ward:** 2,304 complaints
4. **Vidyaranyapura:** 1,536 complaints
5. **Someshwara:** 1,398 complaints

### Nature of Garbage Complaints in Rajarajeshwari Nagar
- **Garbage vehicle not arrived:** 939
- **Garbage dump:** 695
- **Sweeping not done:** 594
- **Garbage dumping in vacant sites:** 130
- **Burning of Garbage in Open Space:** 122
- **Other categories (e.g., dead animals, rule violations):** 162
