Q: What was India's unemployment rate in 2021? Give the number with citation.
elapsed:42s

---

In India, the unemployment rate for **2021** varies depending on the methodology and agency measuring it. The primary figures are detailed below:

### 1. Official National Estimates (Government of India)
According to the **Periodic Labour Force Survey (PLFS)** conducted by the National Sample Survey Office (NSSO) under the Ministry of Statistics and Programme Implementation (MoSPI):
*   **Usual Status (ps+ss)** (long-term/365-day perspective, age 15+): **4.2%** for the calendar year (January–December 2021).
*   **Current Weekly Status (CWS)** (short-term/7-day perspective, age 15+): **7.3%** for the calendar year (January–December 2021).
*   **Survey Period (July 2020 – June 2021)**: **4.2%** (usual status, all ages).

> **Citation:** National Sample Survey Office (NSSO), Ministry of Statistics and Programme Implementation (MoSPI), Government of India. *Press Note on Annual Report, Periodic Labour Force Survey (PLFS) 2021–22* (Released February 2023).

---

### 2. International Estimate (World Bank / ILO)
According to the **World Bank**, which publishes modeled estimates calculated by the **International Labour Organization (ILO)**:
*   The total unemployment rate was **6.4%** (or **6.38%** in precise database revisions) of the total labor force.

> **Citation:** World Bank Open Data. *Unemployment, total (% of total labor force) (modeled ILO estimate) - India* (Series Code: `SL.UEM.TOTL.ZS`, 2021 Data).

---

### 3. Private/Independent Estimate (CMIE)
According to the **Centre for Monitoring Indian Economy (CMIE)**, which uses a stricter definition of active employment:
*   The monthly unemployment rate fluctuated significantly due to the COVID-19 pandemic, ranging from a low of **6.50%** (March 2021) to a high of **11.84%** (May 2021), averaging around **7.9%** for the year.

> **Citation:** Centre for Monitoring Indian Economy (CMIE). *Consumer Pyramids Household Survey (CPHS) - Unemployment in India Database* (2021).
