Q: What was India's unemployment rate in 2021? Give the number with citation.

Depending on the methodology and data source, India's unemployment rate in 2021 is reported as follows:

### 1. World Bank (Modeled ILO Estimate)
* **Unemployment Rate**: **6.38%**
* **Scope**: Total labor force (annual percentage).
* **Citation**: World Bank, *Unemployment, total (% of total labor force) (modeled ILO estimate) - India*. 
* **Source Link**: [World Bank Data](https://data.worldbank.org/indicator/SL.UEM.TOTL.ZS?locations=IN)

### 2. Government of India — Periodic Labour Force Survey (PLFS)
* **Unemployment Rate**: **4.2%**
* **Scope**: Persons aged 15 years and above under usual status (for the survey year July 2020 – June 2021).
* **Citation**: National Statistical Office (NSO), Ministry of Statistics and Programme Implementation (MoSPI), Government of India, *Periodic Labour Force Survey (PLFS) Annual Report (2020-21)*.
* **Source Link**: [Ministry of Labour & Employment (MoSPI Report)](https://dge.gov.in/dge/sites/default/files/2023-09/175_E.pdf)
