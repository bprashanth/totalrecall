Q: What was India's unemployment rate in 2021? Give the number with citation.
elapsed:44s

---

India's unemployment rate in 2021 varies depending on the database, reporting period (calendar year vs. fiscal year), and methodology used. Below are the primary figures with official citations:

### 1. Official Government of India Data (PLFS / MoSPI)
The National Statistical Office (NSO) under the Ministry of Statistics and Programme Implementation (MoSPI) conducts the **Periodic Labour Force Survey (PLFS)**. It reports on a July-to-June cycle based on "Usual Status" (persons aged 15 and above):
* **4.2%** for the period **July 2020 – June 2021** (Rural: 3.3%, Urban: 6.7%)
* **4.1%** for the period **July 2021 – June 2022** (Rural: 3.2%, Urban: 6.3%)

**Citation:** 
* *Ministry of Statistics and Programme Implementation, Government of India.* [Annual Report: Periodic Labour Force Survey (PLFS) 2021-22](https://mospi.gov.in/sites/default/files/publication_reports/AnnualReportPLFS2021-22F1.pdf).
* *Directorate General of Employment, Ministry of Labour & Employment.* [Employment Indicators in India since 2017-18 to 2021-22](https://dge.gov.in/dge/sites/default/files/2023-07/Employment_Indicators_in_India_since_2017-18_to_2021-22.pdf).

---

### 2. World Bank & International Labour Organization (ILO)
The World Bank's World Development Indicators database provides a calendar-year annual modeled estimate from the ILO:
* **5.98%** for the calendar year **2021** (representing a decline from 8.00% in 2020 during the peak of COVID-19 disruptions).

**Citation:**
* *International Labour Organization, ILOSTAT database / World Bank.* [Unemployment, total (% of total labor force) (modeled ILO estimate) - India](https://databank.worldbank.org/metadataglossary/world-development-indicators/series/SL.UEM.TOTL.ZS).

---

### 3. Centre for Monitoring Indian Economy (CMIE) & Other Databases
Independent or regional statistical aggregators compiling monthly and annual household survey data for the calendar year 2021 report:
* **6.38%** for the calendar year **2021** (a decline from 7.86% in 2020).

**Citation:**
* *CMIE / YCharts.* [India Unemployment Rate (Yearly) Historical Trends](https://ycharts.com/indicators/india_unemployment_rate_annual).
