Q: Garbage complaints in Bellandur ward, Bengaluru: from 2019 to 2022 did they go up or come down? Citations.
elapsed:311s

---

Whether garbage complaints in the Bellandur (Bellanduru) ward of Bengaluru went up or down between 2019 and 2022 depends on which grievance tracking platform you look at. 

Analyzing the raw, ward-level open data from two major sources reveals a fascinating shift in how citizens reported municipal waste issues during this period.

---

### 1. Official BBMP Sahaaya Grievances (Official Portal) – **WENT UP**
On the official Bruhat Bengaluru Mahanagara Palike (BBMP) Sahaaya grievance portal, garbage-related complaints **increased** steadily from 2020 to 2022 *(Note: Raw 2019 BBMP Sahaaya data is not publicly available on OpenCity as the public dataset starts in February 2020)*.

*   **2020:** **347** garbage complaints (out of 1,540 total ward complaints)
*   **2021:** **375** garbage complaints (out of 1,480 total ward complaints)
*   **2022:** **431** garbage complaints (out of 2,207 total ward complaints)

This represents an overall **24.2% increase** in garbage grievances logged via official municipal channels between 2020 and 2022.
*   **Key Subcategory Trends:** In 2020 and 2022, "Garbage vehicle not arrived" and "Garbage dump" were the leading complaints. However, 2021 saw a massive spike in **"Burning of Garbage in Open Space"** with 189 registered complaints (comprising over 50% of the ward's entire solid waste grievances that year).

---

### 2. Janaagraha’s "I Change My City" (iCMyC) Portal (NGO Portal) – **WENT DOWN**
On Janaagraha's popular citizen engagement platform, complaints **dropped** dramatically over the same span:

*   **2019:** **55** "Garbage and Unsanitary Practices" complaints (out of 444 total ward complaints)
*   **2020:** **25** garbage complaints (out of 155 total ward complaints)
*   **2021:** **7** garbage complaints (out of 80 total ward complaints)
*   **2022:** **3** garbage complaints (out of 48 total ward complaints)
*   *Yellow Spot complaints (open urination/dumping spots)* also dropped: 17 (2020) → 13 (2021) → 6 (2022).

---

### Why Did the Trends Diverge?
The steep decline in complaints on `I Change My City` does not mean that garbage issues disappeared, but rather reflects a change in citizen reporting habits. Between 2019 and 2022, the BBMP heavily promoted its official `Sahaaya` portal and `FixMyStreet` app, urging residents to report civic issues directly to municipal departments rather than third-party platforms. As citizens migrated to official channels, third-party logs dried up while official municipal logs recorded a steady, real-world increase in waste complaints.
