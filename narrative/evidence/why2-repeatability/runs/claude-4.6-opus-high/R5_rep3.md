The population of **Erode** depends on whether you mean the core city or the broader metropolitan/urban agglomeration:

- **Erode city (municipal area):** Estimated **~234,000** in 2026 (up from 157,101 in Census 2011).
- **Erode metropolitan / urban agglomeration:** Estimated **~721,000 to 808,000** in 2026, depending on the source (up from 521,891 in Census 2011).

Note: India's last official census was conducted in 2011; the 2021 census was postponed due to COVID. All 2026 figures are projections based on historical growth trends.
