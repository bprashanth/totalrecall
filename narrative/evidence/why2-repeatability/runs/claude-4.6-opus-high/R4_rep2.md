Q: Garbage complaints in Bellandur ward, Bengaluru: from 2019 to 2022 did they go up or come down? Citations.
elapsed:182s

---

## Answer: Garbage complaints in Bellandur ward **went up** from 2020 to 2022.

Here are the numbers from BBMP's official Sahaaya grievance portal data (the dataset starts from Feb 2020, so 2019 data is not available in this system):

| Year | Solid Waste Complaints | Total Complaints (all categories) |
|------|----------------------|-----------------------------------|
| 2020 | **347** | 1,540 |
| 2021 | **375** (+8%) | 1,480 |
| 2022 | **431** (+15%) | 2,207 |

**From 2020 to 2022, solid waste complaints rose by ~24%, from 347 to 431.** Total complaints across all categories also jumped 43% (1,540 to 2,207).

Notable shifts within solid waste sub-categories:
- **Garbage burning** complaints spiked massively in 2021 (189 complaints, up from 23 in 2020), then fell back to 43 in 2022.
- **Garbage vehicle not arrived** complaints rose from 128 (2020) to 158 (2022).
- **Garbage dump** complaints grew from 90 (2020) to 126 (2022).
