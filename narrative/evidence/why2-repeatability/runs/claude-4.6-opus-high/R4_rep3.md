Q: Garbage complaints in Bellandur ward, Bengaluru: from 2019 to 2022 did they go up or come down? Citations.
elapsed:189s

---

## Answer: Garbage complaints in Bellandur went **up**

Based on official BBMP grievance data from the Sahaaya 2.0 portal (published via OpenCity), garbage complaints in Bellandur ward (Ward 150) rose steadily from 2020 to 2022:

| Year | Garbage Complaints (Solid Waste category) | Change |
|------|------------------------------------------|--------|
| 2020 | 347 (from 8 Feb 2020 onward) | — |
| 2021 | 375 | +8.1% |
| 2022 | 431 | +14.9% |

**Overall increase: ~24% from 2020 to 2022.**

Note: The 2020 dataset starts from 8 February 2020 (not 1 January), so the true full-year figure for 2020 would likely be slightly higher, making the percentage increase somewhat smaller -- but the upward trend is clear regardless. Also, the Sahaaya 2.0 portal launched in Feb 2020, so 2019 data is not available in this dataset.

### Sub-category shifts

The nature of complaints also shifted notably:
- **"Garbage vehicle not arrived"** was consistently the largest complaint type (128 in 2020, 158 in 2022), except in 2021 when it dipped to 57 -- likely due to COVID lockdowns reducing waste pickup expectations.
- **"Burning of Garbage in Open Space"** spiked dramatically in 2021 (189 complaints, up from 23 in 2020), then fell back to 43 in 2022. The 2021 spike aligns with news reports of rampant garbage burning in Kasavanahalli/Bellandur during the pandemic period.
- **"Garbage dump"** complaints rose steadily: 90 -> 69 -> 126.

### Context

Bellandur ward (pop. ~80,000 per 2011 Census, likely much higher by 2022) is one of Bengaluru's largest wards at 26.4 sq km. Rapid population growth in the IT corridor consistently outpaced waste infrastructure. By 2025, Bellandur ranked 9th among all 198 BBMP wards with 2,257 complaints in just 5.5 months.
