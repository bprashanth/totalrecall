Q: In 2020, how many road related complaints came from Hoodi ward in Bengaluru? Citation also.
elapsed:57s

---

Here are the results:

**In 2020, there were 332 road-related complaints from Hoodi ward in Bengaluru.**

These were filed under the category **"Road Maintenance(Engg)"** and broke down as follows:

| Sub Category | Count |
|---|---|
| Potholes | 134 |
| Road side drains | 80 |
| Road cutting | 45 |
| Water stagnation | 36 |
| Footpath encroachment | 15 |
| Debris Removal / Construction Material | 10 |
| Water leakage on road | 7 |
| Footpath | 5 |

**Citation:** BBMP Grievances 2020 dataset, sourced from BBMP's Sahaaya 2.0 portal, available at [Open City Data Portal](https://data.opencity.in/dataset/bbmp-grievances-data/resource/58808356-4b0a-4b02-9d70-75993b4dcd1c). Original data credit: Vivek Mathew / Vaidya R. License: Public Domain.
