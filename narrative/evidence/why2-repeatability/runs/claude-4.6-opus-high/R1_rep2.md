Q: In 2020, how many road related complaints came from Hoodi ward in Bengaluru? Citation also.
elapsed:64s

---

**In 2020, there were 332 road-related complaints from Hoodi ward in Bengaluru.**

These complaints fall under two BBMP categories — "Road Infrastructure" and "Road Maintenance(Engg)" — with the following sub-category breakdown:

| Sub-category | Count |
|---|---|
| Potholes | 134 |
| Road side drains | 80 |
| Road cutting | 45 |
| Water stagnation | 36 |
| Footpath encroachment | 15 |
| Debris Removal / Construction Material | 10 |
| Water leakage on road | 7 |
| Footpath | 5 |

**Citation:**

BBMP Grievances Data — "BBMP Grievances 2020" (CSV), sourced from the BBMP Sahaaya 2.0 portal, published on Open City Data Portal.
