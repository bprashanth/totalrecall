Here are the population figures for **Erode**, a city in Tamil Nadu, India:

- **Erode city proper (2026 estimate):** ~234,000
- **Erode metropolitan/urban agglomeration (2026 estimate):** ~721,000 to ~808,000 (varies by source)
- **Erode city (2011 Census, official):** 157,101
- **Erode urban agglomeration (2011 Census, official):** 521,891

Note: No official census has been conducted since 2011 (the 2021 census was postponed due to COVID), so 2026 figures are projections based on past growth rates.
