## Answer

In 2020, **332 road-related complaints** (categorized as "Road Maintenance(Engg)") were filed from **Hoodi ward** in Bengaluru, according to BBMP grievance data.

**Citation:**

BBMP Grievances 2020 dataset, hosted on Open City Data Portal (data.opencity.in):
- Dataset: "BBMP Grievances Data"
- Resource: "BBMP Grievances 2020"
- URL: https://data.opencity.in/dataset/bbmp-grievances-data/resource/58808356-4b0a-4b02-9d70-75993b4dcd1c
- Source: BBMP (Bruhat Bengaluru Mahanagara Palike)
- Filter: Ward Name = "Hoodi", Category = "Road Maintenance(Engg)"
- Total matching records: **332**
