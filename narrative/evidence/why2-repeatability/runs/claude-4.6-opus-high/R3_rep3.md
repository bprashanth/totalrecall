Q: What was India's unemployment rate in 2021? Give the number with citation.
elapsed:31s

---

India's unemployment rate in 2021 was **6.38%** (of total labor force), based on the ILO modelled estimate published by the World Bank.

**Source:** World Bank, World Development Indicators — "Unemployment, total (% of total labor force) (modeled ILO estimate)" for India, 2021. Available at: https://databank.worldbank.org (series SL.UEM.TOTL.ZS).
