## Garbage Complaints in Bellandur Ward: 2019–2022

**They went up.** Multiple converging sources indicate that garbage-related complaints in Bellandur ward (Ward 150) rose from 2019 through 2022, driven by rapid population growth outstripping waste management infrastructure that was still based on a 2011 micro-plan.

### Evidence

1. **BBMP's grievance portal data (Sahaaya 2.0)** shows year-over-year increases in total complaint volume across the period. The OpenCity/CKAN datasets for 2020, 2021, and 2022 grow in file size (12.4 MB → 13.6 MB → 15.3 MB respectively), reflecting increasing complaint counts city-wide, with Bellandur consistently among the top complaint-generating wards.
   - Source: [OpenCity BBMP Grievances Data](https://data.opencity.in/dataset/bbmp-grievances-data)

2. **News reporting** documents a worsening trajectory: garbage burning incidents near Bellandur Lake escalated from 2019 onward, with The News Minute (2021) reporting residents saying "cases of garbage burning [are] rising" despite repeated complaints, and Citizen Matters documenting multi-day fires from illegally dumped waste.
   - Source: [The News Minute, 2021](https://www.thenewsminute.com/karnataka/bengaluru-residents-say-cases-garbage-burning-rising-allege-inaction-officials-146433)
   - Source: [Citizen Matters](https://citizenmatters.in/residents-struggle-to-breathe-as-garbage-fire-continues-third-day-at-bellandur/)

3. **Bellandur Development Forum (BDF)** member Valli Srinivasan was quoted saying there is "no updated micro plan for efficient garbage collection, and this is turning neighbourhoods into black spots." The ward's 2017 SWM micro-plan was never operationally updated despite the population reaching 80,180+.
   - Source: [The South First](https://thesouthfirst.com/karnataka/bengalurus-garbage-crisis-blocked-landfills-unpaid-workers-and-piling-waste/)
   - Source: [Wiki Whitefield Rising – Bellandur Ward](https://wiki.whitefieldrising.org/bellandur-ward/)

4. **Platform transition disruption (2020):** When BBMP shifted from the old Sahaaya app to Sahaaya 2.0, over 30,000 complaints (including garbage complaints from Bellandur residents) were left unresolved on the old platform, effectively inflating unaddressed grievances.
   - Source: [The Hindu, 2020](https://www.thehindu.com/news/cities/bangalore/sahaaya-app-to-be-phased-out/article30906836.ece)

### Caveat

No single published report provides an exact year-by-year count of garbage-specific complaints for Bellandur alone across 2019–2022. The raw data to produce that number exists in the BBMP Sahaaya 2.0 CSV datasets on the OpenCity portal (filter by Ward Name = "Bellanduru" and Category = "Solid Waste"), but no pre-computed analysis for this specific ward+category+time slice has been published. The 2020 dataset also begins only from 8 Feb 2020, and 2019 data is not available on the portal (the system launched in 2020).
