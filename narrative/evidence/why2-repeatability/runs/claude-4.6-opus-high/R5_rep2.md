The population of **Erode**, Tamil Nadu, India varies depending on the boundary considered:

- **Erode city proper**: ~**234,000** (2026 estimate, projected from the 2011 Census figure of 157,101)
- **Erode metropolitan/urban agglomeration**: ~**783,000–808,182** (2026 estimate, projected from the 2011 Census figure of 521,891)

These are projections based on the 2011 Census growth rate, as the 2021 Census was postponed due to COVID.

- Census2011.co.in – [Erode City Population 2026](https://www.census2011.co.in/census/city/476-erode.html)
- World Population Review – [Erode Population 2026](https://worldpopulationreview.com/cities/india/erode)
