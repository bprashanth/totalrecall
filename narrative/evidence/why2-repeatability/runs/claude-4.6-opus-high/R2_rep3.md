## Answer

Based on my analysis of the **BBMP Sahaaya 2.0 grievance data** (the official citizen complaints portal), **Rajarajeshwari Nagar** ward had the most garbage complaints between 2020 and 2022, with a total of **2,642 complaints** in the "Solid Waste (Garbage) Related" category.

**Important caveat about 2019:** The BBMP Sahaaya 2.0 portal only launched on **February 8, 2020**, so there is no publicly available ward-level grievance data for 2019 in this system. The analysis covers Feb 2020 through Dec 2022.

### Top 5 wards by garbage complaints (Feb 2020 - Dec 2022):

| Rank | Ward | Garbage Complaints |
|------|------|-------------------|
| 1 | Rajarajeshwari Nagar | 2,642 |
| 2 | Thanisandra | 2,363 |
| 3 | Jnanabharathi Ward | 2,304 |
| 4 | Vidyaranyapura | 1,536 |
| 5 | Someshwara | 1,398 |
