Q: What was India's unemployment rate in 2021? Give the number with citation.
elapsed:31s

---

India's unemployment rate in 2021 was **6.38%** (of total labor force).

**Source:** World Bank, "Unemployment, total (% of total labor force) (modeled ILO estimate) - India," based on the ILO Modelled Estimates database (ILOSTAT), International Labour Organization.  
URL: https://data.worldbank.org/indicator/SL.UEM.TOTL.ZS?locations=IN
