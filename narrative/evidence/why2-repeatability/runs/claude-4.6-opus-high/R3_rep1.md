Q: What was India's unemployment rate in 2021? Give the number with citation.
elapsed:26s

---

India's unemployment rate in 2021 was approximately **7.8%** (annual average).

**Source:** GlobalData, "The Unemployment rate of India (2021–2029, %)" — [globaldata.com](https://www.globaldata.com/data-insights/macroeconomic/the-unemployment-rate-of-india-220172/)

For additional context, the quarterly urban unemployment rates from India's official Periodic Labour Force Survey (PLFS) were:
- Jan–Mar 2021: 9.3%
- Apr–Jun 2021: 12.7%
- Jul–Sep 2021: 9.8%
- Oct–Dec 2021: 8.8%
