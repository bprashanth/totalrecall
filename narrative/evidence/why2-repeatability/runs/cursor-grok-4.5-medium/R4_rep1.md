Q: Garbage complaints in Bellandur ward, Bengaluru: from 2019 to 2022 did they go up or come down? Citations.
elapsed:102s

---

**They went up** — at least on the ward-level complaint record that exists for Bellandur.

### Bellandur ward: BBMP “Solid Waste (Garbage) Related” grievances
From BBMP Sahaaya grievance data published via OpenCity ([BBMP Grievances Data](https://data.opencity.in/dataset/bbmp-grievances-data)):

| Year | Garbage / SWM complaints in Bellandur |
|------|----------------------------------------|
| 2020 | **347** (from 8 Feb 2020; Sahaaya 2.0 start) |
| 2021 | **375** |
| 2022 | **431** |

That is about a **24% rise** from 2020 to 2022. Main sub-types in 2022 were delayed collection vehicles, garbage dumps, and open burning.

**2019 caveat:** This public series does not include 2019 (it begins when Sahaaya 2.0 launched in Feb 2020), so there is no clean Bellandur ward complaint count for a 2019→2022 comparison in that dataset.

### Related context (not the same as ward complaints)
Citywide garbage **black spots** fell from **1,920 (2019)** to **1,356 (2022)** per BSWML figures reported by [The Times of India](https://timesofindia.indiatimes.com/city/bengaluru/bengalurus-black-spots-down-60-in-6-years-but-mahadevapura-faces-hurdles/articleshow/120240450.cms). That article also flags **Mahadevapura** (Bellandur’s zone) as a lagging outlier with persistent dumping/collection stress — consistent with Bellandur’s rising formal complaints even as the citywide black-spot tally fell.

**Bottom line:** For Bellandur ward garbage complaints on BBMP’s system, **2020→2022 went up**. A strict 2019 baseline for the same metric isn’t in the public OpenCity release.
