Q: What is the population of Erode? Give citation.
elapsed:32s

---

**Erode Municipal Corporation: 157,101** (Census of India 2011).

That is the official city figure for Erode (M.Corp): 78,222 males and 78,879 females.

**Citation:** Directorate of Census Operations, Tamil Nadu. *District Census Handbook: Erode* (Census of India 2011, Series-34, Part XII-B), Primary Census Abstract — Erode (M.Corp), town code 803542.  
Source: [Erode District Census Handbook (PDF)](https://cdn.s3waas.gov.in/s3bca82e41ee7b0833588399b1fcd177c7/uploads/2018/06/2018062114.pdf) (also listed on [erode.nic.in](https://erode.nic.in/documents/census/)).

Related 2011 figures from the same census:
- **Erode Urban Agglomeration:** 521,891  
- **Erode district:** 2,251,744  

No newer all-India census has been fully published since 2011.
