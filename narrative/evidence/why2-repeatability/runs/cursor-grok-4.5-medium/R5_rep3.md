Q: What is the population of Erode? Give citation.
elapsed:22s

---

As of the **2011 Census of India**, Erode city’s population was **157,101** (78,222 males and 78,879 females).

The broader **Erode urban agglomeration** had **521,891** people in the same census.

**Citation:** Office of the Registrar General & Census Commissioner, India — Census 2011 (Erode Municipal Corporation / Town code 803542). Archived at: https://web.archive.org/web/20150924144408/www.censusindia.gov.in/pca/SearchDetails.aspx?Id=686226

No full official census has been published since 2011 (the 2021 census was delayed), so later figures are estimates only.
