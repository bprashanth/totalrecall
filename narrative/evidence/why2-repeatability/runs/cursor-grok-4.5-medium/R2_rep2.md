Q: Between 2019 and 2022, which ward in Bengaluru had the most garbage complaints? What is the count? Citations please.
elapsed:113s

---

**Rajarajeshwari Nagar**, with **2,642** solid-waste (garbage) complaints.

That is from BBMP’s Sahaaya 2.0 grievance records for **8 Feb 2020–31 Dec 2022**. There is **no 2019 data** in this system (the portal starts 8 Feb 2020).

| Rank | Ward | Count (2020–2022) |
|------|------|-------------------|
| 1 | Rajarajeshwari Nagar | **2,642** |
| 2 | Thanisandra | 2,363 |
| 3 | Jnanabharathi Ward | 2,304 |

Yearly split for Rajarajeshwari Nagar: 1,085 (2020), 856 (2021), 701 (2022). Category used: `Solid Waste (Garbage) Related` (dumps, missed collection, sweeping, etc.).

**Citations**
1. [Vonter/bbmp-citizen-grievances](https://github.com/Vonter/bbmp-citizen-grievances) — consolidated Sahaaya 2.0 grievances (source: Bengaluru Smart City Limited / BBMP)
2. [OpenCity – BBMP Grievances Data](https://data.opencity.in/dataset/bbmp-grievances-data) — same BBMP ward-level grievances by year (2020+)
