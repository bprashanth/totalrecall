Q: What is the population of Erode? Give citation.
elapsed:24s

---

According to the **2011 Census of India**, Erode’s population is:

- **City (Municipal Corporation):** **157,101**
- **Urban agglomeration / metro:** about **521,776–521,891**

**Citation:** Census of India 2011 (via [censusindia.gov.in PCA record for Erode M.Corp](https://web.archive.org/web/20150924144408/www.censusindia.gov.in/pca/SearchDetails.aspx?Id=686226); also summarized on [census2011.co.in](https://www.census2011.co.in/census/city/476-erode.html)).

That remains the last completed official census; India’s 2021 census was postponed, so later figures are estimates only.
