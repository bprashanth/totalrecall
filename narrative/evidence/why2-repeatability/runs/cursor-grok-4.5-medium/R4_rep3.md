Q: Garbage complaints in Bellandur ward, Bengaluru: from 2019 to 2022 did they go up or come down? Citations.
elapsed:138s

---

**They went up** on BBMP’s official grievance channel (the main municipal complaints system). OpenCity has no BBMP ward file for 2019; the series starts in 2020.

### BBMP grievances — Bellandur, “Solid Waste (Garbage) Related”
| Year | Complaints |
|------|------------|
| 2020 | 347 |
| 2021 | 375 |
| 2022 | 431 |

That’s about a **24% rise** from 2020 to 2022. Same pattern on a like-for-like Mar–Dec window (301 → 337 → 386).  
Source: [OpenCity BBMP Grievances Data](https://data.opencity.in/dataset/bbmp-grievances-data) (BBMP; 2020 file starts 8 Feb 2020).

### Caveat for 2019
Janaagraha’s **I Change My City** log (2019–2022) shows Bellandur “Garbage and Unsanitary Practices” falling (55 → 25 → 7 → 3), but **total** ICMC complaints from the ward also collapsed (444 → 48), so that drop mostly tracks people leaving the portal, not necessarily fewer garbage problems.  
Source: [OpenCity I Change My City Data](https://data.opencity.in/dataset/i-change-my-city-data).

**Bottom line:** On official BBMP complaint counts through 2022, Bellandur garbage grievances **rose**; there is no published BBMP 2019 ward series to start from.
