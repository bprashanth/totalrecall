Q: What was India's unemployment rate in 2021? Give the number with citation.
elapsed:22s

---

**4.2%** (usual status, ages 15+) for calendar year 2021.

Source: India’s Periodic Labour Force Survey (PLFS), Ministry of Statistics and Programme Implementation — [Press note on PLFS Annual Report](https://mospi.gov.in/sites/default/files/press_release/Press_note_AR_PLFS_2021_22_24022023.pdf) (Statement 4: January–December 2021).

Under Current Weekly Status (CWS) for the same period, the rate was **7.3%**.
