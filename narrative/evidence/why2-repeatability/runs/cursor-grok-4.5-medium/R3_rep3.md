Q: What was India's unemployment rate in 2021? Give the number with citation.
elapsed:26s

---

**4.2%** (usual status, ages 15+) for calendar year January–December 2021.

Source: [Ministry of Statistics and Programme Implementation (MoSPI), Periodic Labour Force Survey (PLFS) press note](https://mospi.gov.in/sites/default/files/press_release/Press_note_AR_PLFS_2021_22_24022023.pdf) — Statement 4, rural+urban persons.

Under Current Weekly Status (CWS) for the same period, the rate was **7.3%**.
