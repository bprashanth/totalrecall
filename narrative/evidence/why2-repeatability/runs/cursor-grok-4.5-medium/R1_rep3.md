Q: In 2020, how many road related complaints came from Hoodi ward in Bengaluru? Citation also.
elapsed:65s

---

**332** road-related complaints came from Hoodi ward in Bengaluru in 2020.

That count is from BBMP Sahaaya grievances under the **Road Maintenance (Engg)** category for ward name **Hoodi** (subcategories include potholes, roadside drains, road cutting, etc.). The published 2020 file covers grievances from **8 Feb 2020** onward, not the full calendar year from 1 Jan.

**Citation:** OpenCity / BBMP — [BBMP Grievances 2020](https://data.opencity.in/dataset/bbmp-grievances-data) (source: BBMP).
