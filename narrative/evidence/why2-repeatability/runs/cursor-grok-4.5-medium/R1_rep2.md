Q: In 2020, how many road related complaints came from Hoodi ward in Bengaluru? Citation also.
elapsed:60s

---

**332** road-related complaints came from Hoodi ward in 2020.

That count is grievances filed under the BBMP category **Road Maintenance(Engg)** for ward **Hoodi** in the BBMP Grievances 2020 dataset (OpenCity; sourced from BBMP). The file covers ward-level grievances from **8 Feb 2020** onward.

**Citation:** [BBMP Grievances 2020 – OpenCity / BBMP](https://data.opencity.in/dataset/bbmp-grievances-data/resource/58808356-4b0a-4b02-9d70-75993b4dcd1c)
