Q: What was India's unemployment rate in 2021? Give the number with citation.
elapsed:24s

---

**4.2%** (usual status, persons aged 15+), for calendar year January–December 2021.

Source: Ministry of Statistics and Programme Implementation (MoSPI), Periodic Labour Force Survey (PLFS) press note on the Annual Report 2021–22 — [Press note (PDF)](https://mospi.gov.in/sites/default/files/press_release/Press_note_AR_PLFS_2021_22_24022023.pdf).
