Q: Garbage complaints in Bellandur ward, Bengaluru: from 2019 to 2022 did they go up or come down? Citations.
elapsed:120s

---

**They went up** — at least on BBMP’s official grievance channel for 2020–2022 (comparable 2019 BBMP portal data isn’t in the public dump).

### BBMP Solid Waste (Garbage) Related — Bellandur ward

| Year | Complaints |
|------|------------|
| 2020 | **347** (from 8 Feb only) |
| 2021 | **375** |
| 2022 | **431** |

That’s about **+24% from 2020 to 2022** (+15% from 2021 to 2022). Including related debris/lake/SWD dump complaints: **385 → 427 → 492**.

Citywide, the same BBMP “Solid Waste (Garbage) Related” category was roughly flat/slightly down (24,022 → 22,828), so Bellandur’s rise stands out.

**Source:** OpenCity / BBMP grievances CSVs ([dataset](https://data.opencity.in/dataset/bbmp-grievances-data); 2020 starts 8 Feb 2020). Counts are from the ward-level files for Bellandur, category `Solid Waste (Garbage) Related`.

### Caveat on 2019

Janaagraha’s **I Change My City** log shows Bellandur garbage/unsanitary complaints falling (57 → 27 → 12 → 4 from 2019–2022), but **citywide iCMyC filing collapsed** over the same period (7,383 → 1,551), so that looks like platform shift away from iCMyC after BBMP’s Sahaaya portal, not a clean signal that problems fell.

**Source:** [I Change My City complaints log 2019–2022](https://data.opencity.in/dataset/i-change-my-city-data)

**Bottom line:** On BBMP’s own grievance data, Bellandur garbage complaints **rose** from 2020 to 2022; there is no solid official BBMP series for full-year 2019 to extend that back.
