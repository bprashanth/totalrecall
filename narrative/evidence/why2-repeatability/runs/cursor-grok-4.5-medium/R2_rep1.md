Q: Between 2019 and 2022, which ward in Bengaluru had the most garbage complaints? What is the count? Citations please.
elapsed:163s

---

**Rajarajeshwari Nagar**, with **2,642** solid-waste (garbage) complaints.

That is the highest ward total for BBMP category `Solid Waste (Garbage) Related` over **2020–2022** (2020: 1,085; 2021: 856; 2022: 701). Next were Thanisandra (2,363) and Jnanabharathi Ward (2,304).

**Caveat on 2019:** The Sahaaya 2.0 grievance portal data used here starts on **8 Feb 2020**, so there are **no 2019 records** in this series. For 2019–2022 as asked, the measurable window is Feb 2020–Dec 2022.

**Sources**
1. [BBMP Grievances Data (OpenCity)](https://data.opencity.in/dataset/bbmp-grievances-data) — ward-level CSVs for 2020, 2021, and 2022 (same totals when filtered to `Solid Waste (Garbage) Related`).
2. [Vonter/bbmp-citizen-grievances](https://github.com/Vonter/bbmp-citizen-grievances) — consolidated Sahaaya 2.0 dump sourced from [Bengaluru Smart City Limited](https://smartoneblr.com/NicApplicationStatus.htm).
