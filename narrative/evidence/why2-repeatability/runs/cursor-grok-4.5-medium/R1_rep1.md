Q: In 2020, how many road related complaints came from Hoodi ward in Bengaluru? Citation also.
elapsed:102s

---

**332** road-related complaints came from Hoodi ward in 2020.

That count is all grievances filed under the BBMP category **Road Maintenance(Engg)** for ward name **Hoodi** (including potholes, roadside drains, road cutting, water stagnation, footpaths, etc.).

**Citation:** [BBMP Grievances Data – BBMP Grievances 2020](https://data.opencity.in/dataset/bbmp-grievances-data) (OpenCity; source: BBMP). The 2020 file covers grievances from **8 Feb 2020** onward.
