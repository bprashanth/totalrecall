Q: Between 2019 and 2022, which ward in Bengaluru had the most garbage complaints? What is the count? Citations please.
elapsed:98s

---

**Rajarajeshwari Nagar**, with **2,642** solid-waste (garbage) complaints.

That is from BBMP Sahaaya grievances filed under the category **“Solid Waste (Garbage) Related”**, summed across the publicly available 2020–2022 ward-level files (OpenCity / BBMP). Year split: 1,085 (2020), 856 (2021), 701 (2022). Next were Thanisandra (2,363) and Jnanabharathi Ward (2,304).

**Caveat on 2019:** These archives start **8 Feb 2020**. There is no ward-level 2019 Sahaaya file in the OpenCity or mirrored BBMP datasets, so the figure covers **Feb 2020–Dec 2022**, not calendar 2019.

**Citations**
1. [BBMP Grievances Data (OpenCity)](https://data.opencity.in/dataset/bbmp-grievances-data) — ward-level CSVs for 2020, 2021, 2022; source credited as BBMP  
2. Same data mirrored in [Vonter/bbmp-citizen-grievances](https://github.com/Vonter/bbmp-citizen-grievances) (from Bengaluru Smart City / Sahaaya)
