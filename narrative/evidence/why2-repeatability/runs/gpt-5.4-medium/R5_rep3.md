Q: What is the population of Erode? Give citation.

If you mean **Erode city in Tamil Nadu, India**, the **2011 Census** lists its population as **157,101**.[^1]

If you mean the **expanded Erode City Municipal Corporation area**, the corporation website reports a **2011 provisional population of 498,121**.[^2]

[^1]: Census of India, Primary Census Abstract for `Erode (M.Corp)`: [web archive link](https://web.archive.org/web/20150924144408/www.censusindia.gov.in/pca/SearchDetails.aspx?Id=686226)  
[^2]: Erode City Municipal Corporation: [official page](https://www.tnurbantree.tn.gov.in/erode/)
