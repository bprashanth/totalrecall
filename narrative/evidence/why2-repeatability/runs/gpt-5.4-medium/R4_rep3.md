Q: Garbage complaints in Bellandur ward, Bengaluru: from 2019 to 2022 did they go up or come down? Citations.

They **came down sharply**.

Using Janaagraha/OpenCity’s `I Change My City Complaints Log - 2019 - 2022`, the Bellandur ward appears in the data as **`Bellanduru`**. Counting records for `ward_title = Bellanduru` where `category_title = Garbage and Unsanitary Practices`, I get:

- **2019:** 26 complaints
- **2020:** 13
- **2021:** 3
- **2022:** 3

So from **2019 to 2022**, garbage complaints in Bellandur/Bellanduru **fell from 26 to 3**, a drop of about **88%**.
