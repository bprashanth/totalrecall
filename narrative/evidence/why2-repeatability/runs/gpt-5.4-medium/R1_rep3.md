Q: In 2020, how many road related complaints came from Hoodi ward in Bengaluru? Citation also.
elapsed:60s

---

In the `BBMP Grievances 2020` dataset, `Hoodi` ward had **332 road-related complaints** in 2020, using the category `Road Maintenance(Engg)`.

Citation: the source dataset is OpenCity’s [`BBMP Grievances Data`](https://data.opencity.in/dataset/bbmp-grievances-data), specifically the `BBMP Grievances 2020` CSV described there as “Ward-level grievances filed in 2020 (starts from 8th Feb 2020).” I derived the count by filtering that CSV to `Ward Name = Hoodi` and `Category = Road Maintenance(Engg)`.
