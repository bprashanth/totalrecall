Q: In 2020, how many road related complaints came from Hoodi ward in Bengaluru? Citation also.
elapsed:97s

---

In BBMP’s 2020 grievances data, `Hoodi` ward had **332 road-related complaints**, using the dataset’s road category label `Road Maintenance(Engg)`.

Citation:
- BBMP Grievances 2020 dataset overview: [data.opencity.in/dataset/bbmp-grievances-data](https://data.opencity.in/dataset/bbmp-grievances-data)
- Official datastore query for `Ward Name = Hoodi` and `Category = Road Maintenance(Engg)` showing `total: 332`: [data.opencity.in/api/3/action/datastore_search?...](https://data.opencity.in/api/3/action/datastore_search?resource_id=58808356-4b0a-4b02-9d70-75993b4dcd1c&filters=%7B%22Ward%20Name%22%3A%22Hoodi%22%2C%22Category%22%3A%22Road%20Maintenance%28Engg%29%22%7D&limit=0)

One caveat: that `2020` dataset notes it **starts from 8 Feb 2020**, so it is not a full Jan-Dec capture.
