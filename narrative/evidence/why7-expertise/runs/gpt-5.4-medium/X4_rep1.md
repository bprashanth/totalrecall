Q: For India's 2021 unemployment rate, give me all three: the ILO modelled estimate (World Bank SL.UEM.TOTL.ZS), the PLFS usual status and current weekly status figures, and the CMIE estimate. Label each with its methodology and say which one a district NGO report should use. Citations.

For **India in calendar year 2021**, the three figures are:

- **World Bank / ILO modelled estimate:** **6.38%**.  
  Methodology: the World Bank’s `SL.UEM.TOTL.ZS` series is an **annual ILO-modelled, internationally harmonized estimate** of unemployment as a share of the total labor force, meant mainly for cross-country comparability.  
  Citation: [World Bank API, India, `SL.UEM.TOTL.ZS`](https://api.worldbank.org/v2/country/IND/indicator/SL.UEM.TOTL.ZS?format=json&per_page=70)

- **PLFS usual status (ps+ss):** **4.2%**.  
  Methodology: **official GoI PLFS** estimate for persons aged 15+ in **Jan-Dec 2021**, using a **365-day reference period** and counting both principal and subsidiary economic activity.  
  Citation: [MoSPI PLFS press note, Statement 4](https://mospi.gov.in/sites/default/files/press_release/Press_note_AR_PLFS_2021_22_24022023.pdf)

- **PLFS current weekly status (CWS):** **7.3%**.  
  Methodology: **official GoI PLFS** estimate for persons aged 15+ in **Jan-Dec 2021**, using a **7-day reference period**.  
  Citation: [MoSPI PLFS press note, Statement 5](https://mospi.gov.in/sites/default/files/press_release/Press_note_AR_PLFS_2021_22_24022023.pdf)

- **CMIE / CPHS estimate:** **7.9% in December 2021**.  
  Methodology: **private high-frequency household panel** data from CMIE’s Consumer Pyramids survey; CMIE says the unemployment database covers about **522,000 people aged 15+**, records status **as of the survey date**, and is collected across **three waves a year** for the full sample. CMIE’s public note says unemployment was **7.9% in Dec 2021**, up from **7.0% in Nov 2021**.  
  Citations: [CMIE methodology/product page](https://cmie.com/kommon/bin/sr.php?kall=wproducts&tabno=7010&prd=ue&portal_code=030020050010000000000000000000000000000000000), [CMIE note on Dec 2021](https://cmie.com/kommon/bin/sr.php?dt=20220103135752&kall=warticle&msec=816)

For a **district NGO report**, use **PLFS** as the primary India benchmark. It is the **official Indian labor-force survey**, with transparent definitions and standard use in Indian policy documents. Use the **World Bank/ILO** number only if you need **international comparison**, and use **CMIE** only if you explicitly want a **high-frequency private series**.

If the report needs **one single India 2021 unemployment number**, I would usually cite **PLFS usual status: 4.2%** and note **PLFS CWS: 7.3%** alongside it. If the narrative is specifically about **short-term job stress/current joblessness**, lead with **PLFS CWS** instead.

One caveat: **none of these is a district-level estimate**. They are national benchmarks, not district-representative unemployment rates.
