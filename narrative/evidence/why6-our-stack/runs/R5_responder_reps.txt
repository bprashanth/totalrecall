The total population of Erode district as per the 2011 Census is **22,51,744 persons** (Census 2011). This breaks down as:
- Rural: 10,93,768 persons
- Urban: 11,57,976 persons

The data comes from the District Census Handbook, Erode (Part-B: Village and Town-wise Primary Census Abstract), Census of India 2011, Directorate of Census Operations, Tamil Nadu. There is no more recent population count in our data pack — the next Census is due in 2026, results not yet available.
