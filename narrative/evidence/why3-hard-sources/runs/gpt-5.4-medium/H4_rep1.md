The cleanest published estimate I found for **organic turmeric in Erode** is **Rs. 55,660 per hectare for hired human labour**.

That comes from **R. Rengaraju, G. Kokila, and T. Sakthivel (2022)**, a cost-and-returns study of turmeric cultivation in **Kodumudi block, Erode district**. In **Table 2, “Cost and Returns of Organic Turmeric in the Sample Farms (Rs./ha)”**, they list:

- `Hired Human Labour`: **Rs. 55,660/ha**
- share of total organic cultivation cost: **27.07%**
- total organic cultivation cost: **Rs. 205,623.72/ha**

You can check it here:
- Article page: [IJRESM article](https://journal.ijresm.com/index.php/ijresm/article/view/2355)
- Direct PDF: [PDF](https://journal.ijresm.com/index.php/ijresm/article/download/2355/2293/2929)

For context, that figure is plausible relative to other Erode turmeric studies, but those are **not organic-only**:
- A 2023 Kodumudi/Erode turmeric study reports **human labour = Rs. 40,550/ha** for turmeric generally, not specifically organic: [DOI page](https://doi.org/10.9734/jeai/2023/v45i32106)
- Another Erode turmeric cost study reports **hired human labour = Rs. 60,520/ha**, again for turmeric generally rather than organic-only: [PDF](http://www.biology-journal.org/fulltext/v39i1/ijapb39-1-9.pdf)

So if you want the answer **strictly for organic turmeric farms in Erode from a proper cost study**, I would use:

**Hired labour cost = about Rs. 55,660 per hectare.**
