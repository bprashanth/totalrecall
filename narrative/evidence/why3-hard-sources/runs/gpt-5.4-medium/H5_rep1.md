For the **Aavin/Erode Union cooperative network**, the official Aavin page says **Erode Union has 526 Primary Milk Producers’ Cooperative Societies** and its **daily milk procurement is about 2.30 lakh litres per day**. That is the clearest official figure for milk handled by the district’s co-op societies. Source: [Aavin, Erode Union](https://aavin.tn.gov.in/unions/-/categories/57428).

If you mean **total milk produced in Erode district from all producers**, not just what the co-op union procures, the official **Statistical Hand Book of Tamil Nadu 2022-23** gives **Erode’s total estimated milk production as 473.606 thousand tonnes in 2022-23**. That figure appears in **Table 7.6, “District-wise Estimated Milk Production 2022-23”** in the state handbook. Source: [Tamil Nadu Statistical Hand Book 2022-23, Animal Husbandry PDF](https://www.csaf.des.tn.gov.in/sites/default/files/2025-07/7.%20ANIMAL%20HUSBANDRY.pdf).

So, in short:

- **Milk co-op societies in Erode Union:** **526**
- **Milk handled by those co-ops:** **about 2.30 lakh litres/day**
- **Total Erode district milk production:** **473.606 thousand tonnes in 2022-23**

The two milk figures are **different measures**: one is **co-op procurement**, the other is **district-wide production**. If you want, I can also convert the district production figure into **lakh litres/day equivalent** for easier comparison.
