Q: how many villages come under anthiyur taluk in erode district? give citations i can check.
elapsed:26s

---

**Anthiyur taluk** in Erode district has **30 revenue villages** according to the official Erode District Administration website.

However, some sources count **38 villages** in total (including census/panchayat villages beyond just revenue villages), depending on the classification used.

## Citations

1. **Erode District Official Website (Revenue Administration)**
   - https://erode.nic.in/about-district/administrative-setup/revenue_administration/
   - Lists Anthiyur taluk with **30 revenue villages** under Gobichettipalayam Division.

2. **VillageInfo.in**
   - https://villageinfo.in/tamil-nadu/erode/anthiyur/
   - Lists **38 villages** (includes both urban and rural census villages).

3. **Wikipedia – Anthiyur taluk**
   - https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Anthiyur_taluk
   - Notes Anthiyur taluk comprises four blocks: Anthiyur, Athani, Burgur, and Ammapet.

The discrepancy (30 vs 38) is because the official district site counts *revenue villages* (an administrative unit), while census-based sources count all habitation villages including urban areas. The most authoritative figure is **30 revenue villages** from the district collector's website.
