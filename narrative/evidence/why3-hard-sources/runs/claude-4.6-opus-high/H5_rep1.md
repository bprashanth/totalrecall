## Milk Co-operative Societies in Erode District

### From the 2013-14 Dairy Development Policy Note

- **782 milk producers' co-operative societies** in Erode district
- **38,913 pouring members** (farmers actively supplying milk)

Source: Dairy Development Department Policy Note 2013-14, Table on p. 11
[https://agritech.tnau.ac.in/animal_husbandry/pdf/dairy_development_policy%20note%202013-14.pdf](https://agritech.tnau.ac.in/animal_husbandry/pdf/dairy_development_policy%20note%202013-14.pdf)

### From a 2025 research paper (data for 2022-23)

- **526 co-operative societies** affiliated with Aavin (Erode District Co-operative Milk Producers' Union)
- **29,357 pouring farmers**
- Aavin procures approximately **2,30,000 litres per day** (~83,950 tonnes annually), representing about **16.4%** of total district production

Source: "Economic Analysis of Demand, Supply, and Cost of Milk with Special Reference to Milk Producing Farmers in Erode District of Tamilnadu," *International Journal for Multidisciplinary Research*, Vol. 7, Issue 4, 2025
[https://doi.org/10.36948/ijfmr.2025.v07i04.52999](https://doi.org/10.36948/ijfmr.2025.v07i04.52999)

### Total Milk Production in Erode District

| Period | Production | Source |
|---|---|---|
| 2010-11 | 414,074 tonnes (est.) | Statistical Handbook - Animal Husbandry 2012, TNAU |
| 2022-23 | **473,606 tonnes** | Integrated Sample Survey Report 2022-23 (cited in the IJFMR paper above) |
| 2025 (est.) | ~513,000 tonnes | Projected at 4.07% annual growth (IJFMR paper) |

---

The drop from 782 to 526 societies between the two time periods likely reflects district reorganization (Erode was split to create Tiruppur district in 2009, and some restructuring of cooperative units has occurred since).

You can verify these figures at the links above. The Aavin official site ([https://aavin.tn.gov.in/milk-production-section-2](https://aavin.tn.gov.in/milk-production-section-2)) also has district-union-wise procurement data.
