I was unable to find a publicly available, taluk-level breakdown of ration shops specifically for Thalavadi. The data published online is at the **district level** (Erode district) or **state level** (Tamil Nadu), not broken down by individual taluk. Here's what I can tell you:

**District-level data (Erode):**
- Erode district (which includes Thalavadi and 9 other taluks) has a total of **1,134–1,137 fair price shops** across all 10 taluks.
- Source: [TNPDS Ration Shop Code List 2026 – InstaPDF](https://instapdf.in/tnpds-ration-shop-code-list/)

**State-level full-time vs. part-time breakdown (Tamil Nadu total):**
- Full-time: 25,589 | Part-time: 9,580 | Total: 35,169
- Source: [TNCSC PDS page](https://www.tncsc.tn.gov.in/en/PDS.html)

**No taluk-specific public data exists** for Thalavadi's full-time/part-time split online.
