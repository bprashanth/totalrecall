Based on published cost studies, here's what hired labour costs per hectare for organic turmeric farms in Erode district:

## Key Finding

**Rs. 55,660 per hectare** for hired human labour on organic turmeric farms, constituting **27.07%** of total cultivation cost (Rs. 2,05,624/ha).

This comes from a comparative study of organic vs. inorganic turmeric in Erode. For comparison, inorganic turmeric farms in the same study reported Rs. 50,660/ha (23.42% of costs).

## Citable Sources

1. **Primary source (organic vs. inorganic comparison):**
   - Comparative Economic Analysis of Cost and Returns of Organic and Inorganic Turmeric Production in Erode District of Tamil Nadu. *International Journal of Research in Engineering, Science and Management (IJRESM)*.
   - Full text PDF: https://journal.ijresm.com/index.php/ijresm/article/download/2355/2293/2929
   - Article page: https://journal.ijresm.com/index.php/ijresm/article/view/2355

2. **Supporting study (general turmeric, Erode):**
   - "Cost and Returns Analysis of Turmeric Production in Kodumudi Block of Erode District from Tamil Nadu, India." *Journal of Experimental Agriculture International*, 2023. Reports human labour at Rs. 40,550/ha (27.75% of cost).
   - DOI: https://doi.org/10.9734/jeai/2023/v45i32106

3. **Another Erode study (general turmeric):**
   - Reports hired human labour at Rs. 60,520/ha (24.19% of total cost of Rs. 2,50,083/ha). Published in *Indian Journal of Applied & Pure Biology*, Vol. 39(1), ISSN 0970-2091.
   - PDF: http://www.biology-journal.org/fulltext/v39i1/ijapb39-1-9.pdf

4. **Older Erode study:**
   - "A Study on Cost of Cultivation and Production of Turmeric in Erode District of Tamilnadu." *Humanities, Arts, Medicine and Sciences (HIJRH)*, 2019.
   - DOI: https://doi.org/10.15613/hijrh/2019/v6i1/183885

The IJRESM paper (source 1) is the one that explicitly separates organic from inorganic and gives the Rs. 55,660/ha figure for hired labour on organic farms.
