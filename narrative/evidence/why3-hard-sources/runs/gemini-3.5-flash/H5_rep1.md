The milk cooperative sector in the Erode district of Tamil Nadu, India, is primarily organized under the **Erode District Cooperative Milk Producers’ Union** (operating under the state brand **Aavin**). 

Here is the data on the number of milk cooperative societies and their milk production/procurement across different periods, followed by verifiable citations.

---

### 1. Recent Figures (2024–2025)

* **Number of Milk Cooperative Societies:** 
  * According to the official Aavin website, there are **526 primary milk producers' cooperative societies** affiliated with the Erode Union.
  * The *Tamil Nadu Dairy Development Department Policy Note 2025-26* reports **482 active societies** (403 General, 2 SC/ST, and 77 Women societies) as of early 2025.
* **Milk Production & Procurement:**
  * **Aavin Procurement:** Aavin Erode procures approximately **230,000 litres of milk per day (2.30 lakh LPD)** (around 83,950 tonnes annually) from 29,357 active pouring members.
  * **Total District Production:** The total annual milk production in Erode District is estimated at **513,000 tonnes** (averaging roughly **1.4 million litres per day**). Aavin’s cooperative procurement represents about 16.4% of the district's total milk production. The remaining milk is handled by private dairies (such as Milky Mist), consumed locally, or processed individually.

---

### 2. Historical Figures (2015–2016)

* **Milk Procurement:** 
  * During **2015-16**, average cooperative milk procurement rose to **316,543 litres per day**, compared to **232,417 litres per day** in **2014-15**.

---

### 3. Historical Figures (2012–2013)

* **Number of Milk Cooperative Societies:** 
  * There were **782 primary societies** with **38,913 active pouring members**.
* **Milk Procurement:** 
  * Average daily procurement was **251,000 litres per day** (up from **231,000 LPD** in 2011-12).
