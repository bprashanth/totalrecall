Based on proper economic cost studies of turmeric cultivation in Erode, Tamil Nadu, the cost of hired human labour for organic turmeric farms is detailed below.

---

### Study 1: Rengaraju, Kokila, & Sakthivel (2022)

This study provides the most direct and detailed breakdown of per-hectare organic turmeric cultivation costs in the Kodumudi block of Erode district.

*   **Hired Human Labour Cost:** **Rs. 55,660.00 per hectare**
    *   This represents **27.07%** of the total cost of cultivation (Cost C), which is Rs. 205,623.72 per hectare.
*   **Family Labour Cost (Imputed):** **Rs. 23,560.00 per hectare** (11.46% of total cost).
*   **Total Labour Cost (Hired + Family):** **Rs. 79,220.00 per hectare** (38.53% of total cost).
*   **Comparison (Inorganic/Conventional):** Hired human labour for inorganic turmeric was slightly lower at **Rs. 50,660.00 per hectare** (23.42% of the total Cost C of Rs. 216,382.32 per hectare).

#### Citation:
Rengaraju, R., Kokila, G., & Sakthivel, T. (2022). "Comparative Economic Analysis of Cost and Returns of Organic and Inorganic Turmeric Production in Erode District of Tamil Nadu". *International Journal of Research in Engineering, Science and Management* (IJRESM), Vol. 5, No. 9, pp. 41–44.
*   [Check the Study on IJRESM](https://journal.ijresm.com/index.php/ijresm/article/view/2355)
*   [Direct PDF Link](https://journal.ijresm.com/index.php/ijresm/article/download/2355/2293/2929)

### Study 2: Tholkappian & Rukmani Devi (2013)

An older comparative study in Erode district reports costs on a per-acre basis. It groups human labour and draught animal power under the category of **"Biological Power"**.

*   **Biological Power Cost (Organic):** **Rs. 22,586.00 per acre** (approximately **Rs. 55,810.00 per hectare**).
    *   This is the largest expense component, representing **67.16%** of the total per-acre organic cultivation cost of Rs. 33,630.00 (approx. Rs. 83,100.00 per hectare).
*   **Biological Power Cost (Conventional):** **Rs. 21,950.00 per acre** (approx. **Rs. 54,238.00 per hectare**).

#### Citation:
Tholkappian, C., & Rukmani Devi, P. B. (2013). "Economic Analysis of Organic and Convectional Turmeric Cultivation of Erode District in Tamil Nadu". *IBMRD's Journal of Management & Research*, Vol. 2, No. 1, pp. 32–41.
