To find the precise, real-time number of full-time and part-time ration shops (officially called **Fair Price Shops** or **FPS**) specifically in **Thalavadi Taluk** (bifurcated from Sathyamangalam in 2016), it is helpful to understand how public distribution system (PDS) data is organized and administered in Tamil Nadu.

### 1. Granular Taluk-Level Digitization Status
Currently, there is no public consolidated report that publishes a single number for full-time vs. part-time ration shops solely for Thalavadi Taluk. This is verified by the local regional directory **Thalavadi.com** in its Civic Services section:
> *"Thalavadi Taluk has a network of Public Distribution System (PDS) Fair Price Shops serving each revenue village cluster... Exact shop locations are administered by the Taluk Supply Office and VAOs at village level. Data on individual shop addresses has not been fully digitized in public databases."*

### 2. District-Wide Context (Erode District, Latest Jan 2026)
In Tamil Nadu, PDS statistics are officially aggregated at the district level. Erode District as a whole comprises **10 revenue taluks** (including Thalavadi) and operates a total of **1,270 fair price shops** serving over 746,500 ration cardholders. 

According to official figures released by the Erode District Administration in **January 2026**, the breakdown is as follows:
* **Total Fair Price Shops:** 1,270
* **Full-Time Shops:** 927 (913 run by the Cooperative Department, 14 run by the Tamil Nadu Civil Supplies Corporation)
* **Part-Time Shops:** 341 (340 run by the Cooperative Department, 1 run by the Tamil Nadu Civil Supplies Corporation)
* **Women-Run Shops:** 2 (operated by women’s self-help groups)

### 3. Thalavadi's Hilly Geography and Mobile Shops
Because Thalavadi sits at an average elevation of ~823 m and much of the taluk lies within the environmentally sensitive buffer zone of the **Sathyamangalam Tiger Reserve (STR)**, it is governed under relaxed rules to prevent tribal families in remote villages from walking more than 1.5 to 2 km to fetch rations. 

To serve Thalavadi's **28 revenue villages** and **9 gram panchayats**, the government uses a mixture of:
* **Permanent Full-Time and Part-Time shops** located in major hubs like Thalavadi town and Hasanur.
* **Mobile Fair Price Shops (Mobile Vans)** that periodically visit deep forest and hilly settlements (such as Kongahalli, Thiganarai, and Solagar Thotti) to distribute essential commodities directly to tribal cardholders.
