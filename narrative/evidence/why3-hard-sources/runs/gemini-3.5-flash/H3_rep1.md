**Canara Bank** has the most bank branches in the Erode district of Tamil Nadu. 

### Branch Count
Canara Bank operates **75 to 82 branches** in the Erode district, depending on the listing criteria (some directories include specialized and administrative offices like Regional Offices, Currency Chests, SME-specific branches, and Accounts Sections, while others only list retail branches). 

For context, Canara Bank is also the designated **Lead Bank** for the Erode district, coordinating overall banking and credit planning (such as the Annual Credit Plan) in the region.

### Comparison with Other Banks in Erode District
* **Indian Overseas Bank (IOB):** 49 to 60 branches
* **State Bank of India (SBI):** 55 branches
* **Karur Vysya Bank (KVB):** 36 to 37 branches
* **Erode District Central Cooperative Bank (EDCCB):** 28 branches (out of 36 total, as 8 are located in the neighboring Tiruppur district)
* **Union Bank of India:** 23 branches
* **Indian Bank:** 20 to 21 branches
