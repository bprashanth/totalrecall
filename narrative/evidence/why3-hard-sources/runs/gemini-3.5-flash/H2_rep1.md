According to the official government records of the Erode District Administration, there are **30 Revenue Villages** in Anthiyur Taluk. 

However, depending on the directory or definition used (such as Census data vs. Revenue Department data), you will see different figures:
- **30 Revenue Villages** (Official Revenue Department count)
- **31 Villages** (Census-defined Sub-District count)
- **38 Villages** (Unofficial directories like VillageInfo)

Here is a breakdown of the official and secondary citations that you can check:

### 1. Official Government Citation (30 Revenue Villages)
According to the Erode District Administration under the Tamil Nadu State Government, Anthiyur Taluk is under the Gobichettipalayam Revenue Division and comprises **30 Revenue Villages** distributed across four Firkas (Anthiyur, Ammapettai, Athani, and Bargur).

* **Source Link:** [Erode District - Revenue Administration Setup](https://erode.nic.in/about-district/administrative-setup/revenue_administration/)
* **Tamil Document Link (Names of the 30 villages):** [Erode District Documents - Anthiyur Taluk Revenue Villages](https://erode.nic.in/ta/document/%E0%AE%85%E0%AE%A8%E0%AF%8D%E0%AE%A4%E0%AE%BF%E0%AE%AF%E0%AF%82%E0%AE%B0%E0%AF%8D-%E0%AE%B5%E0%AE%9F%E0%AF%8D%E0%AE%9F-%E0%AE%B5%E0%AE%B0%E0%AF%81%E0%AE%B5%E0%AE%AF%E0%AF%8D-%E0%AE%95/)

### 2. Census-Based Citation (31 Villages)
Because Anthiyur Taluk was created in **November 2012** (bifurcated from Bhavani and Gobichettipalayam taluks), it did not exist as a separate taluk in the 2011 Census of India. Under Census-based tracking of sub-districts mapped to the newer boundaries, there are **31 villages** identified in the Anthiyur Sub-District.

* **Source Link:** [VillageInfo.org - Anthiyur Sub-District Information](https://villageinfo.org/village/634849) (Refer to the section *"How many villages are in the Anthiyur Sub-District?"*)

### 3. Alternative Village Directory (38 Villages)
Some non-governmental administrative directory databases group the local habitations and panchayats under the taluk category, resulting in a list of **38 villages**.

* **Source Link:** [VillageInfo.in - List of Villages in Anthiyur Taluka](https://villageinfo.in/tamil-nadu/erode/anthiyur/)
