Q: how many milk co-op societies were there in erode district, and how much milk did they produce? give citations i can check.
elapsed:146s

---

**Best current official figures for Erode’s milk co-ops**

### Societies
**482** Primary Milk Producers’ Cooperative Societies (as listed in the Tamil Nadu Dairy Development Department Policy Note 2025–26):
- General: 403  
- SC/ST: 2  
- Women: 77  
- **Total: 482**

**Citation:** [Dairy Development Department Policy Note 2025–26 (PDF)](https://cms.tn.gov.in/cms_migrated/document/docfiles/dairy_e_pn_2025_26.pdf) — district-wise societies table (Erode row: `403 / 2 / 77 / 482`). Also linked from the [TN Dairy Development dept profile](https://www.tn.gov.in/dept_profile.php?dep_id=Mw==).

Aavin’s Erode Union page currently says **526** societies affiliated with the union (and **29,357** pouring members). That is a different, union-side count and may include societies counted differently than the Policy Note table.

**Citation:** [Aavin – Erode District Cooperative Milk Producers’ Union](https://aavin.tn.gov.in/unions/-/categories/57428)

### Milk from those societies (co-op procurement)
About **2.30 lakh litres per day** (~230,000 LPD) procured from those societies’ members.

**Citation:** same Aavin Erode Union page above (“Daily milk procurement is around 2.30 lakh liters per day”).

That is **co-op procurement**, not total district milk output. Much milk in Erode also goes to private dairies or local use.

### Related capacity (not the same as production)
Policy Note 2025–26 lists Erode Union dairy/chilling capacity as:
- Dairy processing: **3,00,000** LPD  
- BMC chilling: **2,49,000** LPD  
- **Total capacity: 5,49,000** LPD  

**Citation:** same [Policy Note PDF](https://cms.tn.gov.in/cms_migrated/document/docfiles/dairy_e_pn_2025_26.pdf) (district-wise dairy/chilling capacity table, Erode row).

---

**Short answer to check:** use **482 societies** (Policy Note) and **~2.30 lakh LPD** procured (Aavin Erode Union). If you need total district milk production (all sources), that is a separate ISS Animal Husbandry figure (~473,606 tonnes in 2022–23 in secondary citations of the Integrated Sample Survey), not the co-op-only number.
