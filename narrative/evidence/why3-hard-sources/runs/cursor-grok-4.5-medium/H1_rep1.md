Q: how many ration shops are there in thalavadi taluk of erode, full time and part time both? give citations i can check.
elapsed:189s

As of **2023–24**, Thalavadi taluk has **40** ration (fair price) shops:

| Type | Count |
|------|------:|
| Full-time | **15** |
| Part-time | **25** |
| **Total** | **40** |

**Source (official):** Erode District Statistical Handbook 2023–24, Table **9.1 Number of Fair Price Shops**, citing the District Supply Office, Erode.

- Handbook listing page: [erode.nic.in – Statistical Report](https://erode.nic.in/document-category/statistical-report/)
- Direct PDF (page with Table 9.1): [cdn.s3waas.gov.in …/2025012713.pdf](https://cdn.s3waas.gov.in/s3bca82e41ee7b0833588399b1fcd177c7/uploads/2025/01/2025012713.pdf)
