Q: for organic turmeric farms in erode, what does hired labour cost per hectare, going by any proper cost study? give citations i can check.

---

**About ₹55,660 per hectare** for hired human labour on organic turmeric in Erode, from the clearest published cost study that splits organic vs inorganic and reports hired labour separately.

### Primary citation (organic, hired labour, Erode)

**Rengaraju, R., Kokila, G., & Sakthivel, T. (2022).** Comparative Economic Analysis of Cost and Returns of Organic and Inorganic Turmeric Production in Erode District of Tamil Nadu. *International Journal of Research in Engineering, Science and Management*, 5(9), 41–44.

| Item | Organic (₹/ha) | Inorganic (₹/ha) |
|------|----------------|------------------|
| **Hired human labour** | **55,660** (27.1% of Cost C) | 50,660 (23.4%) |
| Imputed family labour (not hired) | 23,560 | 22,500 |
| Total cost (Cost C) | 2,05,624 | 2,16,382 |

- Area: Kodumudi block, Erode; sample of 90 farmers  
- PDF: https://journal.ijresm.com/index.php/ijresm/article/download/2355/2293/2929  
- Article page: https://journal.ijresm.com/index.php/ijresm/article/view/2355  

Organic farms spent ~₹5,000/ha more on hired labour than inorganic ones in that study.

### Related Erode studies (for context)

1. **Tholkappian, C., & Rukmani Devi, P. B. (2013).** Economic Analysis of Organic and Conventional Turmeric Cultivation of Erode District in Tamil Nadu. *IBMRD’s Journal of Management & Research*, 2(1), 32–41. https://doi.org/10.17697/ibmrd/2013/v2i1/47377  
   - Organic sample: 30 farms (2010–11). Does **not** isolate hired labour; reports “Biological Power” at ₹22,586/acre (~₹55,800/ha if scaled), which bundles labour/animal power. PDF: https://ibmrdjournal.in/index.php/ibmrd/article/download/47377/39286  

2. **Kodumudi turmeric cost studies (not organic-specific):** hired/human labour often ~₹40,550–₹60,520/ha depending on year and whether family labour is included — useful as a local benchmark only.  
   - e.g. JEAI 2023: https://doi.org/10.9734/jeai/2023/v45i32106  

**Bottom line:** For organic turmeric in Erode with a proper hired-labour line item, use **₹55,660/ha** from Rengaraju et al. (2022), and cite that PDF above.
