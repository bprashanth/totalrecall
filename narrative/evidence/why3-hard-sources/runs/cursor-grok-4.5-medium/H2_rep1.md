Q: how many villages come under anthiyur taluk in erode district? give citations i can check.
elapsed:44s

---

**Anthiyur taluk has 30 revenue villages**, per the Erode District administration.

### Primary citation (official)
- **Erode District — Revenue Administration**  
  https://erode.nic.in/about-district/administrative-setup/revenue_administration/  

  On that page, under “Revenue Villages (375)”, row 8 lists:  
  **Gobichettipalayam → Anthiyur → 30**

  The same page also notes Anthiyur has 4 firkas: Anthiyur, Ammapettai, Athani, and Bargur.

### Related official page (village list document)
- **Anthiyur taluk revenue villages (Tamil)** — document page dated 27/06/2018:  
  https://erode.nic.in/ta/document/%E0%AE%85%E0%AE%A8%E0%AF%8D%E0%AE%A4%E0%AE%BF%E0%AE%AF%E0%AF%82%E0%AE%B0%E0%AF%8D-%E0%AE%B5%E0%AE%9F%E0%AF%8D%E0%AE%9F-%E0%AE%B5%E0%AE%B0%E0%AF%81%E0%AE%B5%E0%AE%BE%E0%AE%AF%E0%AF%8D-%E0%AE%95/

### Note on other counts
Some non-official directories (e.g. [VillageInfo.in](https://villageinfo.in/tamil-nadu/erode/anthiyur/)) list **38**, often mixing towns, reserve forests, and census-style entries. For administrative/revenue purposes, the district site’s **30** is the figure to use.
