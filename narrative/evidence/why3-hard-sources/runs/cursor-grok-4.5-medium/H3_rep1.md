**Canara Bank** has the most branches listed for Erode district — about **75**.

### Branch counts (IFSC directory listings)

| Bank | Branches listed in Erode | Source to check |
|------|--------------------------|-----------------|
| **Canara Bank** | **75** | [ProKerala](https://www.prokerala.com/banking/canara-bank/branches.php?state=Tamil+Nadu&district=Erode), [BankIFSCcode.com](https://bankifsccode.com/CANARA_BANK/TAMIL_NADU/ERODE) |
| Indian Overseas Bank | 60 | [ProKerala](https://www.prokerala.com/banking/indian-overseas-bank/branches.php?state=tamil+nadu&district=Erode) |
| State Bank of India | 47 | [BankIFSCcode.com](https://bankifsccode.com/STATE_BANK_OF_INDIA/TAMIL_NADU/ERODE) |
| Indian Bank | 21 | [BankIFSCcode.com](https://bankifsccode.com/INDIAN_BANK/TAMIL_NADU/ERODE) |

One other directory puts Canara at **82**: [AskBankIFSCCode](https://www.askbankifsccode.com/CANARA-BANK/TAMIL-NADU/ERODE).

### Context / caveats

- Canara Bank is the **Lead Bank** for Erode (confirmed by [SLBC Tamil Nadu](https://www.slbctn.com/Lead_Bank.aspx) and recent [Hindu coverage of the district credit plan](https://www.thehindu.com/news/cities/Coimbatore/erode-sets-36205-crore-priority-sector-lending-target-for-2026-27/article71197943.ece)).
- These counts come from IFSC master-style listings, which can include regional offices / SME / processing units and may still tag some Tiruppur-area places under “Erode.”
- For a fully official bank-wise figure, the Lead District Manager (Canara Bank, Erode) or an SLBC/ACP booklet would be the best check; publicly available IFSC directories are the clearest comparable sources online.
