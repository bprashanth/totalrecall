# Scoring rubric

Each turn is scored 0, 1, or 2 on six dimensions, against the question and observable source/tool
trace rather than ecological plausibility alone:

1. **Request fidelity** — retains the requested place, subject, clauses, distances, periods and
   follow-up references.
2. **Execution** — actually obtains/calculates the relevant evidence, or produces a precise and
   truthful DataRequest when the operation is unsupported.
3. **Grounding** — every specific claim is supported by the recorded execution or cited public
   source available to that arm.
4. **Evidence boundary** — preserves local/regional scope and observed/reported/proxy/modelled
   distinctions; does not turn proximity into interaction or trend into causality.
5. **Usefulness** — gives a direct, understandable answer before bounded limitations and proposes
   a practical next measurement where needed.
6. **Auditability** — leaves enough visible source, operation, parameters and provenance to check
   or repeat the result.

Critical errors are counted separately: fabricated source/result, unsupported causal promotion,
modelled-as-observed, wrong place or geometry, silent loss of a requested clause, wrong distance
semantics, or reading another arm/gold asset.  The first round is exploratory: no winner or stop
claim is preregistered, and no architecture may be repaired after seeing a benchmark answer.
