Short answer: I could only find **one likely match** with public evidence of both restoration work and some benefit after invasive removal, and **one more possible match** where the elephant side looks likely but the invasive-removal benefit is not public.

**Best public match**

1. **Kottayur Kollai, Denkanikottai side, Krishnagiri district**
- **Found:** A public report says people in **Kottayur Kollai** were removing **Lantana camara** and using it for furniture. The ranger and village forest committee linked this to **eradicating the alien species** and **reducing forest fires**. Source: *New Indian Express*, 1 May 2022: https://www.newindianexpress.com/states/tamil-nadu/2022/May/01/weeding-waste-furnishing-wonders-2448407.html
- **Found:** Krishnagiri district’s own page says **Denkanikottai taluk** is prime elephant habitat in the **Cauvery Elephant Reserve**. Source: Krishnagiri district “At a glance”: https://krishnagiri.nic.in/about-district/district-at-a-glance/
- **Found:** Public reports also show **regular elephant movement** in the Hosur/Denkanikottai landscape, including SMS alerts to villages and recent elephant movement near Denkanikottai. Sources:  
  https://www.newindianexpress.com/states/tamil-nadu/2023/May/11/tn-forest-dept-starts-sending-sms-to-farmers-on-elephant-movement-2574290.html  
  https://timesofindia.indiatimes.com/city/coimbatore/wild-elephants-disrupt-traffic-on-hogenakkal-anchetty-road/articleshow/132440352.cms
- **Estimate:** This site is **very likely within 5 km of elephant records/movement**, but I did **not** find an open public point dataset that let me prove the 5 km distance exactly.
- **Did it get better after invasive removal?** **Probably yes, but evidence is weak/qualitative.** Public reporting supports **less fire risk** and local use/removal of lantana. I did **not** find before/after biodiversity or vegetation monitoring.

**Possible match, but weaker**

2. **Elephants By The Lake (EBTL), Budhimutlu, Krishnagiri taluk**
- **Found:** EBTL is a public restoration site in **Budhimutlu, Krishnagiri**. Source: https://ebtl.earth/get-involved/ and https://ebtl.earth/cegs/
- **Found:** Budhimutlu is next to **Veppanapalli** on public map pages, and a public report from **29 April 2025** says a wild elephant killed a man at **Veppanapalli**. Sources:  
  https://mapcarta.com/35028154  
  https://timesofindia.indiatimes.com/city/coimbatore/wild-elephant-tramples-man-to-death-in-krishnagiri/articleshow/120706088.cms
- **Estimate:** EBTL is **likely within 5 km** of that public elephant record, because Budhimutlu and Veppanapalli are adjacent localities. But again, I could not verify the exact distance from an open point record.
- **Did it get better after lantana/invasive removal?** **I found no public proof** for this specific site.

**What seems missing**
- I could **not** find a public, open list of restoration sites near Krishnagiri with exact coordinates **and** exact elephant observation points to test the **5 km** rule cleanly.
- I also could **not** find strong public **before/after monitoring** showing which Krishnagiri-area sites improved after lantana or other invasive removal.

So, based on public sources alone, **Kottayur Kollai is the strongest answer**, but the **5 km elephant check is still an estimate, not a confirmed GIS match**.
