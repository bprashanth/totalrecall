Yes. Public studies suggest that birds often **eat Lantana fruit** and can **spread its seeds**, but this depends on the bird species.

**What I found**
- **Birds do eat Lantana and can disperse it.** In South Africa, a field study found 56 native bird species visiting fruiting *Lantana camara*; 28 of them ate the fruit, and the authors concluded birds likely help spread it.  
  Source: Bitani & Downs 2022, *South African Journal of Botany*  
  https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/pii/S0254629922002599
- **Some birds spread seeds, some destroy them.** In the Galapagos, captive-feeding work found mockingbirds passed invasive plant seeds, including Lantana, without losing seed viability, while finches usually crushed seeds and acted more like seed predators than dispersers.  
  Source summary with article link: https://www.researchgate.net/publication/235722248_Invasive_plant_seed_viability_after_processing_by_some_endemic_Galapagos_birds
- In another Galapagos **dry-zone** study, Darwin’s finches ate Lantana fruits but mostly **destroyed the seeds**; they may still cause some **short-distance dispersal** by dropping fruits.  
  Source summary: https://agris.fao.org/search/en/providers/122535/records/65de46e47c7033e84bea6889
- A review focused on India says Lantana fruit dispersal is **mostly by frugivorous birds**, often including mynas.  
  Source: Negi et al. 2019, *The Botanical Review*  
  https://link.springer.com/article/10.1007/s12229-019-09209-8
- An Australian government synthesis also says seed dispersal is **mainly by fruit-eating birds**, and gut passage/removal of pulp can improve germination.  
  Source: NSW Scientific Committee  
  https://www.environment.nsw.gov.au/topics/animals-and-plants/threatened-species/nsw-threatened-species-scientific-committee/determinations/final-determinations/2004-2007/invasion-establishment-and-spread-of-lantana-key-threatening-process-listing

**What I estimate for dry forest restoration areas like EBTL**
- If EBTL has **bulbuls, mynas, thrush-like frugivores, or other small/medium fruit-eating birds**, Lantana is **likely being eaten and spread** there.
- If the bird community is dominated by **seed-crushing birds**, spread by birds may be lower.
