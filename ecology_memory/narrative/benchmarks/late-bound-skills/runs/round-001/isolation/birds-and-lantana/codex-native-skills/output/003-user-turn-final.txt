`Observation:`
- Some bird species in the EBTL bird list also appear in a regional semi-arid Lantana feeding study.
- That study showed some of those birds swallowed Lantana fruits.

`Proxy:`
- This makes Lantana seed spread by birds at EBTL plausible.

`Not supported as a local claim:`
- We do not have evidence that Lantana is present at EBTL.
- We do not have EBTL-local observations of birds eating Lantana.
- We do not have EBTL-local evidence that seeds were passed or dropped.

`How sure are we?`
- For “birds at EBTL are spreading Lantana”: low confidence.
- For “some EBTL birds could spread Lantana if it is present”: moderate confidence as a regional proxy, not a local finding.

`What is missing:`
- Local Lantana mapping at EBTL.
- Bird feeding observations at EBTL.
- Seed dispersal evidence at EBTL, such as droppings, regrowth, or watched fruit removal.
