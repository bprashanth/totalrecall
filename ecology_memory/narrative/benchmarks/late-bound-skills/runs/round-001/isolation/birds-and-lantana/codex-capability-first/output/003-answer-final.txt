There is no local evidence yet that birds at EBTL eat Lantana or spread its seeds.

Local observed evidence:
No local Lantana records were found for EBTL in the public point sources used here.
Some bird species linked to a regional Lantana fruit-use dataset do have public records in the EBTL area, but that does not show they were feeding on Lantana there.

Regional modelled evidence:
There is 1 regional Lantana literature lead and 5 bird-transfer leads. These support a possible mechanism that some birds can eat Lantana fruit and move seeds, but only as a transfer hypothesis from outside EBTL.

Proxy evidence:
The co-occurrence check found 0 matches between the Lantana literature lead and the bird-transfer leads at EBTL. This is not evidence of interaction, same-time presence, or site presence.

What is missing:
Local proof that Lantana is present at EBTL.
Local proof that birds eat its fruits there.
Local proof that birds move or drop its seeds there.

What the field team can collect:
Map Lantana plants at EBTL.
Record bird visits to fruiting Lantana.
Photograph feeding birds and identify species.
Check droppings or perch sites for Lantana seeds.
Track where seedlings appear near roosts, fences, and perch trees.
