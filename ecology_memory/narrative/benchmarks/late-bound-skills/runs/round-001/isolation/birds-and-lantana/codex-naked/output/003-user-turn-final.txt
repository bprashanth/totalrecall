You can say **birds at EBTL probably help spread Lantana**, but you should **not say this is proven for EBTL**.

**What we found**
- Public studies show that many birds, especially **bulbuls and mynas**, **eat Lantana fruit** and can spread its seeds.  
  Sources:  
  Bitani & Downs 2022: https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/pii/S0254629922002599  
  Aruna & Balasubramanian 2017: https://www.faunajournal.com/archives/2017.v4.i4.C.377/fruiting-phenology-and-avian-frugivory-on-lantana-camara-in-a-mixed-dry-deciduous-forest-western-ghats-tamilnadu-india  
  NSW summary: https://www.environment.nsw.gov.au/topics/animals-and-plants/threatened-species/nsw-threatened-species-scientific-committee/determinations/final-determinations/2004-2007/invasion-establishment-and-spread-of-lantana-key-threatening-process-listing
- Public bird records for **Krishnagiri district** include **Common Myna, Jungle Myna, Red-vented Bulbul, Red-whiskered Bulbul, and White-browed Bulbul**.  
  Source: https://avibase.bsc-eoc.org/checklist.jsp?region=INsetnks

**What I estimate**
- If those species occur at EBTL, it is **reasonable to infer** that birds there are **likely** eating Lantana and **may be spreading it**.

**How sure are we?**
- **Medium confidence** for: “Birds at EBTL likely contribute to Lantana spread.”
- **Low confidence** for: “We have proven birds are spreading Lantana at EBTL.”

**What is missing**
- No public **EBTL bird list** that I could verify.
- No public **EBTL-specific study** showing birds eating Lantana there.
- No local evidence such as **feeding observations, droppings with seeds, or germination tests**.

A safe way to write it is: **“Based on studies from similar South Indian dry forest areas, birds at EBTL likely help spread Lantana, but this has not yet been directly confirmed at EBTL.”**
