No. We cannot say that birds at EBTL are spreading Lantana, and our confidence is low.

Local observed evidence:
We do not have local evidence that Lantana is present at EBTL.
We do not have local observations of birds eating Lantana at EBTL.
We do not have local evidence of Lantana seeds in droppings, regurgitated fruit, or new seedlings under bird perches.

Regional modelled evidence:
We do have transfer evidence that some bird species associated with EBTL may also be linked to a regional Lantana fruit-use dataset.
That only suggests a possible mechanism. It does not confirm that it is happening at EBTL.

Proxy evidence:
The earlier co-occurrence check found no matched records at EBTL between the Lantana literature lead and the bird-transfer leads.
That is not evidence of interaction, same-time presence, or seed dispersal.

How sure are we:
Very unsure for EBTL specifically.
At most, we can say bird-mediated Lantana dispersal is plausible in the region, but unproven at EBTL.

What is missing:
Confirmed Lantana presence at EBTL.
Direct feeding observations.
Seed-dispersal evidence linked to birds at EBTL.

What the field team can collect:
Map fruiting Lantana plants at EBTL.
Watch and record bird visits to those plants.
Collect droppings or feeding remains below perches and test for Lantana seeds.
Check for Lantana seedlings under roosts, fences, and perch trees.
