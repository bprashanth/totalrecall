The biggest doubt is non-detection.

Observations:
- EBTL has no king cobra record in the local inventory.
- The local snake survey lasted only three days, from 2024-09-05 to 2024-09-07.

Reports:
- Public georeferenced searches found no king cobra points within 150 km of EBTL.

Proxies:
- EBTL does have many snakes.
- EBTL also has an older spectacled cobra record.

Estimate:
- The main uncertainty is whether king cobras were truly absent, or just missed because survey effort was short and public records are sparse.

What the field team should check at EBTL:
- Search wetter, more wooded parts of the property and edges linked to larger habitat.
- Check streams, ponds, drainages, and dense cover.
- Look for prey signs, especially other snakes.
- Ask staff and neighbours for dated photos or exact sighting details.
- Record all large snake sightings with GPS, date, habitat, and photos.
- If possible, run repeat surveys in different seasons, not only one short visit.

What is still missing:
- A targeted king cobra survey at EBTL.
- Verified nearby records with coordinates.
