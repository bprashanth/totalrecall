Yes, they still could be there, but the current evidence is weak.

Observations:
- EBTL has no king cobra record in the published local snake inventory.
- The only local snake survey I found was a short visual survey on 2024-09-05 to 2024-09-07, so it does not prove absence.

Reports:
- Public georeferenced GBIF+iNaturalist searches found 0 king cobra records within 25 km, 50 km, 100 km, and 150 km of EBTL.

Proxies:
- EBTL does support snakes in general: 14 snake species are listed there.
- One other cobra species, spectacled cobra, has an older property record at EBTL. That shows cobra-friendly habitat is possible, but it is not evidence for king cobra.

Estimate:
- My careful estimate is: king cobra presence at EBTL is possible but currently unconfirmed, and probably low-confidence on present data.
- I would not say “present” or “likely present” from the available evidence.

What is missing:
- Verified king cobra records from nearby landscapes.
- A targeted local survey for large forest snakes, not just the short general survey already done.
