The satellite-based parts are the vegetation signal and the fire signal.

Local proxy evidence from satellite data:
- Vegetation: `greenness trend` uses MODIS annual NDVI trend at the declared EBTL centre point.
- Detail: one 250 m pixel. This is a coarse proxy, not a field measure of plant recovery.
- Fire: `historical fire exposure` uses MODIS active-fire history for the exact site area, plus a separate 5 km exposure view.
- Detail: site-area history plus a broader 5 km buffer. This is also a proxy, not a direct field record of burned area or fire severity.

What is missing:
- The actual satellite results for EBTL
- The restoration start year and the exact years compared
- Matching field checks to confirm what the satellite proxy means on the ground

What the field team can collect:
- Fixed-point vegetation plots or photo points
- Plant cover, survival, canopy, or biomass measures
- Dated local fire incident records and mapped burned patches
