No, we cannot say the restoration work caused the changes from the evidence here.

Local proxy evidence:
- The only named measures in this thread are satellite proxies for greenness and fire.
- Proxy change is not proof of cause.

What is missing for a causal claim:
- The actual EBTL satellite results
- The restoration start year and the exact years compared
- Field measurements from the site to confirm what the satellite proxy means
- A before-and-after design, or a comparison with similar non-restored areas
- Other possible drivers in the same years, such as weather or outside disturbance

What the field team can collect:
- Fixed vegetation plots or photo points before and after restoration
- The same measurements each year: cover, survival, canopy, biomass
- Dated local fire records and mapped burned patches
- A comparison area without restoration
- Records of rainfall, drought, grazing, cutting, or other disturbances

So, at this stage, we can discuss possible change signals, but not restoration causing them.
