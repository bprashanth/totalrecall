Only the **fire proxy** and the **district-level tree-cover-loss-from-fire** part came from satellite-based products.

What came from satellite data:
- **“346 VIIRS fire alerts in Krishnagiri since July 4, 2022”**: this is from **VIIRS active fire alerts** shown by Global Forest Watch, based on NASA satellite detections. These are near-real-time heat/fire detections. Source: [NASA FIRMS active fire data](https://firms.modaps.eosdis.nasa.gov/active_fire/), [GFW fire data explainer](https://www.globalforestwatch.org/blog/data-and-tools/trends-tree-loss-from-fires-unprecedented-detail/).
- **“2023 had the highest tree-cover loss from fire in the district, 64 ha”**: this is from **UMD/GFW tree cover loss from fires**, derived from **Landsat** satellite imagery. Source: [GFW fire-loss explainer](https://www.globalforestwatch.org/blog/data-and-tools/trends-tree-loss-from-fires-unprecedented-detail/), [GFW tree cover loss explainer](https://www.globalforestwatch.org/blog/data-and-tools/2025-tree-cover-loss-data-explained/).

What did **not** come from satellite data:
- The part saying EBTL had restoration activities, native nursery work, saplings, and species counts came from **EBTL’s public pages** and a public blog post, not satellites. Sources: [EBTL About](https://ebtl.earth/about/), [EBTL Restoration Project](https://ebtl.earth/restoration-project/), [Nithin Kamath post](https://nithinkamath.me/blog/elephants-by-the-lake.md/).

How detailed the satellite data is:
- **VIIRS fire alerts**: about **375 m** pixels. Good for seeing that a fire/heat signal was detected nearby, but **not good for drawing exact burned boundaries** or proving what happened on one small site. Source: [NASA FIRMS](https://firms.modaps.eosdis.nasa.gov/active_fire/).
- **Tree cover loss from fires**: about **30 m** pixels from Landsat, updated **yearly**. This is much finer, but it mainly captures **stand-replacing canopy loss**. It can miss **low-intensity understory fires** that do not remove canopy clearly enough at 30 m. Source: [GFW fire-loss explainer](https://www.globalforestwatch.org/blog/data-and-tools/trends-tree-loss-from-fires-unprecedented-detail/).
