We cannot tell yet whether vegetation at EBTL is improving, or whether fire has gone up or down in the same years.

What is missing:
- A defined vegetation measure
- A defined fire measure
- The exact time window after restoration started
- The survey effort or data source used in each year

What the field team can collect:
- Vegetation: the same yearly measure at the same plots or point, such as plant cover, survival, canopy cover, or NDVI-linked field checks
- Fire: yearly fire records for the site, such as burned area, fire counts, or dated fire incidents
- Dates: the restoration start year and the exact years to compare
- Effort: the same method each year so the results are comparable

Right now this is a data gap, not evidence of improvement or decline.
