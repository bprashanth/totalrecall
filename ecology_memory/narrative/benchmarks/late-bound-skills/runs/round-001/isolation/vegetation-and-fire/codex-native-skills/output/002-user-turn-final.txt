The parts from **satellite data** are the two **proxies**:

Vegetation proxy: from **MODIS NDVI**.  
It gave the **2019 to 2024** greenness trend at the **declared EBTL centre**.

Fire proxy: from **MODIS active-fire data**.  
It gave fire exposure for **2020 to 2025** around the site.

The **detail is coarse**:

- Vegetation used **one 250 m MODIS pixel** at the declared site centre.
- Fire used a **5 km buffer** around the site centre, plus a small declared analysis box for fire-location checks.
- So this is **not field data**, **not the whole property**, and **not fine-scale mapping of parts within EBTL**.

What does **not** come from satellite data:

- The report about **eucalyptus removal before pond construction** came from a **local June 2023 site newsletter**, not a satellite.
- The statement that yearly fire went up or down was **not supported** by the available satellite skill.

So, satellite data supported the **greenness trend** and the **fire-exposure proxy**, but only at **coarse resolution**.
