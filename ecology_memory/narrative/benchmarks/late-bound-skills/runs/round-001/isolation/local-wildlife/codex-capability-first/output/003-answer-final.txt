People have recorded **67 bird species** at EBTL, plus other wildlife: **54 butterflies**, **42 odonates** (31 dragonflies and 11 damselflies), and **33 herpetofauna records**.

Local observed evidence:
- Birds: 67 species seen or heard in the **5 to 7 September 2024** survey. Examples: Gray Junglefowl, Short-toed Snake-Eagle, Indian Spotted Eagle, White-cheeked Barbet, Spot-breasted Fantail.
- Other animals:
  - Butterflies: 54 taxa observed in **5 to 7 September 2024**.
  - Odonates: 42 taxa observed in **10 to 13 October 2024**.
  - Herpetofauna: 20 taxa observed during the **5 to 7 September 2024** survey, including **7 frogs, 9 lizards, 3 snakes, and 1 turtle**. Examples: Common Indian Toad, Indian Burrowing Frog, Rock Agama, Common Sand Boa, Star Tortoise.

Local reported earlier records:
- The herpetofauna total of 33 includes **13 earlier property records** that were **not seen in the 2024 survey**. These are older site records, not current survey detections.

Missing data:
- This result is a **group summary**, not a full easy-to-read species list for all animals.
- It does **not** show year-round presence, abundance, or which species local people saw outside the survey dates.
- If the field team wants that, they should collect:
  - full species checklists by group,
  - date and place for each sighting,
  - whether the animal was seen or heard,
  - repeat surveys in different seasons,
  - simple community sighting logs from staff and neighbours.
