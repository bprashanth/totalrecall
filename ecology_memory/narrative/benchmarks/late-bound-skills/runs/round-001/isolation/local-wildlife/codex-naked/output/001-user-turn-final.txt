From public EBTL pages, people have reported or described these at EBTL:

Animals found in public sources:
- Asian elephant. EBTL says a few elephants came onto the land when the project began.
- Langurs. EBTL mentions “a troupe of langurs.”
- Monitor lizard.
- Snakes. EBTL says encounters with snakes are common.
- Scorpions and spiders.
- Sloth bear is mentioned for the area, and EBTL also mentions fresh bear tracks nearby.
- Indian rock python is listed as part of the site’s fauna.

Birds found in public sources:
- Amur Falcon is listed as part of the fauna in the region at EBTL.
- An unspecified bird of prey.
- Other birds are clearly present, but the public pages I found do not give a bird checklist.

What I found vs. what I estimate:
- Found: the animals and birds above are named on EBTL’s public website.
- Estimate: there are probably many more bird species at EBTL, because they run birding walks and biodiversity documentation, but I did not find a public species list or public sighting log.
