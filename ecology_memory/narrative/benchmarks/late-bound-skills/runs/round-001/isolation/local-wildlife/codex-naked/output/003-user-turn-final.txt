From public sources, I cannot give a reliable number.

What I found:
- EBTL says snake encounters are common and that some venomous snakes are present.
- EBTL’s restoration page also names Indian Rock Python in the fauna.

So, from public information, the minimum is:
- At least 1 snake that can be dangerous to people: Indian Rock Python.
- Probably more than 1, because EBTL also says venomous snakes are present, but it does not name them on the public pages I found.

What is estimate:
- “More than 1” is an estimate, not a confirmed count, because the public sources do not list all snake species.

What is missing:
