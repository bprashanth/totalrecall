To know that properly, we need same-time, same-place evidence for both cobras and elephants.

Local observed evidence:
- We do not have direct observations showing both species together at the same place and time.

Local reported evidence:
- We do not have field reports confirming joint presence at the same time.

Local proxy evidence:
- We only have occurrence-record proximity within 10 km in the 75 km area around EBTL.
- That proxy does not establish same-time presence.

Local modelled evidence:
- The 75 km search area was an approximate buffered bbox, not an exact field boundary.

Regional evidence:
- None provided that answers same-time local overlap.

What is missing:
- Cobra and elephant records with exact date, time, and GPS location.
- Records from the same survey period or same day.
- Direct evidence from the same site, not just nearby points in the wider 75 km area.
- Repeat observations to show whether overlap is regular or one-off.

What the field team can collect:
- Camera-trap images or videos with timestamps.
- GPS-tagged sightings of cobras and elephants.
- Fresh tracks, dung, or shed skins logged with time and coordinates.
- Repeated surveys on the same routes and dates.
- If possible, movement data from tagged elephants and verified cobra observations in the same period.
