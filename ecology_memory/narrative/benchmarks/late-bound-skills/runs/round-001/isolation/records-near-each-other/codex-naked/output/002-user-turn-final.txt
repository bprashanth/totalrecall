What I found:
- Public records show `Indian cobra` in the wider EBTL area.
- Public sources also show `elephants` in the wider Hosur-Melagiri landscape.

What I did not find:
- A public pair of records showing `cobra and elephant at the same place and the same time`.
- A public elephant point record close enough to the cobra records to confirm the `within 10 km` part.

So the safe answer is:
- `We cannot say yes from public data.`
- `We also cannot prove no.`

This means:
- `Same general landscape`: yes, likely.
- `Same exact place at the same time`: not confirmed.

- EBTL public location: [EBTL site](https://ebtl.earth/cegs/)
