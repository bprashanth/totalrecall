To know that properly, we need `record-level data` for both species, not just general area information.

What we need:
- `Exact location` for each cobra record and each elephant record, preferably GPS coordinates
- `Exact date and time` for each record
- `A clear distance rule`, such as “within 10 km”
- `A clear time rule`, such as “same day”, “within 24 hours”, or “same week”
- `Reliable species ID` for both records
- `Source details` for each record, such as camera trap, field sighting, rescue log, or public biodiversity record

Then we can test:
- `Place`: are any cobra and elephant records within 10 km?
- `Time`: do those nearby records happen in the same time window?

What I found:
- Public sources show cobras in the wider EBTL area.
- Public sources show elephants in the wider Hosur-Melagiri landscape.

What is missing:
