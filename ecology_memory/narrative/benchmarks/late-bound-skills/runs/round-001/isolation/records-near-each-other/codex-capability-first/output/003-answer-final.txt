Yes. In the 75 km search area around EBTL, there are Indian cobra occurrence records within 10 km of elephant occurrence records.

Local proxy evidence:
- 176 of 218 Indian cobra records matched at least one elephant record within 10 km.
- 83 of 110 elephant records matched at least one Indian cobra record within 10 km.
- The closest matched cobra records in the returned sample were about 0.3 km, 1.25 km, and 1.36 km from an elephant record.
- This is spatial record proximity only. It does not show same-time presence, interaction, abundance, or confirmed presence at EBTL itself.

Local observed evidence:
- None provided in the audited result.

Local reported evidence:
- None provided in the audited result.

Local modelled evidence:
- The 75 km search area is an approximate buffered bbox, not an exact surveyed boundary.

Regional evidence:
- None provided separately from the 75 km search result.

What is missing:
- Same-date or same-time cobra and elephant observations.
- Field-verified records from EBTL itself.
- Exact movement or interaction data.

What the field team can collect:
- GPS-tagged cobra and elephant sightings with date and time.
- Track, dung, shed skin, or camera-trap records with coordinates.
- Repeated surveys inside EBTL and along the 75 km area to confirm whether both species use the same places at the same time.
