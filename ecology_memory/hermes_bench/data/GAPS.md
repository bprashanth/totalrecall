# Known evidence gaps for the EBTL conversation benchmark

- Exact 70-acre property polygon and within-property strata; the declared analysis bbox is larger.
- Repeat mapped invasive-plant extent and removal history sufficient to estimate rebound.
- Property-scale fuel load, fuel moisture, wind/weather and ignition exposure for a fire-risk model.
- Direct soil moisture/water-potential measurements by depth, habitat and season.
- Nursery's complete species roster, provenance, survival, growth and outplanting outcomes.
- Phylogenetic tree/trait coverage and local interaction data for nursery or restoration taxa.
- Systematic mammal, nocturnal herpetofauna, arachnid and seasonal survey effort with repeat non-detections.
- Snake/arachnid use of vegetation, refuge, prey and microclimate features; no local tree dependency study.
- Treatment/control or before/after plot evidence separating restoration effects from rainfall and surrounding land use.
- Community use, grazing, fuelwood, crop-loss, coexistence, restoration-employment and distributional-impact data.

On these topics the correct behavior is a bounded qualitative inference or explicit unknown plus a
specific DATA REQUEST. No number may be supplied merely from model memory.
